================================================================================
THE GATE - pass/fail stated in advance
================================================================================
G0  KILL SWITCH. sfOFF value-identical to sfREF on all 23 tuples.
    "value-identical", not byte-identical: one dict's key order varies between
    processes. A 0/N identity with IDENTICAL differing fields across every
    tuple and no eval field is a harness schema change, not a regression.
    PASS = 23/23.

G1  PRIMARY, STRUCTURAL. sfON searcher off-grid refused steps == 0 in EVERY
    run, all 23 tuples. Immune to world drift. PASS = exactly 0.
    Pre-registered sfOFF value: to be measured (the re-baselined stock).

G2  RE-BASELINED PIN SET. Every pin present in sfOFF clears in sfON, with
    per-pin unit / step it pinned / step it moved.
    THE DIAGNOSIS'S 10 ARE A CROSS-REFERENCE ONLY. Of those 10, 4 reproduce
    identically post-fireproof, 3 ARE ALREADY ABSENT with no fix applied, and
    3 have no post-fireproof record. Crediting the fix for the 3 already
    removed by the fireproof round would be the error D1 corrected.
    PASS = every sfOFF pin cleared AND zero new pins.

G3  NO NEW DWELL RUN. Same-cell occupancy runs >= 2, by cause. The specific
    risk is converting an OFF-GRID pin into a UAV-BLOCKED one, because the
    guard makes the DIRECTION legal, not the MOVE successful (agents.py:852
    also requires not_UAV_adjacent). PASS = searcher UAV-BLOCKED runs and
    burning-steps do not rise.

G4  OSCILLATION, MEASURED NOT ARGUED. Reversals, tight reversals, longest
    two-cell alternation, on the changed steps, per arm. ATTRIBUTION IS
    MANDATORY: a reversal counts against the fix only if at least one leg is a
    guard-substituted step - the last_cell round's entire +29 turned out to be
    one unit on an untouched path. PASS = no unattributed rise.

G5  DRONE STEPS IN FIRE, E1 searcher-only and E2 all-UAV.
    EXPECTATION STATED IN ADVANCE: the diagnosis measured only 0.14 pp of
    genuine search-component improvement available under CRN. Expect the pins
    to clear and exposure to move LITTLE. A null here is NOT a failure; a RISE
    is. PASS = E1 and E2 do not rise.

G6  DETECTION TIMING - THE COST THE FIX EXISTS TO RECOVER. det_max_first_seen,
    before and after, seed-matched. Reported on both the STRICT and CENSORED
    estimators, with which one is quoted stated explicitly.
    PASS = improves, or is explained.

G7  OUTCOMES. rescued must NOT DECREASE on either sample. dead, ff_deaths,
    never_detected, terminal_step reported. Plus the battery cost this fix
    introduces: a refused step drains 0.1, a successful one 0.3, so the bound
    is 0.2 per converted refusal - up to 22.6 points across a dcD-sized arm.

G8  ROUTE_BLOCKED. _ffr_rbcompare.py --new <tag> --old ihrest. Four seeds
    regress at the shipped default: east/707, south/101, south/202, south/404.
    PASS = that same set and nothing new.

G9  TESTS. No NEW failures beyond the 737b6c7 set, BY NAME.
    TWO DECLARED, INTENTIONAL test changes, listed here so they cannot later be

   zero pins bought with ping-ponging is not a fix.
