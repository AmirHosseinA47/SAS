0. ANSWER FIRST
================================================================================================
Q: does a SINGLE extinguish already produce an intact change of whole-arm magnitude and random
   sign (re-roll), while a single clear produces a small local change?

EXTINGUISH - YES (20 fmES tuples, canonical 13 + fresh 7).
  - |d_only| median 125 cells vs |d_all| (the whole arm) median 195; canonical alone 137 vs 133
    (median ratio |d_only|/|d_all| 1.02 canonical, 0.84 both). Range -205..+494; sign +11/-7/0 2.
  - Mechanism measured, not inferred: the first digest divergence is the write step 20/20; the
    extinguished cell turns burnt at the next fire tick (first differing burnt set 1-3 steps
    after the write in 18 runs), so from the tick after that it stops drawing and every
    later-uid cell's draw shifts: stream shift 19/20, lag 4-6 steps in 18 of them (15 in one).
    Burning differences leave the light cone in 18/20, AT the shift tick in 17 (one 3 steps
    later), never before it (18/18). At that step most differing cells are already outside a
    radius-6 cone (e.g. 75 of 89, 179 of 206). 50% of the final has_burned differences (2,586 of
    5,144 cells) lie > 20 cells from the written cell; 74 lie within 3.
  - Physical part alone (same replay with every draw pinned to the no-write run's value, so no
    re-roll): d_pin = 0 in 16/20; nonzero -88, +307, +591, -2 (the two large ones are early
    writes, steps 15 and 54); the pinned difference never leaves the cone (0/20, farthest
    differing cell 24). Re-roll part |d_only - d_pin|: median 140.5, mean 181.3 (mean |d_pin| 49.4).
  - "Random sign" needs one qualification: d_only is NOT independent of the arm. Pearson(d_only,
    d_all) = 0.65 (Spearman 0.54), same sign 12 / opposite 6. The arm carries the SAME first
    extinguish, hence the same shift at the same tick, so it inherits that re-roll; dropping only
    the first extinguish gives an uncorrelated result (Pearson(d_drop, d_all) = -0.08, |d_drop -
    d_all| median 195, max 665), and in that open-loop fire 345 of the 470 later recorded
    extinguishes (73.4%) find no burning cell. The fmES arm's per-run intact change is largely the
    re-roll its first extinguish draws.

CLEAR - NO in the stock model; YES only for its physical effect (20 fmF tuples).
  - Not small: |d_only| median 46.5, mean 68.2 (arm: median 128, mean 135.5), range -189..+213,
    sign +9/-7/0 4, Pearson with the arm -0.42 (Spearman -0.14).
  - Not local: 58% of the final has_burned differences (1,457 of 2,506) lie > 20 cells away.
  - But its PHYSICAL effect is exactly the cleared cell: pinned d_pin is 0 in 18/20 and -1 in 2
    (the cleared cell counts as cleared instead of burned, or as cleared where fire never came);
    at most 1 final has_burned cell differs per run, at distance 0 (15 cells over 20 runs); never
    leaves the cone. Everything else in d_only is re-roll (|d_only - d_pin| median 46.5).
  - The re-roll is DELAYED, not absent: a firebreak cell keeps drawing, so the stream shifts only
    when some burn-out timing differs - the cleared cell itself (5 runs: the 3 scorched clears at
    lag 1-2, and 2 where the fire would have burned it out, lags 61 and 126) or another cell whose
    burning history the clear changed physically (11 runs). Shift in 16/20 at lags 4,5,5
    (scorched) and 36-140 (median of all 16: 106.5 steps); no shift by 240 in 4 (2 clears the
    fire never reached, 2 with only the cell itself differing).
  - That is why the light-cone test flags only 2/20 clears (both scorched): the cone covers the
    whole 50x50 grid 34-51 steps after a write, and 12 of the 13 non-scorched shifts come later.
    The shift detector (first differing burnt set) is what sees them.
  - Inside the full firebreak schedule the first clear barely matters: |d_drop - d_all| median 1
    (values 0 x5, 1 x9, 2, 7, 13, 19, 31, 126), no burning difference at all in 9/20, Pearson 0.98;
    only 4 of 1,695 later clears fail to land (all in south/half/505, whose first clear was scorched).

So the asymmetry round 1 saw per run (fmES coin-flip sign, fmF consistent) is not "extinguish
re-rolls, firebreak is local". Both single writes re-roll the rest of the fire through the shared
draw stream; an extinguish does it at the second tick after the write (18/20; the 2 exceptions had
the cell on its last burning tick: one never shifted, one shifted 15 steps later via another
cell), a clear only once some burn-out timing differs (median 106.5 steps later, 4/20 never).
The physical effect of one write on the recorded fire is 0 for 16/20 extinguishes and <= 1 cell
for 20/20 clears. Whether the fmF arm's consistent per-run gain is physical was NOT measured here
(see 7, open questions).

2. WHY EXACT REPLAY IS POSSIBLE (from the source)
================================================================================================
Construction: _ffr_harness.py main() reproduced - import order agents, common_fixed_variables,
wildfire_model, apply_scenario_config, WildFireModel, serve_dashboard; params dict (scenario
preset, _resolve_role_count_params(num_agents, 2, 2) for half / (None, None) for default,
BATCH_SIZE 300, FIRE_SPREAD_MULTIPLIER 0.75, PROBABILITY_MAP False) + extra_params; the rebuilt
dict is asserted equal to the JSON's recorded "params" (it was: 158 replay processes, all exit 0).
rng = Random(seed) bound
to cfv.SYSTEM_RANDOM, wf.SYSTEM_RANDOM, agents.random; apply_scenario_config; WildFireModel()
under redirect_stdout; debug_log = False. The harness's observers are not installed: each calls
the original and returns its result without drawing from any RNG, so leaving them out cannot move
a draw (the digest matches below confirm it).

What can touch Fire agents or the shared rng after construction (WildFireModel.step, 5738-5786):
  datacollector.collect, BATCH_SIZE guard, _update_fire_tracker_sector_assignments, monitoring,
  _run_pre_move_decision_cycle, _prepare_uav_directions_for_step, MR1/MR2, schedule.step(),
  _run_post_move_decision_cycle, _update_unreachable_victims, _log_step_summary,
  get_dashboard_state.
  - RNG: in agents.py only Fire.__init__ (randint, construction), Fire.step (random.random()) and
    Wind.change_direction (only when FIXED_WIND is False; it is True). In wildfire_model.py only
    set_fire_agents (construction), _prepare_uav_directions_for_step (returns before its draw
    whenever decision_dispatcher is set; __init__ always sets it, line 338) and
    _make_ff_absence_rng (getstate(), consumes nothing). src_extension contains no "random" at all.
  - Fire state writers: Fire.step, Fire.advance, and Firefighter._firefight_execute
    (firefighter_extinguish / firefighter_remove_fuel), called from Firefighter.advance. Fire
    agents are added to the schedule first (set_fire_agents before any UAV/unit), so every
    Fire.advance runs before any write. No other writer of burning/burnt/fuel/has_burned/
    steps_counter exists; the Wind object is never modified.
  - probability_of_fire reads only Fire agents in the Moore-3 neighbourhood; other agents on the
    grid are skipped, so their absence in the replay is irrelevant.
  - For extinguish/clear plans the plan tuple is (kind, cell, cell), so the logged "target" is the
    written cell. firefight_log "step" = evaluation_timesteps_counter, incremented at the start of
    model.step(), i.e. the step whose post-step digest is fire_digests[step-1].
Tick phase: Fire.step increments its own steps_counter before anything else, and ticks when
steps_counter % FIRE_SPREAD_SPEED(3) == 0. Every Fire cell's steps_counter equalled the model
step after every step in all 158 replay processes (0 mismatches), so ticks are steps 3, 6, ..., 240. A write
at step t lands after that step's advance; the light-cone radius at step s is
3 * (floor(s/3) - floor(t/3)) (Moore radius 3 per tick). Note: burn-out (burnt=True,
burning=False) is set inside step(), so later-uid neighbours see it in the same tick; that still
propagates at most 3 cells per tick, and the pinned replays confirm it (0/40 leave the cone).
CAVEAT of the cone test: on 50x50 the cone covers the grid 32-51 steps after a write (study
range), after which a departure cannot be seen; the burnt-set shift detector covers that.

kind (east/half/707, south/half/808, south/half/909), and their fire equals fmOFF 240/240.

Definitions (the analyzer's): ever = cells with has_burned; cleared = fuel <= 0 and not has_burned;
intact = 2500 - ever - cleared; d_X = intact(X) - intact(fmOFF from _firemech_analyze).
d_all uses the arm JSON through _firemech_analyze.run_summary (the arm IS writes=all; exact
replay validated above). Comparisons of only-first use the no-write replay as the reference (it
alone has burnt timing). "shift" = first tick whose draw set differs = (first step with a differing
burnt set) + 3. "de-duplicated" excludes east/def (its OFF fire equals east/half).

5. INSTRUMENT CHECKS AND LIMITS
================================================================================================
- --crn check (appendix E): fmF east/half/404 CRN only-first vs CRN no-write: stays inside the
  cone (first_out None), d +6, 7 differing cells, farthest 6; the stock pair: d +60, 175 differing,
  farthest 42. fmES east/half/101: the recorded first extinguish did NOT land on the CRN fire (cell
  not burning there) - replaying a STOCK arm's writes on a CRN fire is not meaningful for
  extinguishes. --crn is for writes recorded by a CRN arm (the f2 probe outputs, which this task
  did not read), and its match against a closed-loop CRN harness run is NOT validated here.
- --pin-draws is the stock fire with the re-roll switched off. Cells that draw in the ablation but
  not in the recorded run (burnt there, not here) get crn_uniform values: 15,718 such draws over
  the 40 pinned study replays (0 in the identity check). It is exact as an identity (240/240) and
  respects causality (0/40 departures from the cone), but its nonzero d_pin values are one
  realisation of the physical effect, not an expectation.
- Open loop: the write schedule is fixed; units do not react to the changed fire. drop-first of
  fmES is therefore mostly a schedule of misses (73.4% of its extinguishes do not land).
- intact counts a cleared cell as not intact, so a clear the fire never reaches gives d = -1.
- The light-cone test cannot see a departure after the cone covers the grid (32-51 steps after
  the write in this study); read "leaves cone" together with "shift".

6. RE-VERIFIED CONTEXT STATEMENTS
================================================================================================
- "EXTINGUISH makes a cell burnt one tick early so it stops drawing": partly. It turns burnt at the
  NEXT tick after the write, which is earlier than its own burn-out by however many burning ticks
  it had left (not necessarily one); in 1/20 (east/def/303, step 175) the cell was on its last
  burning tick, burnt timing did not change, no shift occurred and d_only = 0; in 1/20
  (south/half/101) the first burnt difference came 12 steps later at another cell. The first shifted draw belongs to the written cell's uid (e.g.
  south/half/404: cell (10,38) = uid 538, shift at tick 18).
- "A FIREBREAK cell keeps drawing; the stream shifts only when some burn-out timing differs":
  confirmed (16 of 20 clears shift; 11 start at another cell's burnt timing, 5 at the cell's own).
- "DRY == OFF 240/240 on 23/23": not re-checked here (not needed for this task).
- Report C-1 "the first extinguish re-rolls the rest of the fire": confirmed and located (2 ticks
  after the write). Round-1's per-run framing of the asymmetry needs the qualification in 0.
