  1. The depot pockets were a CONTROLLER defect, not a depot effect: a safety veto layered on a route
     planner that could not see it produced a livelock; burnable depots still showed it.   CONFIRMED -
     0 depot-pocket oscillations in every A1 arm (both seed sets, B's four arms); searcher pocket steps PRE
     280 -> 0.
  2. Every result at the session-2 defaults (a5a496db) includes 3 runs that never finish and must not be
     used.   RE-CONFIRMED - fx3Z reproduces a5a496db exactly (16/16) with the same 3 (A/N, B/E, C/S).
  3. B2 is the first launch stagger in this project that closes the no-searcher gap without extra returns.
     CONFIRMED on 16 runs - 517 -> 0 PRE; returns: trackers 2.00 -> 2.00, searchers 2.00 -> 1.62.
  4. B3 is a safety net: it did not fire on top of B2.   CONFIRMED with a number - on top of B2 it fired
     2 UAV-steps in 2 of 16 runs; alone, 450 UAV-steps in 16 of 16, never below LOW.
  NEW (for the maintainer to rule on):
  5. The near-field veto-vs-planner livelock is not confined to the escape route: the searcher hazard gate
     produces 10-33-step two-cell alternations on ordinary search and sweep steps, at a5a496db and after
     fix3a. A "livelock-free searcher" claim must count them.
  6. Waiting instead of oscillating trades a livelock for search time: the latched route's fire-waits
     doubled against its prototype and, in one run, pushed a detection past the horizon.
  7. With free-cell docking, docking and charging are positional (inside a depot footprint), which the
     berth code never enforced; the doorstep standoffs of earlier rounds do not occur (0 in the 710 return
     legs of the seven A2 arms).
