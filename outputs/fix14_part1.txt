1. THE CHURN BASELINE - MEASURED, ALL FOUR RUNS, BOTH SIDES
==============================================================================

A "segment" is a maximal run of consecutive steps holding the same finalized
target. Steps with no target are dropped before grouping so an instrumentation
gap cannot manufacture a segment boundary. A "re-target" is a segment boundary:
every segment but the last is abandoned.

Distances are Manhattan from the searcher's pre-move position to the target.
"meanRem" is the distance still outstanding at the moment the target is
abandoned - the brief's "mean remaining Manhattan distance at re-target".

THE TWO RUNS THE COMMIT MESSAGE NEVER REPORTED:

  D_south_101 DOES NOT CHURN. Segments went 18 -> 16 and mean hold 13.3 -> 14.9
  - the opposite direction from the other two treatment runs. One POST segment
  runs 127 steps, over half the run. Section 3 explains why, and it is the same
  reason gate condition 1 failed on this run (distinct cells flat at 14 -> 14).
  D/south is not a weak version of the churn signature; it is a different
  regime, and any hold mechanism must not disturb it.

  A_west_505 (control) is identical on every column, as expected: west wind
  takes the west branch, which the x-clamp fix does not touch. Note the control
  already sits at mean hold 4.3 with 41 of 56 segments lasting <= 3 steps. The
  west branch churns natively and always has. This matters for the gate: a
  "healthy" mean hold is not a property the west branch demonstrates.

WHAT THE NUMBERS SAY THE SEARCHER IS ACTUALLY DOING (post-fix, churning runs):

  - Targets are picked ~30 cells away (meanStart 32.4 / 28.9).
  - They are abandoned ~28-30 cells away (meanRem 30.1 / 27.2).
  - Net distance closed per segment: 2.2 and 1.6 cells. Against a ~30-cell
    gap, the searcher gives up having covered ~6% of the journey.
  - 48 of 70 and 55 of 73 segments last <= 3 steps.
  - No segment on any POST run ends within COMMIT_ARRIVAL_DIST of its target.
    The closest any abandoned target ever got was 11 and 10 cells (minRem).

  Crucially, meanRem barely moved BASE -> POST (27.8 -> 30.1, 28.6 -> 27.2).
  The searcher was always abandoning targets ~30 cells out. What the x-clamp
  fix changed is not the distance at abandonment but the FREQUENCY of it: 15
  and 20 re-targets became 69 and 72. Under the clamp, consecutive argmaxes
  were relocated onto the same handful of lattice cells (x in {8,41}), so
  successive re-targets were invisible - they collapsed into one segment. The
  clamp was not preventing re-selection; it was disguising it by mapping many
  different choices onto the same output. Removing it revealed churn that was
  already there, and freed the argmax to wander further.

  This is a sharper statement of the root cause than "the clamp provided
  stability as a side effect of being wrong", and it changes the design: a
  hold mechanism is not restoring something the clamp used to do correctly.
  It is adding stability that has never existed on this path.

  Corollary, and the reason ARRIVAL alone cannot carry the design: at a mean
  closure of 0.65 cells/step (2.2 cells over a 3.4-step hold, near the movement
  cap), a 30-cell traverse needs ~45 steps of uninterrupted commitment. Nothing
  in the current code grants it.

WHAT THE CONSTANTS IMPLY, GIVEN SECTION 1's MEASUREMENTS - stated now so the
outcome can be read against a prior expectation rather than rationalised after:

  TIMEOUT budget = 2.0 x meanStart = 64.8 (D/north), 69.2 (D/south),
  57.8 (A/north), 57.2 (A/west) steps. Over a 240-step run that permits at
  most ~4 timeouts even if every hold ran to its limit. TIMEOUT is a backstop,
  not a working release condition.

  ARRIVAL requires closing ~30 cells. At the observed ~0.65 cells/step ceiling
  that is ~45 steps - inside the timeout budget, so arrival is reachable, but
  only if STALLED does not fire first.

  STALLED (12 consecutive non-closing steps) is therefore the binding
  condition, and the mechanism's behaviour will be dominated by it. Expected
  mean hold lands in the low-to-mid tens of steps: comfortably above the
  3.3-3.4 churn baseline, and in the same band as the 11-15 pre-fix level
  without reproducing the 12-15-distinct-targets degenerate regime, since the
  targets being held are now the scorer's own argmax rather than two lattice
  columns.

  Gate condition 1 should therefore pass. It is not guaranteed: if the
  deflection geometry produces stalls faster than 12 steps apart, mean hold
  will sit nearer 6-8 and condition 1 becomes a judgement call on "materially".

3. INTERCEPTION POINT - WHICH CALL SITE PRODUCES THE CHURN
==============================================================================

_finalize_coverage_target has four call sites, all inside
_compute_wind_aware_search_target or its callee:

  L3336  inside _pick_global_coverage_escape_target - finalizes the coverage
         argmax and returns it
  L3600  the escape_target commit path - re-finalizes an ALREADY-HELD target
         while it is still >3 cells away
  L3638  the _gradual_escape_target path - guarded by
         not _coverage_mode_active(wind_state)
  L3822  the corridor-waypoint path - the tail return

EVIDENCE (per-step "branch" field, all four runs, both sides):

run           side   pick_global_coverage_escape   finalize_only_escape   other
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
D_north_101   BASE                           209                     30       1
D_north_101   POST                           239                      0       1
D_south_101   BASE                           209                     30       1
D_south_101   POST                           112                    127       1
A_north_101   BASE                           209                     30       1
A_north_101   POST                           209                     30       1
A_west_505    BASE                           185                     54       1
A_west_505    POST                           185                     54       1

The churn is produced by pick_global_coverage_escape. It carries 239/240 and
209/240 of the steps on exactly the two runs whose mean hold collapsed to ~3.3,
and its target goes straight out of _compute_wind_aware_search_target at L3677
with no commitment of any kind. Every step, _pick_global_coverage_escape_target
re-scores the whole candidate grid and returns a fresh argmax.

THIS ALSO EXPLAINS D/south, THE RUN THAT DID NOT CHURN. Its POST run spent 127
of 240 steps in finalize_only_escape - the L3600 path, reachable only while
coverage mode is INACTIVE (coverage_active is False on 128 of its 240 steps,
against 1 and 31 on the other two treatment runs). That path already holds a
target: it re-finalizes wind_state["escape_target"] and returns it until the
searcher is within 3 cells. D/south's 127-step segment is that existing hold,
not a coincidence. So a hold mechanism is not novel behaviour for this
codebase - it is behaviour the escape_target path already has, that the
coverage path lacks. D/south is the working case; the design generalises it.

Note also that L3638's gradual-escape path is unreachable under coverage mode
(its not-_coverage_mode_active guard), which is why it appears in no run's
branch counts.

DECISION - INTERCEPT INSIDE _finalize_coverage_target:

  1. It is the single common exit. gen_inner records a "finalize" entry on
     every step that produces a target, on all eight run-sides. Nothing reaches
     the executor without passing through it. Intercepting at L3336 alone would
     leave the corridor path (L3822) free to interleave a different target on
     the steps where it fires.
  2. It already receives everything the four release conditions need -
     wind_state, ax, ay, fire_cells, smoke_cells - with no signature change for
     the hazard sets. Part 3 requires reusing the fire/smoke sets the hazard
     re-validation fix threads through the finalizer rather than adding a
     second hazard check; those sets exist here and nowhere else on the path.
  3. Because the finalized and proposed series are identical (Section 1),
     holding at the finalizer is equivalent to holding at the generator return
     for every metric in the gate, with strictly wider coverage.
  4. It is strictly narrower than cfaeb21's site. That round wrapped
     _compute_wind_aware_search_target, which holds the target for ALL of that
     function's exits - including the corridor path, the escape_target commit
     path at L3600, and the early sweep returns. The finalizer sits one level
     down and inside the coverage branch, so D/south's existing 127-step
     escape_target hold keeps its own release rule instead of being overridden
     by a second one. Given that D/south is the run that already works
     (Section 1), not disturbing it is worth the narrower scope.
  5. Reaching it requires current_role in ("victim_searcher", "victim_search")
     - the gate at L1766 in _generate_path_options is the only route to
     _try_generate_wind_aware_victim_search_option. So "per victim_searcher
     UAV" is satisfied structurally, with no role check to add. It also means
     the hold governs SEARCH only: when live victim targets exist, L1767 takes
     the victim path and the wind-aware generator is never called, so a held
     search target cannot delay a rescue.

ONE SIGNATURE CHANGE IS REQUIRED: step_index. _finalize_coverage_target does not
currently receive it, and hold duration, the stall window and the timeout are
all measured in steps. It cannot be counted internally: gen_inner shows the
finalizer running 2-3 times per step on many steps (finalize / pick_global_escape
appearing twice on 20-31 steps per run), so a per-call counter would over-count
by 2-3x. step_index is already in scope at all four call sites -
_compute_wind_aware_search_target computes it at L3512 and passes it to
_pick_global_coverage_escape_target - so it is threaded, not derived. Progress
bookkeeping is guarded on step_index changing, so repeat calls within one step
read the cache without advancing any counter.

CACHE LOCATION - wind_state, as the brief anticipated:

  wind_state is per-UAV by construction: _wind_search_state(simulation, uav_id)
  returns simulation._wind_search_target_state[uav_id], and
  _default_wind_search_state() backfills any missing key on every read. That is
  exactly how recent_x_positions, coverage_y_commit and the x-clamp fix's own
  west_strip_done / east_strip_done are already stored. New keys added to
  _default_wind_search_state() inherit the same per-UAV lifetime and the same
  backfill safety for states created before this change.

  The alternative - a cache on the generator instance - is not viable:
  _diag_target_following.py's header records that the executor builds a fresh
  LocalAdaptationSpaceGenerator() on every call (uav_executor.py:3737), so
  instance state does not survive between steps. wind_state is the only
  per-UAV store with the right lifetime.

4. PREDICTION FOR THE "UNSAFE" RELEASE, STATED BEFORE RUNNING
==============================================================================

The brief asks whether UNSAFE should be rare, given the hazard re-validation
fix cut the hazard-target share to 1.3% at proposal time.

PREDICTION: NO - UNSAFE will not be as rare as 1.3%, and the gap between the
two figures is the point. The hazard fix guarantees a target is safe at the
INSTANT IT IS PROPOSED. A hold keeps a fixed cell for a mean of 12+ steps
while the hazard field moves under it. These are different exposures, and the
hold creates the second one for the first time.

Measured hazard field over the four POST runs (fire_n / smoke_n per step):

  run           fire peak   smoke peak   peak hazard share of the 2500-cell grid
  D_north_101         132          250                                    ~15%
  D_south_101         502          746                                    ~50%
  A_north_101         132          240                                    ~15%
  A_west_505          126          310                                    ~17%

Smoke is the larger and more mobile component. On D/south it covers up to half
the grid at peak. A cell held for 12-45 steps in that field has a materially
non-zero chance of being overrun, and the hazard re-validation fix cannot
prevent it - by construction it only ever runs at proposal time.

SHARPER, FALSIFIABLE FORM: UNSAFE should be rare on D/north and A/north
(~15% peak hazard share) and NOT rare on D/south (~50%). If UNSAFE instead
comes back uniformly near zero on all three, the hazard fix generalises to
mid-hold hazard change and the prediction is wrong. If it comes back
concentrated on D/south, the mechanism is proposal-time-only as argued and the
hold is doing work the hazard fix cannot. Either outcome is reported.

OUTCOME (added after measurement; this section is left as written, since its
value is that it was written first): BOTH PARTS OF THIS PREDICTION ARE WRONG.
The held target sits in hazard on 0.2% of held steps, below the 1.3%
proposal-time figure rather than above it, and D/south - predicted worst - had
zero. See outputs/fix14_report.txt, section "THE 'UNSAFE' PREDICTION, CHECKED
AGAINST THE DATA", and outputs/fix14_hazard_check.txt.

5. WHAT PART 3 WILL DO, GIVEN THE ABOVE
==============================================================================

Cache in wind_state, checked at the top of _finalize_coverage_target's coverage
branch, per victim_searcher UAV:

  commit_target        the finalized (tx, ty) being held
  commit_set_step      step_index at which it was set
  commit_initial_dist  Manhattan distance from the searcher when it was set
  commit_best_dist     best (smallest) distance achieved during the hold
  commit_no_progress   consecutive steps without improving commit_best_dist
  commit_last_step     last step_index at which bookkeeping advanced
  commit_held_steps    steps held so far
  commit_issued        holds opened, for the probe
  commit_breaks        per-reason release counters, for the probe

These key names are taken from cfaeb21's probe variant
(_diag_target_following_COMMITLOG.py), which already snapshots commit_target,
commit_held_steps, commit_initial_dist, commit_issued and commit_breaks out of
wind_state into ws_entry. Reusing the names keeps the fix14 probe a minimal
delta on that file rather than a new instrumentation scheme, which is the
"same instrumentation discipline as cfaeb21's round" the brief asks for.

Release when any of ARRIVAL (euclid <= 2.0), UNSAFE (cell in fire_cells or
smoke_cells now), STALLED (hold_no_progress >= 12), TIMEOUT (steps held >
2.0 x hold_set_man). On release, clear the cache and let the caller's freshly
computed target through unchanged for this step; the next call re-arms the hold
on whatever the normal path chooses. No forced re-finalize in the same step.

Expected effect on the gate, from Section 2: condition 1 satisfied in the
low-to-mid tens; conditions 2-3 inherited from Part 2 since the held target is
the scorer's own argmax rather than a clamp product; condition 4 preserved
because west wind never enters this branch and D/south's existing 127-step
escape_target hold is on a different call site that the change leaves alone.
