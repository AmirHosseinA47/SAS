  o_j = (P/2) j / (n-1) steps, L = 100 - 0.3 o_j. The s searchers take the most widely separated
  positions - j = floor(k (n-1) / (s-1)), k = 0..s-1 (both ends, exactly half a cycle apart, for s = 2;
  j = 0, full charge, for s = 1); the trackers take the remaining positions in unique-id order. B keeps
  its multiplier (0.5 x L).

          C (3T+2S)  T 91.75 | T 83.5 | T 75.25 | S 100 | S 67.0
