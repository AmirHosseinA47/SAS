AMENDMENT 4 - THE STATIONARY-YIELD FIXES (maintainer ruling after FOLLOW-UP 2; written BEFORE any
code or run of this follow-up)
================================================================================
RULING: item 4 OFF accepted. Fix item 2 before merging, one switch; validate on the all-together arm
(item 4 off) with the pre-terminal window throughout; if the fixes do not remove the long depot
clusters, SHIP ITEM 2 OFF ('hold' reverts to the old moving hold) and record both the moving hold and
the clusters it avoided. Record as a paper finding: making the fail-safe hold genuinely stationary
exposed a depot coordination deadlock that the old moving hold had masked.
Review agents: 3 of 3 used - this follow-up has NO independent review; it relies on revert-checked
tests, identity arms and this pre-registration.

THE RULES (switch STATIONARY_YIELD_FIX; ships 1, off only on an exact integral 0; read only while
UAV_HOLD_STATIONARY is on; 0 = the item-2 behaviour of 611c2a4 exactly)
  1. NO EDGE ESCAPE FOR A STATIONARY YIELD. A yield (the fail-safe hold the dispatcher gives the UAV
     that must yield) no longer escapes on the grid's outer ring or on a committed direction that
     leaves the grid: a stay cannot leave the grid and move() already refuses off-grid targets. The
     fire-tracker hazard escape stays (safety overrides a yield), and so does the stuck-count escape
     (the ruled backstop). A YIELD STEP RECORDS NO DRIFT: whatever the yield commits (stay, step
     aside, backstop escape), a refused move on a yield step is not a deviation from the UAV's own
     intent - it is right-of-way coordination, which COLLISION_RISK already reports - so the step
     intends its own cell (drift 0).
  2. STEP ASIDE. A yielder whose cell is the cell a partner needs moves off it instead of staying.
     The partner's needed cell: the cell its committed move of THIS step targets (partners have lower
     ids and are dispatched first, so their commit is known), or, without a commit this step, the
     target of its last move if that move was refused. The step-aside cell: an in-bounds 4-neighbour
     with no UAV on it, not a partner's cell or needed cell, not a depot cell, strict hazard level 0
     (not burning, not smoke); the one farthest from the partners' needed cells, then from the
     partners, then from the grid edge, then the lowest direction index. None -> stay (the backstop
     remains). The step aside is exempt from the searcher gate and the final direction safety pass,
     both of which could turn it back into the partner's cell; its own filter excludes the hazards
     they guard against.
     Order of a yield step: tracker hazard escape > step aside > stuck-count escape > stay.
  3. NO YIELD AT A DEPOT. A pair produces no yield when EITHER UAV is on a return leg or docked, or
     stands in the DEPOT APPROACH AREA: every cell within Manhattan DEPOT_APPROACH_RADIUS = 3 of a
     depot cell (the depot's doorstep ring, 1, plus the collision radius, 2: every UAV that can be in
     a collision pair with a UAV on the doorstep). A UAV's launch leg - from its berth until it first
     stands outside the area - lies inside the area, so the rule covers it. WHY: returns and launches
     converge on a few fixed cells (the berths sit at each depot's inner corner); a stationary UAV
     there is an obstacle on the only lanes in and out, and mutual right-of-way among UAVs that all
     launched together is exactly the ~55-step freeze of C/south. Near a depot the move rule (no two
     UAVs on a cell) and the depot's own berth / dock-fix logic sequence the traffic; COLLISION_RISK
     still fires there (a real condition), but it no longer holds anyone in place. Symmetric on
     purpose: the literal "does not hold another UAV" (the partner side) would still let a UAV inside
     the area stop for one outside, and a stop inside the area is the obstacle.
  (4) OFFERED, NOT SHIPPED - YIELD_ONLY_WHEN_CONTENDING (ships 0; on only on an exact integral 1; read
     only while STATIONARY_YIELD_FIX is on). A UAV yields only if some eligible partner can contend
     with it this step: the partner's committed target (its current cell when it has no commit or
     stays) is within Manhattan 1 of the yielder's cell. A partner whose move keeps it >= 2 away
     cannot enter the yielder's cell and cannot take the cell the yielder moves to. WHY OFFERED: in
     the current shipped arm 7 of the 15 yield stalls >= 10 steps are AWAY from depots (depot distance
     12-36) with a partner that mostly MOVES (a pair travelling together; the higher id yields 3 steps
     in every 4) - no rule of 1-3 touches them, so the validation item "no yield stall >= 10 steps
     while a victim remains" is PREDICTED TO FAIL under rules 1-3 alone. Measured as a separate arm for
     a ruling; it does not change the shipped system.
