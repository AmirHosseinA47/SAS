6. FIREFIGHTER DEATHS - THE RISK METRIC
================================================================================
  Every death classified from the feature's own log:
    engaged      the unit's advance ON its death step had a firefighting plan
    prev         the step before did (its last decision before the fatal tick; a
                 unit can only die on a cell already burning when its advance runs)
    streak       engaged anywhere in its unbroken idle run that ended at death:
                 it reached that ground by firefighting and never left the idle state
    preempted    its latest rescue assignment landed WHILE it was engaged, and it

10. CORRECTIONS THIS ROUND MAKES - TO THE BRIEF AND TO THE RECORD
================================================================================
  C-1 THE DRY ARM IS THE ROUND'S KEY INSTRUMENT; THE BRIEF'S burnt_cells GATE
      WOULD HAVE PROVED NOTHING.
      - Extinguish pushes burnt_cells up, and firebreak pushes it down.
      - The first extinguish re-rolls the rest of the fire, so ANY write - even
        a useless one - moves it.
      - Attribution (DRY == OFF 240/240, WET diverging exactly at the first write)
        is what shows cause. It holds on 23/23 runs (section 3.1).
      - The maintainer recorded this as the fourth time a specified gate measured
        the wrong thing.
  C-2 THE #9 ROUND'S WIDTH-3 FIREBREAK RESULT WAS STRAIGHT-FRONTS-ONLY.
      - A 3-wide grid-step (manhattan) band leaks on a 45-degree front: maximum
        ignition 0.0125 beyond it, both winds.
      - A band 3 wide in straight-line (euclidean) distance blocks completely,
        in every orientation, because distance-to-set is 1-Lipschitz.
      - Measured with the model's own probability_of_fire:
        outputs/_firemech_band_probe.txt, and a unit test.
      - Recorded as a CORRECTION appended to outputs/burntcells_part1.txt, which
        lists where the unqualified claim was carried forward
        (scorched_report.txt:69, gridscale_part1.txt:151).
  C-3 Fire.fuel HAS ONE RUNTIME WRITER, NOT THREE. Fire.burning has three.
      (Re-verified by whole-tree grep, design 0.1.)
  C-4 TWO OF THE BRIEF'S ARMS ARE IDENTITY PROOFS. Extinguish at reach 2 cannot be
      done from ground the 3-cell buffer allows, so "extinguish-only" and "both"
      without suppression are identical (every recorded value) to OFF and to
      firebreak-only.
      Proven 13/13 each. fmES / fmFS / fmDRY replace them.
  C-5 THE APPROVED DESIGN ITSELF HAD A DEFECT, found by adversarial review of the
      implementation before any wave (design addendum C1-C3):
      - under suppression an approach could enter a cell whose retreat distance
        reverts to the buffer on arrival: a zero-work approach<->retreat 2-cycle
        on 28-37 frozen-fire placements per fire, corrected with a look-ahead
      - retreats at the full buffer never latched suspension
      - the exit guard was required far from fire
      A first review pass lost its results to a workflow script error (promises
      passed where thunks were needed); the findings were recovered from the
      journal and verified.
