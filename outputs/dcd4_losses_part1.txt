THE RETURN LEG CAUSES NONE OF THE SIX LOST VICTIMS. THE SPAWN CAUSES ALL SIX.
  - Spawn only at dcD's depots (dlS) reproduces dcD exactly on all four tuples.
  - That covers every firefighter, every victim, and every eval field, over all 240
    steps.
  - The return leg, recharge and the distance-derived reserve are therefore not
    necessary for any of the six. Section 2.0 has the measurement.
  - The brief's working assumption ("the cost is the RETURN LEG") does not hold for
    these losses.

VERDICT: (D) SOMETHING ELSE. The six come from one change (dcD's launch geometry)
and split three ways:
  1. east/707 v0, v3 - a fire-free-world artifact of SHARED searcher code. This tuple
     has no fire after step 3. The shipped hazard gate then turns every searcher step
     into +x on whatever row the searcher is on, so it parks at the east edge. The
     depot launch changes which rows those are. Every NW-launched arm loses the same
     two victims. Closest to (C): not a dcD mechanism.
  2. south/101 v3, south/202 v0 - the NW FIREFIGHTER HOME. The victim was found; the
     nearest free firefighter was 58-65 cells away on a NW berth, or already sent
     there. Moving the firefighters (dlF) saves both. Single-NW arms lose them too.
     (B) for this configuration: the only repair moves units that also carry dcD's
     gains.
  3. south/101 v0, south/404 v0 - SPLIT 2 HOMES ONE SEARCHER AT SE UNDER SOUTH WIND.
     Single-NW (bsspawn, MODE 1) rescues both.
       - A named, scoped candidate (hook: wildfire_model.py _assign_depot_homes
         :738-761). An (A)-type mechanism, not an (A) verdict.
       - The supporting comparison is post hoc and on the data that raised it:
         dcD - dcB rescued is east +6 / south -5 on the 10 committed worlds.
       - It replicates only weakly and confounded on the independent fourth set.
       - On its own tuples it swaps one loss for another (south/404 W dies instead).
       - With it the gate still fails on 3 of 18 seeds.
       - Do NOT implement it (section 8).

======================================================================
NOT FOUR COINCIDENCES. ONE CHANGE, A FIXED SIGNATURE, THREE ROUTES.
  - The spawn change alone suffices for all six (dlS).
  - Victims spawn on the same four cells in every run, so dcD's launch geometry leaves
    a fixed signature. Over all 50 distinct dcD half-role runs (drhD, dfD and d4D,
    de-duplicated):
      - N (25,48) is detected at steps 14-16 in 50 of 50, by the NW-launched searcher
      - S (25,3) is detected at steps 14-16 in 46 of 50, always by the SE-launched
        searcher
      - the nearest firefighter then sits on a NW berth, 65 cells from S
  - All six lost victims are E or S: the two spawns farthest from the NW berths (58 and
    65 cells). The gains are mostly N and W, which D reaches early from nearby.

THE THREE SOUTH LOSSES SHARE ONE EVENT SEQUENCE:
  1. Step 14: the SE searcher, flying west along y=4, detects S; ff_unit_0 is sent from
     NW, md 65.
  2. E is detected late (52 / 68 / 58, vs Z's 9 / 6 / 7).
  3. At E's detection n_available = 0 on all three tuples.
They diverge after that:

  202   the S trip succeeds (76) but leaves the unit at the south edge, 58 cells from E
  404   E's detection is so late that even an immediate dispatch (dlF) fails
The east loss is different: a fire-free world with no dispatch involved at all.

THE WIND HYPOTHESIS. What dcd4 said (dcd4_report.txt:98-121):
  - "+6 of the +7 dead are on south/half"
  - "EXPLORATORY STRATUM VIEW ... Not pre-declared ... Read this as a hypothesis: the

Does it hold here? The sample cannot say:

    p = 0.044). Post hoc; about 36 stratum comparisons; Bonferroni takes it above 1.
  - All four loss tuples are canonical/fresh tuples, inside the prior-27 sample that
    produced the hypothesis. All 5 south seeds are also east seeds in the same 18.
  - Conditioned on Z being at its 4-rescue ceiling: 8/11 south vs 2/5 east (p = 0.30).
  - Wider 57 distinct: tuples losing rescues, south 10/23 vs east 2/27 (p = 0.006).
    Same defects: the hypothesis came from this data.
  - The only within-seed test (the 10 seeds run under both winds): p = 0.34.
  - The fourth set alone: 5/13 vs 1/13 (p = 0.16).
FOUR SEEDS IS FOUR SEEDS. They support no wind claim.

What the mechanism records add:
  - The AGGREGATE dispatch-distance penalty is the same in both winds: first dispatch

STATUS: the wind effect has a plausible, code-level mechanism (the SPLIT 2 home under
