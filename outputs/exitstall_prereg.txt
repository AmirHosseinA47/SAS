VERDICT RULE
  (A) DEFECT WITH A SCOPED FIX: the stall is caused by a defect in code or state that
      a scoped change removes, whether or not it is the hypothesised one. The report
      then says which, and whether it is specific to the ungated configuration.
  (B) REAL BUT INERT: the stall is real, but costs no outcome (no rescue lost, delayed
      past a horizon, or death) on any recorded run.
  (C) NOT AS DESCRIBED: the hypothesised cause (firefighting state leaking into the
      rescue) or the premise (zero with firefighting off) does not hold. (C) can be
      combined with (A) or (B) for the stall itself; the report states both parts.
  Nothing is implemented in this part. A fix, if any, is scoped and the round STOPS
  for confirmation.
