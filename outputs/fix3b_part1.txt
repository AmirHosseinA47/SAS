2. BELIEF (ANALYSE / KNOWLEDGE)
================================================================================
2.1 REPRESENTATION: ONE MAP, EXACT - NOT ONE PER VICTIM.
  - State space per unfound victim: the grid cells, plus an absorbing DEAD state.
  - p_t(c) = P(an unfound victim is alive at c | all observations to t); p_t(DEAD) = the rest.
  - Expected count of unfound, alive victims at c: lambda_t(c) = N_unf(t) * p_t(c).
  - WHY ONE MAP IS EXACT, not an approximation, under the main prior (2.2):
    - The victims are i.i.d. a priori, move independently of each other, and meet the identical sequence
      of disc footprints.
    - The detection of victim j reveals nothing about victim k (independence).
    - So every unfound victim's posterior is the same p_t. The PHD intensity is N_unf * p_t (Mahler 2003,
      first-moment filter). Per-victim maps would hold N_unf identical copies.
  - Cost: O(cells) per step, independent of N.
  - Per-victim maps are needed only for a prior that differs per victim (e.g. last-known positions, H1).
    H1 is not in the main plan (D-1).
  - Renormalising a single PDF after a detection would inflate other cells. The design never does that;
    it decrements N_unf.

2.2 PRIOR - MISSION-BRIEFING INFORMATION ONLY. Each piece is justified as available in a real mission:
  K1 the area of operations: 50 x 50, closed (people are not assumed to leave it). This is the SAR search
     area.
  K2 the number of missing persons N (A 5, B 4, C 3, D 4): from calls and resident registers.
  K3 own depot locations (NW x0-4 y45-49, SE x45-49 y0-4). A person there would be seen, so depots are
     excluded from the prior.
  K4 the reported ignition / fire perimeter at t0, from the tracker UAVs and an external IR or satellite
     feed. Cells burning at t0 are excluded, since a person there is dead or has fled.
  K5 the UAV sensor's detection range (8 cells, a sensor specification).
  PRIOR H0: p_0 uniform over the 50x50 cells minus the depots minus the cells burning at t0; p_0(DEAD) = 0.
  NOT GIVEN (would be oracle or cheating; checked in code review):
    - the ring formula, angles, radii or fixed cells;
    - the flee constants (trigger 3, leash 6, strict improvement, scan order);
    - managed last_known_position (the true spawn);
    - the leaky counts (F6);
    - the per-seed ignition cell ahead of time.
  UNDER THE LEGACY RING ENVIRONMENT, H0 is honest but MISSPECIFIED (every seed places victims on the ring).
  The main evaluation therefore uses a RANDOMISED-SPAWN environment (D-1) in which H0 is EXACTLY the
  generating distribution. The legacy ring runs as a secondary, misspecified-prior condition.

2.3 NEGATIVE-OBSERVATION UPDATE, DERIVED FROM THE DETECTION RULE (F1).
  D_t = the union over ALL on-grid UAVs (every role; trackers make about 30% of first detections) of the
  Euclidean-8 disc at their POST-MOVE cells.
  Source: LocalObservation.current_position / uav_resource_model current_position, written post-move
  (local_uav_monitor.py:318-319; wildfire_model.py:1512). Never the visibility square (F2).
    p(c) <- p(c) * (1 - P_d(c)) / Z,  with Z = 1 - sum_c p(c) P_d(c) over cells and DEAD, and
    P_d(c) = SEARCHER_BELIEF_PD * 1[c in D_t].
  - With the derived P_d = 1 the swept cells are zeroed and only motion refills them.
  - THE SMOKE QUESTION (brief item 1): a smoke-dependent likelihood ("a miss in smoke lowers the belief
    less") has no support in this simulator. Detection ignores smoke (F1).
  - The general form P_d(r, s) is kept as ONE named parameter: SEARCHER_BELIEF_PD_SMOKE, the P_d the
    belief assumes for smoke cells.
    - Its derived value is 1.0 (= the simulator).
    - A pre-registered MISSPECIFICATION sensitivity sets it to 0.5: a cautious operator who distrusts smoke
      pays for weaker negative information.
  - The simulator's detection rule is NOT changed (D-2).

2.4 MOTION (PREDICT) - TWO OPERATOR MODELS, NEITHER THE SIMULATOR'S RULE.
  Fire input: the managing system's fire belief (fire_runtime_model). Today it equals ground truth
  (F6). The paper states this as assumption A3: a near-real-time perimeter from trackers plus an IR feed.
  (a) DIFFUSION (SEARCHER_TARGETING = 2). A lazy 4-neighbour random walk:
      - stay with probability 1 - q; otherwise move to each in-bounds, non-burning neighbour in equal
        share; the grid edge reflects;
      - q = SEARCHER_BELIEF_DIFFUSION_Q = 0.1;
      - it is fire-agnostic in direction.
  (b) FIRE-AWARE FLEE (SEARCHER_TARGETING = 3). A stochastic, soft model an operator could hold, from
      protective-action evidence: people respond to visible flames and smoke, with delay and some
      non-response.
      - d(c) = Euclidean distance to the nearest believed burning cell. The simulator uses Manhattan
        distance with a hard threshold.
      - alarm(c) = logistic((D50 - d)/s), with D50 = 5, s = 1.5. The simulator uses a hard trigger at 3.
      - An alarmed victim moves with probability p_go = 0.8, and stays otherwise.
      - The move is a softmax over {stay, 4 non-burning neighbours} with utility beta * (d(n) - d(c)),
        beta = 1.5. Sideways moves and staying are allowed; there is no strict-improvement rule.
      - There is no leash and no onward-exit rule.
      - An unalarmed victim follows (a) with q = 0.02.
      Parameters are FIXED NOW from the literature, never fitted to simulator runs (fitting would recover
      trigger 3 and leash 6 - the "inverse crime", Kaipio & Somersalo 2005).
  BURN-OVER (both models, and "motion off"): after the predict step, alive mass on a cell believed burning
  moves to DEAD with p_bo = SEARCHER_BELIEF_BURNOVER = 0.5. The operator cannot tell dead from not yet
  found, and the simulator never detects a dead victim.
  MOTION OFF (ablation, SEARCHER_BELIEF_MOTION = 0): the identity transition plus burn-over. Negative
  information then only accumulates.
  Victims move at the UAV's speed (1 cell/step). This is a simulator property the paper states (A6), about
  10x a walking person relative to a multirotor.
2.5 DETECTED VICTIMS LEAVE THE BELIEF: N_unf decrements on each distinct first detection
  (managed.confirmed / the victim_runtime_model record count). p_t is unchanged (2.1). A detected victim's
  later motion is dispatch's business: the simulator tracks it perfectly (A7).
2.6 ORDER PER STEP (matches the simulator; R1/R3):
  - post-move knowledge refresh: PREDICT (fire committed this step) -> MEASURE (post-move discs) ->
    BURN-OVER -> detections (N_unf).
  - Step 1 also applies one MEASURE at the launch geometry before any move.
  - The analyse layer reads the belief pre-move and never mutates it (ARCHITECTURE_BOUNDARIES.md:24-34).
2.7 PLACEMENT AND COST.
  - New knowledge model src_extension/knowledge/victim_search_belief.py (numpy, float64 50x50; numpy 2.2.6
    is in .venv, scipy is not).
  - Constructed in _init_runtime_knowledge, updated from _apply_monitoring_to_knowledge
    (wildfire_model.py:2443-2450) as an explicit call.
  - Not in apply_time_decay, whose order is wrong (update_all_models runs after measurement).
  - Registered in runtime_models for analysis and planning.
  - Draws NO random numbers (the fire stream stays paired).
  - Cost: a few numpy passes over 2500 cells per step.
  - The belief is built ONLY when SEARCHER_TARGETING is in {1, 2, 3}, so the default path allocates
    nothing.

3. TARGETING (PLAN)
================================================================================
3.1 CANDIDATES. The cells t with:
  - distance >= SEARCHER_EDGE_BAND from the grid edge (the disc still reaches the ring from edge distance
    4);
  - on a stride-SEARCHER_BELIEF_STRIDE (2) lattice;
  - Manhattan > 2 from the searcher;
  - and that pass BOTH hard constraints (3.2, 3.3).
3.2 HARD REACHABILITY (the binding constraint, outputs/session3b_first_constraint.txt).
  - One BFS per searcher per plan over the ADMISSIBLE set of A1-R's fire mode:
    - not burning, not strict smoke;
    - burning distance >= T = min(d0, SEARCHER_ROUTE_CLEARANCE = 6);
    - no other UAV;
    - A1-D's edge-band rule: enter a band cell only by strictly reducing penetration.
  - t is admissible iff the BFS reaches within Manhattan 2 of it. L(t) = the BFS path length (not
    Manhattan).
  - The blocked-set construction is EXTRACTED from _route_within_fire_field (uav_executor.py:2165-2185,
    :2221-2229) into one helper, used by A1-R, the planner's check and the executor's steer. That gives one
    definition, no drift, and ONE OWNER. The identity arm proves A1-R unchanged by the extraction.
  - Ablation SEARCHER_TARGETING_REACHABILITY = 0 (never shipped): candidates are all non-band cells, with
    L = Manhattan distance. The steer still uses the admissible BFS and falls back when there is no path.
    That reproduces "target choice blind to reachability".
3.3 HARD BATTERY FEASIBILITY (F8). Admit t only if
      b - 0.3 * L(t) > agents.rtb_trigger_level_at(uav, t)
  - 0.3 = drain per moving step; the trigger is the live one evaluated at the target, and '>' is strict
    as in the trigger.
  - A chosen target is never cut off by the return trigger before arrival, and the existing margin (39.23,
    free-cell depot distance) is kept.
  - It uses the BFS length, so it is stricter than the trigger's Manhattan distance.
  - B3 (scenario B) stays the only mechanism below tau.
  - Ablation SEARCHER_TARGETING_BATTERY = 0: no filter. The role-agnostic return override still fires, so
    the ablation measures legs cut off mid-way.
3.4 SCORE: EXPECTED DETECTION GAIN PER UNIT TRAVEL TIME (Black / Blackwell index with travel cost; Lau et
  al. 2008). Two stages, pure numpy:
  (i)  S = lambda convolved with the disc (197 shifted adds). Pre-rank by S(t) / max(L(t), L0), with
       L0 = 4 as a floor against near-zero paths. Keep the top M = 12.
  (ii) For each of the 12: G(t) = the sum of lambda over the UNION of discs along the BFS path to t,
       including t (a dilation of the path mask). score(t) = G(t) / max(L(t), L0).
       "Mass the footprint would cover along and at the target, per unit path length", as briefed.
  - Scored on the CURRENT belief, not T^L(b), a stated myopic simplification. Motion is local (victims move
    only near fire), and the along-path term partly compensates.
  - No optimality claim: ratio-greedy has no guarantee (Khuller et al. 1999; Trummel & Weisinger 1986).
3.5 COMMITMENT - THE ANTI-OSCILLATION RULE. Index rules switch back and forth when indices swap after each
  negative update (Lossner & Wegener 1982), which is exactly an (O)-type mechanism. So a target is HELD
  and re-planned ONLY when:
  (a) reached (Manhattan <= 2);
  (b) no admissible path (re-checked every step). The target goes on a G = 15 step cooldown (cells within
      3 of it are excluded) and counts as a GIVE-UP;
  (c) battery-infeasible;
  (d) swept: S(held) < 0.25 * S at issue (another UAV or the fire took its mass).
  - There is no score-based switching. Re-checking every step, not only at issue, is what makes
    reachability hold during the leg in a moving fire.
3.6 FALLBACK. No admissible, feasible candidate with S > 0: the CURRENT behaviour for that searcher, and a
  R = 10 step hold before the new strategy tries again, so the two steerers cannot alternate step by step.

================================================================================
4. COORDINATION (PLAN, JOINT)
================================================================================
  GREEDY SEQUENTIAL ALLOCATION WITH FICTITIOUS NON-DETECTION.
  - Held targets go first (their path discs are conditioned out).
  - Then the free searchers in order of their best stand-alone score (ties by uav id). Each picks its best
    target on lambda' = lambda with the discs along the already-assigned paths zeroed.
  WHY NOT HUNGARIAN:
  - Kuhn-Munkres is optimal only for ADDITIVE utilities. Overlapping footprints make detection submodular,
    not additive.
  - Hungarian would need footprint-disjoint candidates, a stricter candidate set than we have.
  WHY GREEDY:
  - The detection objective is monotone submodular and "one target per searcher" is a partition matroid,
    so greedy on the GAIN objective is within 1/2 of optimal (Fisher, Nemhauser & Wolsey 1978; Hollinger et
    al. 2009; Singh et al. 2009).
  - This bound is for gain, not for our ratio score. It is stated as motivation, not as a guarantee.
  - Cost per step: O(m * M * L * 197) numpy work, with m <= 2 searchers in B/C/D, 1 in A, and a few under
    GLOBAL_PLANNER_MODE = 1.
  - Negligible against the 2.0 s the model already spends per step. Measured and reported (10.4 E8).
  Ablation SEARCHER_TARGETING_COORDINATION = 0: each searcher chooses on the unconditioned lambda, so
  overlap is possible.

       - where: the no-victim branch of UAVExecutor._resolve_direction_intent, after uav_executor.py:1337

       - it acts only if SEARCHER_TARGETING is in {1, 2, 3}, AND the ctx marker's step == this step (a

       - it clears _last_escape_method on entry (V1 (d));
       - steer = the first step of the SAME admissible BFS as 3.2 (the shared helper), and the first step

       - label 'victim_search_targeting', _last_escape_method = 'targeting_bfs';
       - no path -> return None -> the current chain runs. That is a planner/executor mismatch, and it is

  GATE EXEMPTION. execute() exempts the label from _apply_victim_searcher_hazard_gate only together with
    _last_escape_method == 'targeting_bfs', as A1-R's routed steps are exempt.

      the fire near-field (clearance T), smoke cells (never entered).
    - NOT covered (V1 (c), the same trade as A1-R): the gate's smoke-DISTANCE near-field retreat and its
      buffer lookahead.
    - Reported, not hidden: searcher strict fire/smoke steps and smoke-proximity steps, per arm.
  THE OLD CHAIN'S STATE. On a targeting step _sync_wind_search_execution_state is skipped, so the fallback
    resumes from its own, unadvanced state. The hook does not assume wind_state is untouched this step: the

  EMERGENCY safe_hold (V1 (b)). The hook acts wherever the current no-victim chain acts today, including
    under an EMERGENCY safe_hold (where current searchers already search). This keeps the behaviour class
    unchanged; D-8 asks for a ruling.
  ROLE CHANGES (GLOBAL_PLANNER_MODE = 1). The per-searcher commitment store is added to

6. BASELINE AND REFERENCE ARMS
================================================================================
6.1 LEAST-OBSERVED (SEARCHER_TARGETING = 1) - the lean design, kept, with two evidence-driven corrections.
  - KEPT: the 7x7 tiles on the band's interior (36 on 50x50); the anchor = the interior centre;
    reached = Manhattan 2; tile age = mean age over searchable cells, with never-covered = now + 1; ties
    within 10 steps, then the shortest admissible path; hold until reached; give-up cooldown G = 15;
    fallback hold R = 10; the same admissible BFS as the reachability check and the steer.
  - CORRECTION 1 (F2): "age" comes from the belief model's per-cell last-DISC-cover step, not the
    visibility map (wrong footprint, smoke and environment-monitor stamps). Both arms then see the same
    sensor.
  - CORRECTION 2 (F7): the choice is delivered through the same planner post-pass + hook as the Bayesian
    arms. Its coordination is "exclude tiles other searchers hold"; with coordination off there is no
    exclusion. Its battery filter is 3.3.
  - Literature position: idleness-driven patrolling (Machado et al. 2002), i.e. a frontier / coverage
    heuristic.
6.2 RANDOM WALK (SEARCHER_TARGETING = 4), equivalent to the exemplar (F9).
  - Searchers only.
  - Each step, one uniform draw from {0,1,2,3} per searcher, CONSUMED EVERY STEP (docked or not), so the
    stream stays aligned.
  - It uses a DEDICATED stream seeded from the SYSTEM_RANDOM state digest with a salt distinct from
    _make_ff_absence_rng's (wildfire_model.py:2610-2626), created in reset() before set_fire_agents. It
    never draws from the fire stream.
  - Committed through _commit_execution_direction, so return-to-base, docking and the move-refusal rule
    are identical to every arm.
  - PRIMARY: ungated, as in the exemplar. SEARCHER_TARGETING_RW_GATED = 1 passes it through the searcher
    hazard gate (a control).
  - UAVs are fire-immune, so being ungated costs exposure only. Koopman's random-search law
    1 - exp(-W L / A) is plotted as the analytic reference.
6.3 CURRENT (SEARCHER_TARGETING = 0, DEFAULT): the shipped fix3a searcher, byte for byte.

  -> SAR datum / scenario priors (IAMSAR Vol. II; USCG/Soza 1996; SAROPS, Kratzke, Stone & Frost 2010);

  -> nothing new.

  -> POC_after = POC_before (1 - POD) (USCG 1996 eq. 2-4); Stone 1975; Koch 2007
  -> nothing new; ours is P_d = 1 on the derived disc.
- Moving-target prediction
  -> Markov-chain moving-target search (Pollock 1970; Brown 1980; Washburn 1983; Eagle 1984; Stone,
     Royset & Washburn 2016); recursive Bayesian UAV search (Bourgault, Furukawa & Durrant-Whyte
     2003-2006; Lanillos et al. 2012/2014); reactive / escape-aware maps (Liao et al., Sensors 2024)
  -> the kernel is conditioned on the LIVE FIRE (a hazard the target flees). Fire-driven human motion
     exists only in evacuation simulators (Zheng et al. 2011 fire floor field; WUI-NITY 2021) and learned
     policies (Mendoza et al. 2025, PPO), not as the predict step of an explicit belief. "Not found" rests
     on web search only (see below).

  -> Black / Blackwell index; Lau et al. 2008 travel costs; switching pathology (Lossner & Wegener 1982)
  -> nothing new; the commitment rule is an engineering answer to a known pathology.
- Hard reachability
  -> constrained-path search (Stewart 1979; Eagle & Yee 1990); risk-constrained searcher (Sato & Royset
     2010); SafeMDP (Turchetta et al. 2016); shielding (Alshiekh et al. 2018)
  -> a TIME-VARYING admissible set from the same fire field as the belief, re-checked each step, with ONE
     owner for check and steer.
- Battery feasibility
  -> fuel constraints (Sato & Royset 2010); SAROPS endurance; Di Franco & Buttazzo 2016; Sundar &
     Rathinam 2014
  -> nothing new.
- Joint allocation
  -> sequential submodular allocation (Nemhauser et al. 1978; Hollinger et al. 2009; Singh et al. 2009;
     Corah & Michael 2019); utility-minus-cost exploration (Burgard et al. 2005); SAROPS no-overlap
  -> nothing new.
- Least-observed baseline
  -> idleness-based patrolling (Machado et al. 2002); frontier exploration (Yamauchi 1997)
  -> a baseline.
- MAPE-K placement
  -> MAPE-K (Kephart & Chess 2003); uncertainty-reduction tactics (Moreno et al. 2018); UAV exemplars
     (DARTSim, DragonFly, UNDERSEA, SUAVE, Dronology); wildfire exemplar line (Vilchez, Troya & Camara,
     SEAMS 2024, fire tracking); MAPE-K-HMT (Cleland-Huang et al., TAAS 2024)
  -> extends the wildfire exemplar line from fire tracking to victim search, with a posterior in K.

  -> search theory reports POS and time to detection; stability as a control property in SAS (Filieri et

  -> pre-registered structural gates (never-finish, oscillation of every kind) next to POS / time to

12. WHAT EACH FINDING MEANS FOR THE PAPER
================================================================================
  - F1 (perfect, smoke-blind sensor). The derived model is cookie-cutter detection: this exemplar has no
    imperfect-sensor effect to exploit. Reviewers will ask about smoke; the answer is the misspecification
    sensitivity and a stated limitation, not a claimed likelihood.
  - F3 (fixed ring). Every earlier result in this project is conditional on one victim geometry. A belief
    method evaluated there would be tested against a misspecified prior on 3-5 fixed cells, with launch-
    detected victims dominating. The randomised-spawn environment is what makes a Bayesian claim testable.
    It is an exemplar change and must be reported as one.
  - F4 (flee only near fire). The expected BF-vs-BD difference is small unless victims are near fire. The
    motion-awareness claim is H(A5) and may be null. A null is reported as a result.
  - F6 (omniscient fire picture). BF's advantage is an upper bound under perfect fire knowledge (assumption
    A3); a tracker-only fire picture is future work.
  - F7 (waypoints not live). The paper documents that the MAPE-K plan-to-execute channel for searchers was
    dead in the exemplar, and describes the delivery that makes it live. That is a finding for SAS
    engineering, and the same class as this project's earlier silent-delivery findings.
  - F10 (variance). The n = 10 per cell plan detects about 10-20 step shifts; smaller effects are reported
    as CIs, not significance.
  - If G-O falls to 0 with the reachability ablation bringing it back: direct evidence for the causal
    claim of session3b_first_constraint.txt. If not: the claim is not supported at this layer, and the
    episode classes say which layer owns the rest.

15.4 D-7 - THE GREEDY GUARANTEE, STATED PRECISELY
  - OBJECTIVE. Expected detected mass F(S) = sum_c lambda(c) * (1 - prod_{s in S} (1 - P_d,s(c))),
    where S is a set of (searcher, target) choices, each covering the union of discs along its path and
    at its target. F is a weighted coverage function, so it is MONOTONE and SUBMODULAR. This holds for
    P_d = 1 and for the PD_SMOKE < 1 sensitivity (probabilistic coverage is submodular too).
  - (1 - 1/e) DOES NOT APPLY to our allocation. That bound (Nemhauser, Wolsey & Fisher 1978) is for
    greedy under a CARDINALITY constraint: any k elements from one common ground set. Our constraint is
    "one target per searcher", and each searcher has its OWN candidate set (its own reachability,
    battery and path). That is a PARTITION MATROID. For it, greedy - including the "locally greedy"
    variant that visits searchers in a fixed order, as ours does - is guaranteed 1/2 (Fisher, Nemhauser &
    Wolsey 1978). (1 - 1/e) under a matroid needs the continuous-greedy / pipage algorithm (Calinescu,
    Chekuri, Pal & Vondrak, SIAM J. Comput. 40(6), 2011), which we do not use.
  - The 1/2 bound holds for the GAIN objective F. We greedily maximise the RATIO gain / path length,
    which carries NO guarantee (Khuller, Moss & Naor 1999).
  - So the paper states: the allocation objective is monotone submodular; greedy on F would be within 1/2
    of optimal under the partition-matroid constraint; our ratio-greedy is a heuristic motivated by, not
    covered by, that bound. Per-step cost: O(m * M * L * 197) numpy work, measured.

16.2 R-3 BURN-OVER = 0.1 PER STEP (was 0.5).
     CHANGED AFTER THE SCREEN AND BEFORE THE MEASUREMENT ROUND - the paper discloses this.
     Reasoning:
       - Victims flee fire, so a burning cell does not imply its victim is likely dead.
       - The predict step runs before burn-over, so mass that flees has already left the burning cell.
       - Burn-over therefore mostly judges the belief's imprecise placement of the front. The front
         advances about as fast as a victim, so a victim at the front is most likely one step ahead.
       - At 0.5, one step of overlap removed half the mass. In the screen, that moved 81% of an alive,
         never-found victim's mass to DEAD (report 4.3).
       - At 0.1 the half-life is about 6.6 steps, under a quarter of a cell's 21-30-step burn, so mass that
         truly stays in fire is still mostly removed within one burn.
     The value is NOT fitted to simulator runs.
     SENSITIVITY (10.7, replacing {0.2, 0.8}): p_bo in {0.2, 0.5}. 0.5 = the original pre-registered value
     (ruled); 0.2 = the original lower point.
