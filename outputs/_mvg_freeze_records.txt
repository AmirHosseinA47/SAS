22.9 PER-DEATH REVIEW - THE CAUSATION RULE FOR FIREFIGHTER DEATHS IN ARMS M AND MU
Written 2026-10-07 ~07:58, BEFORE any knockout replay finished or was read (31 replays queued at 07:52).

QUESTION (maintainer): was each firefighter death in arm M CAUSED by a fix step?

EVIDENCE PER DEATH (unit U dies at step t in arm X in {M, MU}, cell c):
  E0  arm 0 of cell c (the committed record): does U die at t, at another step, or survive?
  E1  the records of X: U's own LIVE fix events (mv_events, kind a / b), the leg it died on, route_blocked raises,
      unassigns, its last cells; the dying leg = from U's last bind up to its death, or, for an unbound unit, the
      leg that ended in the unassign that left it unbound.
  R0  replay of X's run line with boards (must reproduce the record exactly; else the death is UNRESOLVED).
  KO-OWN     replay with U's own (a)/(b) decisions knocked out at every step <= t (today's step instead).
  KO-LAST    replay with only U's LAST own live fix decision before t knocked out.
  KO-OTHERS  replay with every OTHER unit's (a)/(b) decisions knocked out at every step <= t (U's own stay live).
  "Survives" in a replay = U is alive at step t (its later fate is reported, not used).

CLASSES (first that applies):
  C-NONE   U dies at the same step t in arm 0 and U's rows are identical between arm 0 and X up to t
           (the fixes did not touch this death). Also: U's death precedes the cell's first divergence.
  C-PROX   CAUSED BY ITS OWN FIX STEP (proximate): (i) U took >= 1 live fix step on its dying leg; AND
           (ii) U survives in KO-OWN; AND (iii) the route-closure chain holds: after U's last own fix step on the
           leg, the fire closed U's way (a route_blocked raise for U, or U's clean distance to its target became
           infinite) before U reached its target, and U died without being re-bound to a route it could pass.
           Sub-label "single step" when U also survives in KO-LAST.
  C-BUTFOR FIX-DEPENDENT, NOT PROXIMATE BY MECHANISM: (i) and (ii) hold but (iii) does not.
  C-DOWN   DOWNSTREAM: not the above, and U survives at t in arm 0 (or dies at another step): the death follows
           from the fixes' change of the run (other units' fix steps, the changed fire, binds or timing), not from
           U's own fix step. Sub-label "via others' fix steps" when U survives in KO-OTHERS.
  UNRESOLVED R0 does not reproduce, or the evidence conflicts; stated with the conflict.
A death of the same unit at the same step in X and in arm 0 with differing rows before t is reported as
C-NONE only if U's own rows are identical up to t; otherwise it is classed by the rules above.

LIMIT, stated: a knockout changes the trajectory after the knocked-out decision; survival in a knockout is a but-for
statement about that decision under this run's CRN fire, not a claim that the step was unsafe. (iii) is what makes a
but-for result a STRANDING caused by the step.
