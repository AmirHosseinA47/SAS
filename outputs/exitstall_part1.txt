  THE HYPOTHESIS
    State from the firefighting mode survives into the rescue and drives the
    carrying leg.

    (C) NOT AS DESCRIBED.
    Refuted by code reading and by the probe on all 11 stalled legs:
      - No firefighting or idle field is read on any carrying step.
      - The engaged retreat range K is never read on a carrying step.
      - Nothing but _move_toward moves a carrying unit.
      - Clearing the fields on the carrying leg changes nothing, position for
        position.
      - The hypothesised state was simply ABSENT on 6 of the 7 ungated stalled
        legs. Only south/half/1048395951 carried any firefighting field
        (_firefight_suspended=True), and it was never read.

  THE PREMISE: "zero in control, present only when firefighting is on"
    The literal premise is FALSE.
      - With the fire mechanic inactive, 32 of 909 distinct recorded legs stall.
        The mechanic is inactive when a run has no firefight_log; 57 such runs
        were configured with FF flags but never engaged. None of the 32 depends
        on them.
      - The CRN control (11c3661 code, mechanic off) stalls on the same victims
        as the ungated arms.
    The matched comparisons show no detectable net increase in the NUMBER of
    stalled completed legs:
      - pooled OFF 10/340 vs ON 11/333
      - Mantel-Haenszel odds ratio 1.1
      - 95% CI for the difference: -2.4 to +3.2 points
    That cannot exclude an increase, and the strata disagree: on U30 stock it is
    0/104 vs 5/102 (Fisher p=0.03).
    With the mechanic on, stalls are LONGER and carries break MORE OFTEN:
      - excess steps on stalled legs: 478 ON vs 308 OFF
      - broken carrying legs: 7 ON vs 3 OFF, mostly round 2's 5 vs 1

  THE STALL ITSELF
    (A), provisionally. A defect with a scoped candidate fix whose effect is
    ESTIMATED (offline replay, fire held fixed: stalled legs 56 -> 20), not
    measured.

    It is the carrying leg's movement rule. _move_toward is a memoryless greedy
    step that prefers any fire-clear cell to progress. The exit cell is fixed at
    pickup, blind to fire, and it is the only cell that completes the rescue.

    Why (A) here, when a78f393 ruled the same tier-1/3 cycle (B) on the APPROACH
    leg:
      - On the approach, half of the cycles (31/62) were ended by the survival
        retreat.
      - On a carrying leg nothing can repair the cycle: no retreat, no path
        search.
      - The stall has a measured outcome cost:
          - it pushes rescues past step 240;
          - its broken tail ends in the victim's death (section 6).

    Is it specific to the ungated configuration?
      - The MECHANISM is not. The code is identical in the control and on this
        branch, and dates from the initial import a4a01b4. The control stalls
        too.
      - The ungated configuration does EXPOSE MORE CARRIES to it: on U30 stock,
        5 stalled legs vs 0 (248 vs 0 excess steps).
      - For 2 of those 5, the cause is shown to be when the carry starts
        (section 5, H3).
      - For the other 3, the shipped arm's fire writes and unit allocation also
        differ before the carry, and the cause is not isolated. No stored state
        drives any of the 5.

    The fix is scoped in section 8 and NOT implemented. Every candidate changes
    the control and every baseline too, so it needs its own measured round.

  BEFORE MERGING

5. H2 / H3 - THE MECHANISM, AND WHY THE ARMS DIFFER
================================================================================
  THE RULE (agents.py:2369-2464; static, and recomputed per step by the probe)
    Candidates
      The in-grid 4-neighbours, in the fixed order +x, -x, +y, -y (1958-1965).
      Burning cells are dropped. Each remaining cell is flagged:
        improving
        fire-adjacent
        smoky
        preferred (the larger-axis step)
    Tiers
      tier 1  improving and clean (not fire-adjacent, not smoky)
      tier 2  maintaining and clean. Always empty: one step always changes the
              manhattan distance by 1.
      tier 3  ANY clean cell. In practice this is a step AWAY from the exit,
              chosen by neighbour order alone: the preferred cell is never in
              tier 3.
      tier 4  least risk over all non-burning cells, where risk is
              100 x fire-adjacent + 10 x smoky, then distance.
    - No memory. The unit always moves unless every in-grid neighbour burns. In
      that case it holds and raises route_blocked (2411-2413): the gateway to
      instance (i).
    - On a carrying leg the survival retreat is off (1973) and the path search
      is off (2388).
    - Only a change in the fire or smoke lets a cycle continue to a
      completion. It can end WITHOUT a rescue three ways:
        the route_blocked drop
        the 30-step isolation timeout
        carrier death
  THE CYCLE
    - At p the improving cell is fire-adjacent or smoky, so tier 3 steps back
      to q.
    - At q, p is clean and improving again, so tier 1 steps forward.
    - The tiers run 3,1,3,1... for as long as the board holds.
    - The exit cell, the only improving cell from distance 1:
        burning -> always refused.
        fire-adjacent or smoky -> refused whenever a lower-risk non-burning
          neighbour exists; otherwise entered in tier 4 (e.g. 2070841104,
          step 111).
    - Why barriers persist:
        - Burning is re-drawn every fire tick, so cells re-ignite. The exit
          cell of 1433805104 burned 4 times between 210 and 249.
        - Smoke is one-shot per cell. After a cell stops burning, its smoke
          lasts about 4 more steps if it burned to exhaustion. If it went out
          with fuel left, it lasts up to about 30-39 steps.
        - Observed post-fire smoke tails in the traces: 1-36 steps, median 3.
    - Entering a non-burning exit cell cannot be fatal:
        - The casualty check on the entering step passes, because fire changes
          only in Fire.advance, which runs before every firefighter.
        - On the next step the rescue completes (1820), and the removal runs
          before the casualty check.
        - So the refusal protects against nothing the model scores.

  XS_RESET=assign clears the same fields on the unit's first on-grid advance
  with a target after an advance without one. Assignments made while a unit is
  off grid are not reset; none of those carried a stale field here.
  6 of the 7 runs were no-op resets: every field was already default.
  The one that bit is south/half/1048395951.

  The latch
    - ff_unit_0 was enclosed at (18,0), all three neighbours burning.

      latched _idle_retreat_stalled.

      extinguish at step 65 (T=1, dist 2) had left the unit in.
    - In positioning-only the same unit went west at 66 and never entered the
      pocket.
    - So the code is pre-mechanic idle-retreat code, but the situation was
      created by firefighting.

  What the stale latch changed
    - The unit was assigned at 78.
    - Recorded run, step 79: the stale latch sent it down
      _assigned_one_step_retreat (2077-2080). That failed, so _move_toward ran;

      followed.

      unchanged (263).

    - A genuine instance of the class. Nearly inert here; its frequency in the
      wider record is unmeasured.
