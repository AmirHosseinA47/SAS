VERDICT IN ONE PARAGRAPH
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
A1 closes as DIAGNOSED, FIX ATTEMPTED, MEASURED HARMFUL. Three penalty
magnitudes spanning two orders of magnitude (+1, +10, +100) were run seed-matched
over all 23 runs. All three produce the SAME outcome: firefighter_deaths 6 -> 8
on the 13-run sample and 14 -> 17 pooled, against a small victim-side gain
(rescued 29 -> 30 / 51 -> 52, victims dead 23 -> 22 / 37 -> 36). None of the
three named deaths moved - two of those three runs came out trajectory-identical
under every arm, so the penalty did not act on them at all. The displacement
check, which the brief named as the specific failure mode to guard against,
fails decisively: on the runs an arm actually perturbed, firefighter time on
unburnt green ground rose +19.8 pp while time on scorched ground fell only
-1.0 pp, because the penalty evicted units from BURNT ground (-18.8 pp) - the
only ground in the model with a guaranteed-zero future hazard. Two independent
measurements explain why this is structural rather than a tuning failure:
(i) at matched ignition probability, scorched and virgin ground ignite at
indistinguishable rates, so `has_burned` carries no hazard information that the
existing fire-adjacency term does not already carry; and (ii) burnt ground is
essentially never adjacent to green ground (1 of 10,933 burnt-cell observations),
so the scorched band is the mandatory boundary layer of the scar and taxing it
puts a toll on the only door to the safest ground in the model. RECOMMENDATION:
ship nothing. The diagnosis in outputs/burntcells_part1.txt stands as a correct
description of the code; the proposed remedy is measured to be counterproductive.

1. WHERE A NEW TERM WOULD ENTER, AND WHAT IT COMPETES AGAINST
================================================================================

1.1  THE FUNCTION, QUOTED IN FULL (agents.py:665-673)
------------------------------------------------------
    def _firefighter_cell_risk(self, cell: tuple[int, int]) -> int:
        if self._cell_contains_active_fire(cell):
            return 1_000_000
        risk = 0
        if self._cell_adjacent_to_fire(cell):
            risk += 100
        if self._cell_has_active_smoke(cell):
            risk += 10
        return risk

Every term is present-tense. There is no notion of future risk anywhere.

1.2  EVERY CALLER, WHOLE TREE (4 sites, all in agents.py)
----------------------------------------------------------
  :799  _survival_move                 current_risk = risk(own cell)
  :888  _retreat_candidates            risk = risk(each neighbour candidate)
  :983  _revalidate_idle_retreat_stall current_risk = risk(own cell)
  :1119 _move_toward                   risk = risk(each free neighbour)
There are no others; the grep is clean tree-wide.

1.3  THE RANKING THAT CONSUMES IT (_pick_improving_retreat, agents.py:917-956)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
        ideal_reachable = [c for c in candidates if c["ideal"]]
        if ideal_reachable:
            return max(ideal_reachable, key=lambda c: (c["dist"], -int(c["risk"])))
        improving = [
            c for c in candidates
            if int(c["improvement"]) > 0
            or (int(c["risk"]) < current_risk and int(c["dist"]) >= current_dist)
        ]
        if improving:
            return max(improving, key=lambda c: (int(c["improvement"]),
                                                 int(c["dist"]), -int(c["risk"])))
        return None

  plus three more consumers of the same per-candidate `risk`, all in
  _survival_move: the no-improvement fallback max(candidates, (dist, -risk)),
  and the at-cap branch max(required_reachable, (dist, -risk)).

  In EVERY one of those keys `risk` is the LAST component. It never outranks
  fire distance; it only breaks exact distance ties. The one place `risk` is
  load-bearing rather than a tiebreak is the `improving` admission clause:
  raising current_risk widens the set of neighbours that count as an
  improvement without gaining fire distance.

1.4  THE APPROACH PATH (_move_toward, agents.py:1081-1176) - THE KEY FINDING
----------------------------------------------------------------------------
The tier chain that moves an assigned unit toward its victim is:

    tier 1: improving   and not adjacent_fire and not smoke   -> min(dist_after, preferred)
    tier 2: maintaining and not adjacent_fire and not smoke   -> min(dist_after, preferred)
    tier 3:                 not adjacent_fire and not smoke   -> min(dist_after, preferred)
    tier 4 (chosen is None): min(scored, key=(risk, dist_after, preferred))

TIERS 1, 2 AND 3 NEVER READ `risk` AT ALL. They filter on adjacent_fire/smoke
and rank on distance-to-target. `risk` is consulted only in the tier-4 fallback,
which is reached only when EVERY free neighbour is fire-adjacent or smoky - and
a scorched cell that is neither would have been taken by tiers 1-3 before tier 4
was ever reached, so in tier 4 the new term is again only a within-class
tiebreak among already-bad cells.

CONSEQUENCE, DERIVABLE BEFORE ANY RUN: on the normal approach to a victim -
which is exactly the s26-s36 window the three named deaths need - a term added
to _firefighter_cell_risk is INERT AT ANY MAGNITUDE, because the code path that
chooses the step does not consult risk. This is a structural property of the
tier chain, not a question of how large the penalty is. Section 4.5 confirms it
empirically: all three named units still enter their death cell under every arm.

1.5  WHAT A NEW TERM OF SIZE S WOULD COMPETE AGAINST
-----------------------------------------------------
Stock risk values are drawn from {0, 10, 100, 110}. Adding S to scorched cells:
  S <  10  cannot reorder any smoke-vs-clean or adjacent-vs-clear decision;
           acts only where those present-tense terms already tie.
  S == 10  a scorched clean cell ties a green smoky cell, so risk can no longer
           separate them and the -risk tiebreak falls through to list order.
  S == 100 a scorched clean cell ties a green fire-adjacent cell.
This is what the three arms in section 3 were chosen to span.

2. CONFIRMING THE PREDICATE
================================================================================

Proposed: scorched == has_burned and not burnt and fuel > 0 and not burning.

JOINTLY NECESSARY AND SUFFICIENT, verified against the Fire state machine
(agents.py:22-125):

  * `burnt` is written True only at agents.py:109-110, guarded by
    `if self.has_burned and self.fuel <= 0`. `fuel` is written in exactly two
    places - the constructor (7..10) and the strict subtraction at :107-108 -
    so it can never increase. Therefore burnt => fuel <= 0 permanently, and
    `fuel > 0` already implies `not burnt`. The `not burnt` clause is redundant
    but harmless, and it is kept as a guard in case the two ever diverge.
  * The decrement and the burnt test sit in the same throttled block, on
    consecutive lines, so fuel <= 0 sets burnt in the same tick. There is no
    reachable state with has_burned and fuel <= 0 and not burnt.
  * `not burning` is necessary because a burning cell must keep returning
    1_000_000 from the fire branch, which precedes any new term.
  * SUFFICIENT for "already burned once and can still ignite":
    probability_of_fire (agents.py:53-84) short-circuits to 0 only when burnt,
    and otherwise gates on `self.fuel > 0`. A cell satisfying the predicate
    therefore has a live, non-zero ignition path.

  The four states are mutually exclusive and exhaustive: burning / burnt /
  scorched / virgin-green, with green == not has_burned exactly.

READABLE AT SCORING TIME WITHOUT ANY NEW REFERENCE - YES. `has_burned`,
`burnt`, `fuel` and `is_burning()` all live on the Fire agent, reachable through
the identical `self.model.grid.get_cell_list_contents([cell])` +
`type(agent) is Fire` path that _cell_contains_active_fire (agents.py:629-638)
and _cell_has_active_smoke (agents.py:641-656) already use.
_firefighter_cell_risk already holds `self.model.grid`. No model reference, no
new plumbing, no cached state. The helper used in the arms is 15 lines and
mirrors the two existing predicates exactly.

  IMPORTANT COROLLARY, established from the same source read and confirmed
  empirically in section 5.1: probability_of_fire NEVER READS has_burned. It
  reads the cell own fuel as a gate and then sums over BURNING neighbours in a
  Moore radius-3 disc. A scorched cell and a virgin cell facing the same burning
  neighbourhood therefore draw from the identical distribution. `has_burned` is
  a POSITIONAL PROXY for "inside or on the rim of the scar", not a material
  property of the ground. This is the fact the whole round turns on.

5. WHY IT IS HARMFUL - TWO INDEPENDENT MECHANISM MEASUREMENTS
================================================================================

5.1  SCORCHED GROUND IS NOT MORE IGNITABLE. IT IS BETTER POSITIONED.
---------------------------------------------------------------------
outputs/_sc_mech2.py snapshots every non-burning cell with fuel > 0 BEFORE each
model.step() and resolves it after (mesa runs step() and advance() inside one
model.step(), so ignition is already committed on return). cell_prob read
afterwards is exactly the clamped probability that step drew against.

  IGNITION RATE PER EXPOSURE, BY cell_prob BAND    (D/east 101 | D/south 404)
      cell_prob band     scorched    virgin  |  scorched    virgin
      0.02 - 0.05           3.4%      3.2%   |     3.3%      3.4%
      0.05 - 0.10           7.7%      8.1%   |     6.7%      7.4%
      0.10 - 0.20          16.0%     14.6%   |    14.4%     15.1%
      0.20 - 0.35          25.3%     26.5%   |    26.4%     23.0%
      0.50 - 0.75          69.0%     68.6%   |    72.4%     70.2%
      0.75 - 1.01          74.3%     78.1%   |    74.2%     77.1%

  AT MATCHED PROBABILITY THE TWO POPULATIONS ARE INDISTINGUISHABLE, in every
  band, in both seeds - exactly as the source predicts, since
  probability_of_fire never reads has_burned. The entire difference is exposure:

      mean cell_prob per exposure   scorched 0.2104  virgin 0.0119   (east 101)
                                    scorched 0.1978  virgin 0.0291   (south 404)

  Virgin cells sit at cell_prob exactly 0 in 117,499 of 129,420 exposures
  (90.8%, east 101) because nothing near them is burning. Scorched cells are, by
  construction, the cells that were near the fire.

  THIS ALSO RECALIBRATES THE 96% FIGURE. Part 2 reported that 96.6% of scorched
  cells re-ignite, against 0 for burnt cells. Both halves are correct, but the
  comparator that makes 96% look alarming was never measured. Over the same
  runs the VIRGIN population ignites at 63.5% (east 101) and 95.7% (south 404) -
  because by step 240 the fire has reached most of the grid. The 96% figure is
  mostly "the fire eventually gets nearly everywhere", not evidence that
  scorched ground is a distinct hazard class.

  And the one real physical difference runs the wrong way for the fix: a
  scorched cell has partially depleted fuel, so if it does relight it burns for
  FEWER steps than virgin ground would. On physics, scorched ground is weakly
  SAFER than virgin ground at equal neighbour configuration.

5.2  SCORCHED GROUND IS THE ONLY DOOR INTO THE BLACK
------------------------------------------------------
FIREFIGHTER STEP TRANSITIONS, arm none, all 23 runs:

      from \ to    green     scorched   burnt     burning
      green        2881      53         0         0
      scorched     36        103        32        0
      burnt        0         24         162       0
      burning      0         0          0         0

  green -> burnt: ZERO, ever. burnt -> green: ZERO, ever. All 32 first entries
  onto burnt ground came off a scorched cell - 100.0%.

GRID GEOMETRY (outputs/_sc_geom.py, 3 seeds x 4 snapshots at steps 60/120/180/240):

      burnt cells with at least one GREEN 4-neighbour:
          1 of 10,933 burnt-cell observations   (0.009%)
      4-neighbour mix of burnt cells, across all snapshots:
          green 0.00-0.02%   scorched 15.3-31.4%   burnt 48.2-82.6%

  This is structural, not seed-specific. A cell only becomes burnt after 21-30
  steps of burning, and probability_of_fire spreads over a Moore radius-3 disc,
  so every 4-neighbour of a long-burning cell has itself ignited well before
  that cell burns out. The outer boundary of the scar against virgin ground is
  therefore ALWAYS a band of scorched or burning cells.

  CONCLUSION: the scorched band is the mandatory boundary layer of the burn
  scar. A penalty on scorched ground is a toll on the only route into - and out
  of - the safest ground in the model. That is the mechanism behind the -632
  burnt firefighter-steps in section 4.3, and it is why the harm does not go
  away by tuning the magnitude down.

6. DECISION
================================================================================

Against the brief acceptance list, this round lands on the fourth outcome:
BOTH (ALL THREE) ARMS MAKE THINGS WORSE.

  GATE ITEM                                        RESULT
  firefighter_deaths decreases                     FAILS  6 -> 8 / 14 -> 17
  victims dead does not increase                   passes 23 -> 22 / 37 -> 36
  ff-steps on unburnt green does not rise          FAILS  +19.8 pp on perturbed
                                                          runs (+307 steps)

RECOMMENDATION: ship nothing. Close A1 as "diagnosed, fix attempted, measured
harmful". Specifically:

  * The DIAGNOSIS in outputs/burntcells_part1.txt is upheld as a description of
    the code. _firefighter_cell_risk really does score scorched ground
    identically to virgin ground, the signal really is present on the agent and
    never read, and 3 of the 6 baseline deaths really are on scorched ground.
  * The REMEDY is refuted. `has_burned` adds no hazard information the existing
    fire-adjacency term does not already carry (5.1), and the population it
    selects is the gateway to the safest ground in the model (5.2), so
    penalising it is net harmful at every magnitude tested.
  * The victim-side gain (+1 rescued, -1 victim dead on both samples) is real
    but small and comes essentially from one run (east_half_505, r1->r2), where
    it was bought with a firefighter death. It is not a reason to ship.
  * The outcome delta is small-sample: +3 deaths over 23 runs, from 5 runs whose
