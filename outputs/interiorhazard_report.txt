1.1  common_fixed_variables.py (+22 lines, all but one comment), after the feature-2 constants:

        VICTIM_SEARCHER_HAZARD_RETREAT_RANGE = 99

     with the comment block giving the three meanings (0 = the retreat fires only within 2 cells
     of a grid edge and is scored toward the interior; 1..98 = it fires when the nearest strict
     fire/smoke cell is within that many cells; >= 99 = it fires on every gated step), the
     scoring rule when on (hazard distance only; the edge is left to the edge-blocked filter and
     the planner margins, which are live regardless), and the history (the rule ran always-on and
     hazard-only for the whole record because the grid size was unreadable; 99 is that behaviour
     made intentional, 0 is what the edge-only code did once the read worked, measured at +1.1
     points of searcher steps on a burning cell). Overridable per run through
     apply_scenario_config; deterministic.

1.2  src_extension/execution/uav_executor.py (+63 / -5 lines):
     - `import common_fixed_variables as _cfv`: the module handle, so the constant is read at
       CALL time. The file's existing `from common_fixed_variables import ...` names are
       import-time copies that a per-run override cannot reach (the W3 pattern); the new read
       follows agents.py's victim_flee_trigger_distance() instead.
     - `_hazard_retreat_range()` (new, before _victim_edge_blocked_direction): the call-time read,
       clamped to >= 0, 0 on a bad value.
     - `_apply_victim_searcher_hazard_gate`, item (4) of the gate: the `D(pos) <= 2.0` block
       becomes

            retreat_range = self._hazard_retreat_range()
            if pos is not None and model is not None:
                cell = (int(pos[0]), int(pos[1]))
                if retreat_range > 0:
                    retreat_now = (retreat_range >= 99
                                   or self._min_strict_hazard_distance(cell) <= float(retreat_range))
                else:
                    retreat_now = self._distance_from_boundary(cell[0], cell[1], model) <= 2.0
                if retreat_now:
                    retreat = self._retreat_to_safe_interior_direction(agent)
                    ... (the relabel rule and return, unchanged)

       plus a docstring naming the gate's order (strict hazard on the current cell -> retreat;
       edge-blocked direction -> retreat; this rule; the 3-cell lookahead with re-ranking) and a
       comment giving the history. Item (6), the fallback ranking's `D(cell) * 10.0` term, is
       0.0 unless the range is 0.
     - `_retreat_to_safe_interior_direction`: `edge_scored = self._hazard_retreat_range() == 0`;
       the 14*D term, the +18 inward bonus and the fallback 10*D term apply only when
       edge_scored; the 8*hazard and 6*hazard terms always. With the range on, the function is
       the accident's retreat exactly: the strictly safe neighbour furthest from any fire or
       smoke, ties by direction index.
     - Nothing else touched: C5 (_victim_near_edge_escape_required), C3 (the hold-branch
       near_boundary read), the tracker-side helpers, the twice-per-step gate call, and the
       dimension fix itself are as e76c806 left them.

7. RANGE 6 vs RANGE 99 - IS THE MECHANISM LOCAL?
================================================================================================
The maintainer's test, stated before the runs: if 6 recovers most of 99's benefit the mechanism
is genuinely local; if it does not, 99 is preserving an accident deliberately.
  IT DOES NOT.
  - Exposure: range 6 recovers 29% of the fix -> 99 reduction on the canonical 13 and 69% on the
    fresh 10. Pooled over 10,320 searcher steps: fix 356 burning-cell steps, range 6 320, range
    99 267, 8520706 260 - range 6 recovers 40%.
  - Outcomes: range 6's rescued / dead / firefighter counts are e76c806's on BOTH samples
    (37/11/8 canonical, 27/8/8 fresh), including the fresh 3 -> 8 deaths the held round flagged;
    range 99's are 8520706's on the canonical 13 (13/13 seeds) and on 8/10 fresh seeds.
  - Mechanism: the retreats that range 6 adds fire only near fire, and they change trajectories
    on 11 of 13 canonical seeds without changing a count; the retreats range 99 adds beyond 6
    cells - 61% of the accidental override's interior firings had the fire nine or more cells
    away (Part 1, 2.2) - are what puts the searchers on 8520706's paths and keeps them out of the
    fire's 3-cell neighbourhood. The benefit is the far-field, target-blind bias away from the
    fire, i.e. an open-space bias, not hazard avoidance at the moment of danger.
  So the default 99 is a deliberate choice to preserve an accident, made knowingly: it is the
  behaviour every outcome in the campaign's record was produced under, it is now named,
  documented, tested and switchable, and range 6 is the measured alternative that gives back the
  fix's regression on victims (dead 8 on fresh) for a partial exposure recovery. Range 6 is not
  recommended; it is on record.
