1.1  THE TIER STRUCTURE
------------------------
  Cells holding ACTIVE FIRE are dropped at 1107-1108, before any tiering.
  Of what survives:
    tier 1  improving (dist_after < dist_before), not adjacent_fire, not smoke
    tier 2  maintaining (dist_after == dist_before), not adjacent_fire, not smoke
    tier 3  anything not adjacent_fire and not smoke  (so: worsening but clean)
    tier 4  fallback over ALL non-fire neighbours, min by (risk, dist, !preferred)
  Within tiers 1-3 the pick is min by (dist_after, 0 if preferred else 1).
  `preferred` is the greedy single-axis step, x first when |dx| >= |dy|.

1.2  THE CLAIM "NO ANTI-OSCILLATION TERM" - CONFIRMED, EXHAUSTIVELY
--------------------------------------------------------------------
  CONFIRMED.  The chosen cell is a deterministic, memoryless function of
      (self.pos, target, the burning-fire cell set, the active-smoke cell set,
       grid bounds)
  and nothing else.  Established by enumerating every `self.` reference in
  1081-1176 and taking the transitive closure of its callees - which is exactly
  `_path_exists_avoiding_fire` (1030), `_fire_cells` (617), `_neighbor_cells`
  (675), `_cell_contains_active_fire` (629), `_cell_adjacent_to_fire` (658),
  `_cell_has_active_smoke` (641), `_firefighter_cell_risk` (665),
  `_mark_route_blocked` (1067), plus three `model.grid` calls.  None of them
  consults where the unit has previously been.  There is no `random` call, so
  the choice is also RNG-free, and `min` is order-stable over `scored`, built in
  the fixed `_neighbor_cells` order (+1,0),(-1,0),(0,+1),(0,-1).

  Fields READ but which provably cannot damp the choice:
    `exiting`     (1100) gates only the reachability BFS.  Its product,
                  `route_blocked_now`, appears at 1127 and 1172 only - never in
                  a tier pool or a min-key.
    `status`      read at 1068 and 1173.  Both reads happen after `scored` is
                  frozen (1105-1121) and `chosen` derives solely from `scored`.
    `assigned`    read at 1175, after `chosen` is bound.  Selects a status
                  string, nothing else.
  Fields WRITTEN and never read back for movement:
    `_last_move_risk` is WRITE-ONLY in production; its only reader anywhere is
    a diagnostic script.  `_last_move_tier` is read only into telemetry strings
    consumed by the XAI explanation engine.

  ONE CORRECTION TO THE PRECISE PHRASING.  "`_move_toward` is memoryless" is
  true of the CHOSEN CELL but not of the METHOD: `_mark_route_blocked` (1068)
  reads `self.status` across steps to de-duplicate the handler call, and that

  memoryless overstates it.  Measurement below shows the point is moot for the

5. THE MECHANISM
================================================================================
5.1  WHY A 2-CYCLE IS POSSIBLE AT ALL
---------------------------------------
  On a 4-connected grid with Manhattan distance, a neighbour's distance to the
  target is ALWAYS dist_before +/- 1.  So:

    tier1 -> tier1 is IMPOSSIBLE.  A->B improving means d(B) = d(A)-1; B->A
    improving would need d(A) < d(A)-1.

  The reachable cycle is tier1 <-> tier3, and it has one shape:

    * The unit stands on a clean cell F.  The step toward the target, N, is
      clean and improving, so TIER 1 takes it: F -> N.
    * From N, every improving neighbour is adjacent to fire or smoky.  Tier 1
      and tier 2 are empty.  TIER 3 takes the best CLEAN cell, which necessarily
      WORSENS distance - and F is it: N -> F.
    * From F, N is clean and improving again.  Tier 1 takes it.  Forever.

  Both cells are clean.  The unit is safe and stationary, pressed against a
  hazard barrier it will not cross, on a board where nothing has changed.

5.2  DEMONSTRATED, NOT ARGUED  (outputs/_mto_cycle_demo.py)
-------------------------------------------------------------
  The REAL `agents.Firefighter._move_toward` and the real tier chain, risk
  function and BFS were bound to a stub whose ONLY overrides are the three
  grid-content predicates, reading from explicit frozen sets.

    case 1  fire wall at x=4, target (2,5), start (7,5)
            -> path (7,5),(6,5),(7,5),(6,5),... tiers 1,3,1,3,...
               STABLE LIMIT CYCLE, period 2, 2 distinct cells in 24 steps.
    case 2  the same shape made of SMOKE, no fire anywhere
            -> identical period-2 cycle.  The mechanism is not fire-specific.
    case 3  control, no barrier -> walks straight in on tier 1.
    case 4  wall sealed to the grid edges -> route_blocked raised on all 24
            steps AND the unit still cycles.  Raising the signal does not stop
            the dithering; the code comment at 1128-1129 says so explicitly.

  So on a frozen board the cycle is genuinely infinite.  The board is not frozen
  - section 6.

5.3  THE DEEPER READING
------------------------
  This is a greedy-descent local minimum, and the reversal is its symptom.
  `_move_toward` picks a step by one-step lookahead with a hazard filter; it has
  no global path awareness at all.  At a barrier, one-step lookahead has nothing
  to descend to, so it alternates.

  An anti-oscillation memory does not address that.  It converts a stationary
  ping-pong into a wander - the unit would still not path AROUND the barrier,
  because nothing in the method knows the barrier has an end.  This is the
  central reason the "adding a term" framing does not lead anywhere good: the
  term would suppress the SIGNATURE while leaving the situation unchanged, and
  would cost the unit its safe holding cell to do it (section 7).

6. "NO MECHANISM TO REPAIR" IS FALSE
================================================================================
6.1  MEASURED: WHAT ACTUALLY ENDED EACH EPISODE
-------------------------------------------------
  The 144 reversal-steps form 62 maximal consecutive-reversal EPISODES.
  Determinism means an episode continues until an input changes, so "what ended
  it" is the right unit of analysis, and it is read from the trace:

        31  ( 50.0%)  BOARD     the fire/smoke picture around the pair changed
        31  ( 50.0%)  SURVIVAL  `_survival_move` took over as hazard closed in
         0             HORIZON   still cycling at step 240

  62 OF 62 EPISODES WERE REPAIRED.  Not one survived to the end of the run.
  Episode lengths: 35 of length 1, 13 of 2, and a tail to a single longest of
  14 steps.  Every episode is a 2-cell pair, confirming period 2.

6.2  WHY THE WORLD ALWAYS REPAIRS IT
--------------------------------------
  Per-cell hazard is one-shot and absorbing-to-clean: a cell burns at most once,
  smokes at most once, and is permanently fire-free and smoke-free afterwards.
  The hazard configuration that produces a given 2-cycle therefore cannot
  persist.  Fire spread re-evaluates on ticks where the counter % 3 == 0; the
  smoke mask can change on any tick.  All Fire.advance() calls run BEFORE
  Firefighter.advance() within a tick, so a unit sees the current board.

  The honest statement is: there is no repair mechanism INSIDE `_move_toward`,
  but the hazard field's own evolution is an unconditional one outside it, and
  `_survival_move` is a second.  Those two account for 62 of 62.

6.3  WHY route_blocked NEVER FIRES FOR THIS DITHER - AND IT IS STRUCTURAL
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
  `_path_exists_avoiding_fire` treats ONLY ACTIVE FIRE as impassable.
  Fire-adjacency and smoke are invisible to it.  But the tier filter refuses
  cells for exactly those two reasons.  So the BFS reports the route OPEN while
  every forward step is being refused.

  Measured over all 141 tier-3 legs, the improving neighbours that were rejected,
  by the flag that rejected them:

        144  ( 67.3%)  adjacent to fire      <- invisible to the BFS
         43  ( 20.1%)  smoky                 <- invisible to the BFS
         27  ( 12.6%)  adjacent to fire AND smoky

        214 of 214 (100.0%) rejected by a flag the BFS cannot see.
        ZERO rejected by active fire itself.

  This is why 4.7 found route_blocked open at 144 of 144.  The structural
  prediction and the measurement agree exactly, from opposite directions.

6.4  THE ONE TIMEOUT THAT DOES NOT CATCH IT
---------------------------------------------
  The model's only no-progress timeout, `_update_unreachable_victims`
  (UNREACHABLE_STREAK_STEPS = 30), tests whether distance decreased since the
  previous step.  In a 2-cycle distance decreases on every other step, so the
  streak resets continuously and the timeout never trips.  Worth recording; it
  did not matter here, because 62 of 62 episodes ended long before 30 steps.

8.2  THE BFS ASYMMETRY (section 6.3) IS ITSELF WORTH A NOTE
-------------------------------------------------------------
  `route_blocked` asks a question about FIRE while the movement filter answers a
  question about FIRE-ADJACENCY-AND-SMOKE.  The two disagree by construction,
  and 214 of 214 measured rejections fall in the gap.  This is not a defect in
  `_move_toward` - the signal is doing what its comment says - but it does mean
  the route_blocked replacement pathway can never be triggered by the dominant
  stall mode.  Out of scope for this round; recorded for whoever looks at
  route_blocked next.


================================================================================
9. VERDICT
================================================================================
  (B) REAL BUT NOT A DEFECT.  Leave documented; change nothing.

  What is true, and confirmed:
    * `_move_toward` has no anti-oscillation term.  Provable, exhaustive, and
      it never had one on any ref.
    * 144 of 186 tight reversals originate there.  Reproduces exactly, seed for
      seed, at this HEAD.
    * On a frozen board the cycle is genuinely infinite.  Demonstrated with the
      real code.

  What is false:
    * "There is no mechanism to repair."  62 of 62 episodes were repaired, by
      board evolution (31) and `_survival_move` (31).  None reached the horizon.

  Why it is not a defect:
    * The step back is the code's own top-ranked pick in 144 of 144.
    * The unit is on clean ground at both ends in 141 of 144, and in the 3
      exceptions it is fleeing danger correctly.
    * A guard is never better (0 of 144), equal at best (69), worse at worst
      (75) - and its worst case is taking away a unit's only clean cell, which
      is the failure that killed units in the last_cell round.
    * The cost is 144 reversal-steps out of 5316 firefighter-steps alive: 2.71%.
      Median episode 1 step; longest 14.
    * The reversal is the SYMPTOM of greedy descent at a barrier, not the cause.
      An anti-oscillation term would hide the signature without changing the
      situation, at the price of the unit's safe holding cell.

  This is now the fourth consecutive round to find that constraining firefighter
  movement more tightly makes outcomes worse or no better.  On the evidence here
  that is not a coincidence: tiers 1-3 are already a hazard-avoidance filter,
  and every additional constraint composes with it by removing the last clean
  option rather than by improving the choice.

  IF ANYONE DOES REVISIT THIS, the lead is not an anti-oscillation term.  It is
  that `_move_toward` already computes a full fire-avoiding BFS every transit
  step (1101) and throws away everything except one boolean.  Taking the first
  step of that BFS instead of a greedy axis step would dissolve the local
  minimum outright rather than mask it.  That is a materially larger behavioural
  change than this round is scoped for, it would need its own harm measurement,
  and the BFS's fire-only notion of passability (8.2) would have to be
  reconciled with the tier filter's first.  Noted as a direction, not proposed.
