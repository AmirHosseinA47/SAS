VERDICT, BY THE MAINTAINER'S OWN RULE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"If it is rare, the behavioural effect is near-zero ... If it is common, this is a
real change to fire spread and must be gated as one."

IT IS NOT MERELY COMMON - EVERY FIRE THAT SPREADS AT ALL REACHES A DEPOT. In the
existing corpus a depot cell burns in 25 of 27 runs (22 of 24 DISTINCT fires), and
the two exceptions are not fires that missed: in both, the fire died on its first
tick and exactly ONE cell ever burned. In 19 of 27 runs the first depot cell ignites
while rescue outcomes can still move (17 strictly before a recorded terminal step,
2 in runs with no terminal step at all); in 6 it ignites after.

So this is a fire-spread change. It needs ITS OWN GATED ROUND, on its own branch
and commit. The display items (banner, table cell) stay a separate, display-only
commit with their own value-identity proof against d3640e3, exactly as the
maintainer asked: "keep the display items separate rather than bundling a
fire-spread change into a rendering commit."

This document is that round's Part 1. Nothing is implemented. Decisions: section 6.

2. THE TWO EFFECTS, KEPT APART
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1 BOOKKEEPING - attributable arithmetic, and it is TWO numbers, not one
   50 cells (2.0% of the 2,500-cell grid) can no longer burn. The harness records
   each cell's FINAL state, so the split is recoverable today, per run:

      depot cells that EVER burn          682 over 27 runs   mean 25.3 (0-47)
      ... of which at step 240: BURNT     432                mean 16.0 (0-24)
                                SCORCHED  214                mean  7.9
                                BURNING    36                mean  1.3
      (sum(burnt) over all 2,500 cells equals eval.burnt_cells in 27 of 27 runs)

   So BY CONSTRUCTION, in a run where fire reaches a depot:
      untouched vegetation rises by that run's EVER-burned count   (mean +25.3)
      burnt_cells falls by that run's BURNT-at-240 count           (mean -16.0)
   - two different numbers. Both are listed per run in outputs/_dfp_corpus2.txt
   (e.g. east_def_101: 25 ever, 20 burnt), so each can be subtracted exactly.
   One caution: a fuel-less never-burned cell is counted as UNTOUCHED VEGETATION
   by the ground counts (has_burned 0, burnt 0, burning 0), although it has no
   fuel. "Intact" rises without a single tree being saved.
   This arithmetic is exact only for the DEPOT-CELL component. The rest of the
   grid is 2.2.

2.2 BEHAVIOURAL - and exactly where it starts
   The mechanic the maintainer named already exists: Fire.firefighter_remove_fuel
   (agents.py:154-164) sets fuel = 0 on an ignitable cell and nothing else. Read
   with Fire.step (agents.py:89-115) it has a property that makes ATTRIBUTION
   possible rather than merely correlation:
     - it draws NO random number, and Fire.__init__ has already drawn the cell's
       fuel (agents.py:23) before it runs - so clearing at init shifts nothing;
     - a fuel-less cell KEEPS DRAWING its number on every fire tick
       (agents.py:103; probability_of_fire returns 0 at :84-85, so it never
       ignites). A BURNT cell, by contrast, returns early WITHOUT drawing (:91-95);
     - a neighbour's ignition probability reads only is_burning() of adjacent
       cells (agents.py:72), never their fuel.
   THEREFORE the real arm's BURNING and BURNT state equals the control's exactly,
   cell for cell, until the first tick on which a depot cell WOULD have ignited.
   The corpus gives that tick per run. A verifier searched every reader of .fuel /
   get_fuel / get_prob / cell_prob in agents.py, wildfire_model.py, src_extension
   and the top-level modules and found no counter-path: the Smoke object captures
   fuel at construction, before any init-time clear; wildfire_model.py and
   src_extension never read fuel; the rest is rendering. THREE CONDITIONS, which
   hold for every planned arm but must be said:
     (a) FIXED_WIND must be True (common_fixed_variables.py:10). With it False,
         Wind.apply_wind draws once per (fuelled cell, burning neighbour) inside
         probability_of_fire's fuel > 0 branch; a fuel-less depot cell would skip
         those draws and shift the shared stream BEFORE any depot ignition. No
         runner overrides FIXED_WIND.
     (b) the fire mechanic must be off: agents.py:2417 reads fuel to build its
         plan, so with FF_FIREFIGHT_* armed the depot cells drop out of planning
         from step 0.
     (c) "cell for cell" is about burning / burnt. Depot FUEL differs from step 0,
         and depot cell_prob from first exposure - which matters for the
         instrument (section 4).
   After that tick two things separate the arms: (1) PHYSICAL - neighbours of the
   depot lose igniting neighbours. Spread uses a Moore radius-3 neighbourhood cut
   at EUCLIDEAN distance 3 (distance_rate, common_fixed_variables.py:633-638), so
   34 cells around a corner block (39 within Moore 3, 5 of them beyond Euclidean 3
   of every depot cell) see a lower ignition probability while the depot would
   have been burning. A corner block shields nothing BEHIND it - behind it is the
   map edge - so this is a local dent, not a firebreak across the fire's path.
   (2) STREAM OFFSET - from the first burn-OUT that differs between the arms (at
   the latest the first depot burn-out; possibly an earlier neighbour that only
   ignited because of a burning depot cell) one arm has a cell that has stopped
   drawing and the other does not. From there the two arms consume the shared RNG
   stream at different rates and everything downstream is re-rolled.
   That second effect is the one fire-mechanic round 2 measured: a pure
   draw-stream offset with NO write moves final intact by a median of 155 cells
   (signed sd 276) with no systematic sign. So per-run signs on rescued / dead
   after the divergence step are NOISE, and nothing below may be read run by run.

3. DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1 WHERE. wildfire_model.reset(): set_fire_agents() runs at :182, the station is
    built at :191. Immediately after :191, for every cell of every depot, call the
    EXISTING firefighter_remove_fuel() on that cell's Fire agent. No new write
    path. Its precondition (not burning, not burnt, fuel > 0) holds for all 50
    cells at init: the ignition cell is drawn with margin 10 from every edge
    (set_fire_agents, :2275-2277) and a depot lies within 5 of a corner.
    Both depots. A cell with no Fire agent (DENSITY_PROB < 1) is skipped.
3.2 KILL SWITCH. BASE_STATION_FIREPROOF in common_fixed_variables.py, shipped 1,
    read through an accessor in agents.py at call time like every other
    base-station setting. 0 = today's behaviour: the loop is not entered, nothing
    is written, and the run must equal d3640e3 on every recorded value except
    tag / repo / wall_s and the arm's own params - the identity rule round 3 used.
    (The repo's recorded hazard applies: base-station accessors fall back to the
    SHIPPED value, so a junk override arms the feature. Say so in the comment, as
    the flip round did.)
3.3 DRY ARM. BASE_STATION_FIREPROOF_DRY_RUN = 1: the depot cells are identified and
    logged but firefighter_remove_fuel is NOT called. Nothing about the fire
    changes, so every recorded fire value - digests included - must equal the
    control's.
3.4 A DISPLAY CONSEQUENCE THAT TOUCHES THE BASE-MARK BANNER. A never-burned cell
    with fuel 0 renders VEGETATION_COLORS[0] = #414141 on both surfaces
    (normalize_fuel_values(0) = 0) - the same hex as FIRE_COLORS[0], with no legend
    entry. The whole depot floor would be a permanent dark grey, #4d3354 under the
    0.22 tint. For the banner: the white keyline stays at 10.95:1; the #770099
    field would sit on its weakest MAP-MODE ground, 17.22 dE (16.80 in probability
    mode). The honest comparison is not with tinted green but with what a depot
    floor already is for much of a run - tinted burnt, 18.68 dE, 18.3% of SE
    cell-steps today: 18.68 -> 17.22, made permanent. The mark still passes on its
    black/white pair. Decision 6.3.
3.5 A HARNESS NOTE FOR THAT ROUND. outputs/_ffr_harness.py:810 assumes "fuel
    exhausted on a cell that never burned: only a firebreak produces it", and
    fire_cleared_unburned_final counts such cells. With this shipped ON both are
    off by up to 50 whenever the fire mechanic is armed.

THE ATTRIBUTION INSTRUMENT - corrected. The harness's per-step fire digest hashes
FUEL as well as burning and burnt (outputs/_ffr_harness.py:482). dfpON changes the
fuel of 50 cells at init, so its digest differs from the control's at the FIRST
recorded step of EVERY run. A prediction registered on that digest - which is what
my first draft did - fails 23 of 23 by construction and proves nothing. The
attribution must be registered on a fuel-blind observable: the per-step BURNING
sets, reconstructible offline from burn_intervals, which the harness already
records per cell. (The dfpDRY == dfpOFF digest identity is unaffected: DRY changes
no fuel.)

PRE-REGISTERED, on the 23:
   - ATTRIBUTION: in each of the 22 runs with a depot ignition, dfpON's per-step
     burning set first differs from dfpOFF's AT that run's first depot ignition
     step in the control (listed per run in outputs/_dfp_corpus2.txt; the earliest
     is 54, south_half_1010). Before that step the two are identical.
   - east_half_707, where the fire dies on tick 1: dfpON equals dfpOFF on every
     recorded value EXCEPT fire_digests / fire_final_digest (fuel is hashed) and the
     arm's params. It is a VACUOUS null - no burning cell ever comes near a depot -
     and is recorded as such, not as evidence.
   - BOOKKEEPING, depot-cell component only: in dfpON zero depot cells ever burn;
     the control's per-run ever-burned and burnt-at-240 counts are the exact shifts.
     NO one-signed inequality is registered on the whole grid: the guaranteed term
     is ~25 cells and the re-roll noise is ~155 with no sign.
   - the 6 runs whose first depot ignition is after terminal_step cannot change
     rescued, dead or never_detected, by construction. (Supported on the corpus:
     across the 25 runs with a terminal step, no victim row changes after it.)
Reported per the brief: rescued, dead, ff_deaths, never_detected, terminal_step,
burnt_cells, untouched vegetation, and drone steps in fire - E1 searcher-only and
E2 all-UAV, interior-hazard definition (control 3.55% / 2.42% on the 18 gate seeds;
4.21% / 2.83% on the 23).

THE GATE, as the maintainer wrote it, with two readings made explicit:
   - drone steps in fire FALLS
   - rescued does not decrease - judged POOLED and against the dfpDRY decomposition,
     not run by run (2.2). If pooled rescued falls, the pinned-draw fire replay from
     fire-mechanic round 2 (outputs/_fm2_fire_replay.py) separates a physical loss
     from a re-roll.
   - the burnt_cells and vegetation shifts fully accounted for as bookkeeping, any

   - no NEW route_blocked failures beyond the four known. CAUTION: east/707,
