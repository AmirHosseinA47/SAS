0. THE HEADLINE
================================================================================
  THE GATE PASSES ON EVERY ITEM, AND EVERY PRE-REGISTERED STOCK NUMBER CAME OUT EXACTLY.
  But the policy that achieves it is NARROWER THAN WHAT WAS ASKED FOR.

  THE TRADE, first, because it is the result:
    The mission gate keeps about TWO THIRDS of the stock-model fire effect
    (+1,908 of ungated extinguish+S's +2,867 intact over 23 runs) and only about a
    SIXTH of the PHYSICAL effect (+239 of +1,379 under common random numbers, the
    instrument section 3 argues is the one that describes the mechanic). The +1,908
    and both CRN figures differ in kind: +1,908 is measured in tree this round, while
    +239 is a PROJECTION of the round-2 probe arm (measured +248, with its one
    strict-closed run, south/half/1010 at +9, replaced by its control). Round 3 ran no
    CRN arm.
    It buys that by giving up everything firefighting does before the rescue
    mission is decided.
    Against the supervisor's wording - "by the time drones are looking for the
    victims, the firefighter must fight with the fire" - THIS IS NOT THAT. These
    firefighters fight fire almost entirely AFTER the rescue mission is over:
    every engaged step in all 23 runs falls after terminal_step, by construction.
    That is a limitation of what the evidence permits, not a design preference.
    The alternative - firefighting while the drones search, which is what was
    asked for - is measured and costs rescues: -2 of 68 in the stock realisation,
    and, under common random numbers where no re-roll is possible, -6 of 72 from
    POSITIONING alone. NO ARBITRATION RULE TESTED PREVENTS THAT BY CONSTRUCTION
    (section 2).

  WHAT SHIPS (OFF by default, as round 1 did)

      True when every managed victim whose state is present has status "rescued" or

2. THE ARBITRATION QUESTION: WHAT WAS TESTED AND REFUSED
================================================================================
  These are MEASURED REFUSALS, not untried options. All dry (fire identical to the
  control 240/240 on every run), 23 runs, rescued against fmOFF's 68:

  (a) COMMITMENT LIMIT - REFUSED. A leash of N cells from where a unit went idle.
        leash 3 -> 67 rescued, ff deaths 12 (+2)      leash 8 -> 69 rescued
      Leash 3 still differs from the control on 5 of the 9 items round 1 changed AND
      ADDS a loss of its own (east/half/303 victim_1), while leash 8 adds a different
      one (east/half/909 victim_0 dies). There is no dose-response: the tighter leash
      is the worse arm. A 3-cell head start is already enough to re-time the chain.
  (b) RESERVE - REFUSED. Keep one unit un-engaged.
        only unit 0 engages -> 68      only unit 1 engages -> 68
      Both hit the control's total by cancellation, not by construction: unit-0-only
      is +1 canonical and -1 fresh, so it would fail "on either sample" by luck alone,
      with the fire untouched. Both keep round 1's south/909 loss; unit-1-only also
      keeps south/404. With two units it also halves the work available.
  (c) DISTANCE-AWARE CHOOSER - REFUSED, a false lead. There is nothing for it to
      minimise: the dry arm's first dispatches are 5.4 cells CLOSER on average than the
      control's, not farther (n=81 first ok assigns; -7.8 cells on the 58 that land on
      an engaged unit), and assign-to-contact is 5.9 steps shorter. At the first dispatch - where
      the losses are decided - the two units stand 1 cell apart on 21 of 23 runs and the
      other unit is exactly 1 cell farther on 22 of 23. The dispatcher already picks the
      nearest.
  (d) THE TIME LIMIT - ADOPTED. A commitment limit taken to its limit in time. It is
      the only rule measured to leave every victim's outcome identical to the control:
      161 of 161 gated probe runs in Part 1, and now 23 of 23 in-tree runs plus all 18
      route_blocked seeds.

  WHY NO SPATIAL RULE CAN WORK. The loss is not a unit being far away when the call
  comes. It is the re-timing of the whole rescue chain, which begins in the first
  14-16 steps when both idle units walk toward the fire before any victim is even
  confirmed. Every perturbation of that walk re-rolls which victims live, in both
  directions, and the size of the perturbation barely matters.

3. THE CORRECTION TO ROUND 1: THE TWO LOSS MECHANISMS ARE ONE
================================================================================
  ROUND 1 REPORTED TWO SEPARATE FINDINGS. Both reduce to the same cause: DISPLACEMENT
  OF THE FIRE'S SINGLE SHARED RANDOM-NUMBER STREAM, not fire behaviour.

  (i) Round 1's DE-DUPLICATED "-1 positioning, -1 writes" split of the rescued loss
      (counted over 23 runs the same arm is -2 positioning, +0 writes). The write-caused half
      is south/half/404. Traced: ten firebreak clears at steps 9-16 delay the burn-out
      of ONE cell, (8,35) - 42 cells from victim_0, whose survival decides the loss.
      That delay leaves one extra cell drawing at tick 84, so 772 probability-bearing
      cells - every one above the displaced cell in draw order - read a different
      random number from that tick, and the rest of the fire is a new realisation. Victim_0 then lives 48 steps longer,
      holds the last surviving unit, and victim_2 is never reached.
      Closed-loop bisection confirms it is the re-roll and not any particular write:
      the prefix with no writes reproduces the dry arm exactly, and all four re-rolled
      prefixes that were run (s = 12, 13, 14, 16 - four distinct fires, three distinct
      behaviours, since s=12 and s=13 agree on every agent field) lose victim_2.
  (ii) "Extinguish is erratic" (a per-run coin flip). Same mechanism: an extinguished
      cell stops drawing two ticks after the write, and the fire differs outside the
      physical light cone from exactly that tick on 17 of 18 runs. With common random
      numbers - each cell's draw a function of (seed, cell, tick), so a write cannot
      displace anyone else's number - extinguish is ONE-SIGNED: 16 up / 0 down / 7
      unchanged counted (the pre-registered rule's own de-duplicated output is 13 up /
      0 down / 7 unchanged, sign-test p 0.0001), by a rule frozen in code before the
      data existed.

  HOW BIG IS THE DISPLACEMENT? Measured directly, with no write at all: shifting the
  stream by a single draw at one tick moves final untouched vegetation by a median 155
  cells (signed sd 276) and changes the burned set by a median 284.5 cells. It has NO
  systematic direction - the paired +k / -k contrasts give means -76 to +116 with
  p 0.047-0.91, and Part 1 flags the two nominally significant results (that T=60
  contrast, and a T=90 wind interaction at p 0.002) as unexplained rather than
  interpreted - so it is not a bias to correct for, it is noise that swamps per-run
  signs. Round 1's per-run "consistency" counts are therefore weaker evidence than
  they looked.

  WHICH INSTRUMENT DESCRIBES THE MECHANIC: THE CRN ONE. The stock model answers "what
  happened in this realisation, including the re-roll the write triggered"; common
  random numbers answer "what the write did to the fire". For a mechanic's physical
  effect the second is the question. That is why the headline quotes both, and why the
  gated configuration was chosen on agreement between them.

  WHAT THIS DOES *NOT* CORRECT. Firebreak's ungated stock gain is NOT an artifact:
  replayed on the same fire with pinned draws, the physics alone account for +1,209 of
  its +1,783 over the 17 de-duplicated writing tuples, and the residual is
  statistically indistinguishable from the pure re-roll noise (p 0.89). Under the
  closed-loop CRN arm, firebreak is instead about zero on the comparable de-duplicated
  basis: -148 (7 up / 9 down / 4 unchanged). THE TWO VALIDATED INSTRUMENTS DISAGREE ON FIREBREAK'S
  PHYSICAL SIZE, they measure different counterfactuals, and this round does not pick
  one. It did not have to: firebreak fails the both-samples test under the gate in
  BOTH instruments, which is why the shipped configuration is extinguish only.

5. OPEN ITEMS, STATED AS OPEN
================================================================================
  - PINNED REPLAY vs CRN DISAGREE on firebreak's physical size (+1,209 of +1,783 over
    17 tuples versus about zero). Both instruments are validated; they measure
    different counterfactuals. UNRESOLVED, and deliberately not decided here.
  - THE COMPLETION THRESHOLD IS NOT MEASURED. Under the gate it is moot by
    construction: no dispatch can occur once every victim is resolved, so nothing can
    preempt. Ungated, the archived estimate of finishing an abandoned band is 7-12
    cells against a certain dispatch delay of ~8.5 steps on a fifth of dispatches. A
    verifier's larger estimate was never archived and IS NOT RELIED ON. If the
    threshold ever matters, it needs its own measurement.
  - WHAT THE GATE GIVES UP is measured, not assumed: 324 of the ungated arm's 490
    extinguishes survive it, but the physical effect falls from +1,379 to about +239
    under CRN (the probe arm's +248, projected onto the strict predicate), because the
    early fire is where the leverage is.
  - UNMEASURED: west and north winds, runs beyond 240 steps, other scenarios, and more
    than two firefighters. Scenario D has 2 units; with more, both the fire effect and
    the re-timing cost would change.
  - THE FRESH GAIN RESTS ON SIX RUNS. Of the fresh 10, four never open the gate; of
    the canonical 13, three never do.

6. WHAT THE MAINTAINER DECIDES NEXT
================================================================================
  The feature is shippable in the sense the round set out: the gate passes, and the
  mechanic works. Two honest framings of the same result:
    - As a rescue-safety property, this is as good as it gets: rescue outcomes are
      untouched BY CONSTRUCTION, not by luck, and that is now proven in-tree on 23
      runs plus 18 gate seeds.
    - As firefighting, it is a fraction of the ask. The units fight fire only after
      the mission is decided, and the physical effect of that is about a sixth of what
      the same configuration does when it is allowed to fight from the start.
  IF FIGHTING DURING THE SEARCH IS THE REQUIREMENT, this round's evidence says the
  price is rescues moving, and by two different channels:
    - in the stock realisation, -2 of 68 (round 1), of which the write-caused half is
      the re-roll traced in section 3;
    - under common random numbers, where no re-roll is possible, -6 of 72 from
      POSITIONING alone (the dry arm). In that same instrument the configuration
      proposed here costs 0 rescues ungated (extinguish+S) and the full round-1
      configuration -3.
  No arbitration rule tested prevents either channel BY CONSTRUCTION: leash 8 and both
  reserve arms happen to match or beat the control's pooled total, but each still flips
  individual victims in both directions, and leash 3 is a net -1. That is a product
  decision about which risk to carry, not a further engineering question.
  The next round's flip, if taken, is EXTINGUISH=1 and ENGAGED_RETREAT_RANGE=1; the
  gate is already the default.
