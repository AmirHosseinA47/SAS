THE RULING (maintainer, after the Part 3 STOP): options (a) and (b) together, in one follow-up on fix3a,
on the same sample. GATES UNCHANGED - (N) and (O) are not relaxed after the fact.
  (a) ONE OWNER FOR NEAR-FIRE STEERING. When the searcher hazard gate would veto a step - including
      ordinary search and sweep steps, not only the route - the step goes to the latched route instead
      of the memoryless retreat. No second, memoryless steering path may remain for searchers near fire.
  (b) A BOUNDED WAIT. A latched route with no path gives up its target after a fixed number of steps.
      The bound is fixed here, before any run, with its reasoning; one alternative bound is reported as
      a sensitivity result, not as a second chance.

HOW THE STEERING WAS MAPPED (read-only; outputs/fix3a_r2_map_*.txt keep the four maps)
  A read-only workflow of 4 mapping agents (lenses: steering paths, target sources, label consumers,
  post-route guards), then my own reading. These are NOT review agents; 1 of the 3 review agents is used,
  2 remain for the review of this round's code. One mapping agent rooted one Grep at the repo (glob *.py) -
  a breach of the quarantine rule as written; ripgrep honours .git/info/exclude, which lists the
  quarantined directory, and nothing from it was returned or opened. Recorded in the report.
  What the map established (file:line in the maps):
  - The memoryless retreat _retreat_to_safe_interior_direction has 4 live callers, all inside the searcher
    hazard gate: on-cell hazard (G1), edge-blocked direction (G2), the near-field retreat within 6 of
    FIRE OR SMOKE (G3, the 3b(i) rule - the twin of the 3b(ii) route veto A1-R removed), the lookahead
    re-rank / final retreat (G4 / G5).
  - The gate runs inline at 5 sites (planner wind target, unforced wind target, the lawnmower sweep, the
    escape-target fallback, the retarget fallback) and AGAIN in execute() on the result; the known-victim
    branch (computed_from_target / victim_escape_committed / victim_stuck_escape) and hold_escape are
    gated only there. The latched route owns only the escape / forced-retarget steps.
  - The route measures "near" by burning cells (d0); the gate by fire or smoke. The route's BFS does not
    plan within the edge filter, and breaks to a wait when its first step meets true smoke the plan did
    not see. A stale _last_escape_method (never reset per step) can exempt a fallback step from the
    second gate pass.
  - The fail-safe role-preserving search path (_execute_role_preserving_search) applies
    _apply_final_direction_safety, a second memoryless hazard escape; it is dormant in practice (fail-safe
    actions carry no uav_id) but reachable in code.
  - Targets: in coverage mode (any victim unresolved) the dominant sources are the commit-hold target and
    the pocket machinery's sticky escape_target; the generator's relocation (_finalize_coverage_target) can
    re-issue a blacklisted cell; nothing consults the route's state. C/S 9610 (the Part 3 (N) run): the
    long wait of searcher 2504 was a sticky escape_target, (45, 45) then (44, 45) - a one-cell shift that a
    strictly target-keyed latch treats as a new route.
  - Return-to-base legs are steered by agents.py (A2's dock path) and ignore the executor's direction.
  - Session 3b's territory (the generator's target selection / region targeting) is NOT modified by this
    design: the give-up CALLS the generator's existing helpers (_clear_target_hold,
    _blacklist_target_neighborhood) and refuses targets on the executor side.

1. THE RULES (two new switches; every new behaviour only while its switch is on AND A1-R is active)
================================================================================
1.1 (a) SEARCHER_FIRE_ROUTE_OWNER (ships 1; off only on an exact zero)
  a1 THE STEP TARGET. Each searcher step records the target it steers toward, at its source: the known
     victim, the escape target, the planner / executor wind target, the sweep target, the retarget
     target. Reset at the start of every execute() (with _last_escape_method).
  a2 THE HAND-OFF. The searcher hazard gate (both invocations) first runs unchanged (its pocket state
     write is kept). If the searcher is LATCHED on a route whose goal is within Manhattan 2 of this step's
     target, or the gate took ANY branch other than its pass-through (G1-G5 - a veto, even one whose
     retreat happens to equal the planner's direction), the step is planned by the latched route toward
     the step's target, through _attempt_pathfinding_toward_target with the fire mode forced. With no
     step target the route's current goal is used; with neither, the no-goal outcome (a4).
  a3 THE ROUTE'S FIRE MODE is entered when forced (a2), when latched, or when the strict hazard distance
     (fire OR smoke - the gate's own measure) is <= SEARCHER_GATE_NEAR_RANGE (6): one measure of "near".
     The clearance T = min(burning distance, 6) and "smoke impassable" are unchanged (R-2, R-3).
     LATCH IDENTITY: a goal within Manhattan 2 of the latched goal is the same route (the latch and its
     counter survive a one-cell shift of the target); the stored goal follows it.
  a4 NO PATH / NO GOAL: on a hazard cell -> the legal neighbour (in bounds, not edge-blocked, no hazard, no
     UAV) with the largest burning distance; inside the near field -> a strictly farther legal neighbour
     (FIRE_RETREAT); else a WAIT (FIRE_WAIT, a stay). A goal reached (Manhattan <= 2) is a no-goal step.
  a5 PLANNED WITHIN THE EDGE GUARD: the fire-mode BFS applies the edge filter as a DIRECTED transition
     constraint (the exact predicate of _victim_edge_blocked_direction on (cell, next cell), penetration
     rule with SEARCHER_CORNER_ESCAPE on, margin rule off), so no guard can veto a route step.
  a6 FIRST-STEP REPLAN: a BFS whose first step meets a hazard the plan did not see (true smoke) replans
     with that cell blocked (at most 4 times) instead of breaking to a wait.
  a7 ONE OWNER IN execute(): route-owned steps (labels ending "_fire_route", FIRE_RETREAT, FIRE_WAIT) are
     exempt from the second gate pass; the exemption no longer depends on _last_escape_method.
  a8 THE FAIL-SAFE SEARCH PATH: for a victim searcher, _execute_role_preserving_search applies the
     searcher gate (with the hand-off) instead of _apply_final_direction_safety.
  a9 LABELS: a handed-off moving step is labelled <origin>_fire_route for the origins
     victim_search_wind_aware, ..._retarget, ..._retarget_to_interior, ..._sweep, computed_from_target,
     victim_escape_committed, victim_stuck_escape; any other origin -> victim_search_fire_route (never
     built on "hold" or "hazard_retreat"). No-path steps keep the canonical FIRE_RETREAT / FIRE_WAIT labels.
     A route-owned sweep step clears force_sweep as a sweep step does.
  NOT CHANGED, AND WHY: 'hold' stays and yield_step_aside (collision yields, not hazard steering; the step
  aside already excludes hazard cells); return-to-base legs (A2's dock path owns them; fire-blind by the
  base-station design, not a veto-over-planner pair).

1.2 (b) SEARCHER_ROUTE_BOUNDED_WAIT (ships 1; off only on an exact zero) and
    SEARCHER_ROUTE_WAIT_LIMIT = W (an integer >= 1; anything else RAISES - the fix1 fraction precedent)
  b1 THE COUNT: per latch, the number of distinct model steps (a step-index guard - the executor can run
     0, 1 or 2 times per step) on which the latched route produced no path (FIRE_RETREAT or FIRE_WAIT),
     excluding steps on a return-to-base leg. CUMULATIVE over the latch - NOT reset by a routed step: in
     C/S 9610 the waits came in runs of 15, 30 and 50 steps separated by 1-2 routed steps, which a
     consecutive count would never bound. Reset when the latch is released (a new goal beyond Manhattan 2,
     the goal reached) and on a give-up.
  b2 THE GIVE-UP, on the W-th no-path step: record the goal as GIVEN UP until step + G; release the
     generator's commit hold (_clear_target_hold, reason "route_give_up"); blacklist the goal's 5x5
     neighbourhood (_blacklist_target_neighborhood - the generator's own exclusion, 15 steps); clear the
     escape target if it is within Manhattan 2 of the goal; set force_coverage_escape and
     force_interior_retarget (the next generator call re-picks); release the latch. The step itself is the
     no-path outcome already computed.
  b3 THE REFUSAL, while a give-up is live: a step target within Manhattan 2 of a given-up goal is refused at
     its source - the escape target is cleared, a wind target is treated as absent (the searcher's own
     lawnmower sweep, the executor's no-target fallback), the sweep advances to its next lane, a known
     victim is skipped for the next nearest. The generator's relocation can re-issue a blacklisted cell
     (its own docstring: 58 breaks onto 2 cells); this is the backstop.
  G = WIND_TARGET_BLACKLIST_COOLDOWN = 15 steps: the refusal lasts exactly as long as the generator's own
  blacklist - the executor enforces it on the sources that do not consult it, no longer.

1.3 THE BOUND - W = 20 (fixed here, before any run). REASONING:
  (i)   The anchor: the longest pre-latch fire-wait was 39 steps. W must bound every latched wait well
        inside that: a latched searcher never idles on one target longer than the worst pre-latch case.
  (ii)  The data (outputs/_fx3b_waitdist.py over the Part 3 A1 arms already on disk - no new run): 670
        latched no-path episodes, median 6, p75 13, p90 34, p95 47, longest 104. 355 ended with the path to
        the SAME target reopening (median 4 steps); 314 ended with the planner retargeting on its own.
        Reopenings within W: 71% at 10, 81% at 15, 85% at 20, 87% at 25, 91% at 39. Idle steps in episodes
        beyond W: <= 4002 at 10, 3050 at 15, 2415 at 20, 944 at 39.
  (iii) The controller's own definition of a pocket: WIND_POCKET_CAMP_THRESHOLD = 20 stationary steps is
        where the existing pocket machinery already declares a searcher camped and forces an escape. W =
        20 makes the route's give-up agree with it.
  W = 20 keeps 85% of the waits that would have reopened (the knee of the curve: 10 -> 20 recovers 14
  points, 20 -> 39 only 6 more) and cuts the tail where the idle time is (105 episodes, <= 2415 steps).
  THE SENSITIVITY BOUND: W = 39, the pre-latch longest - reported, never used to pass a gate.
