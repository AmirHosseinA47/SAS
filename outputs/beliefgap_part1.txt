Neither carries the two names. read_value takes the dict branch, .get() misses,
and the default {} is returned. There is no other outcome available.

Construction of global_analysis_result: wildfire_model.py:1707-1712 builds it as
`analysis_snapshot.global_result.to_dict()` plus exactly four assignments

getattr branch of read_value could not find one. Single live call site:
wildfire_model.py:1713, reached from _run_adaptation_space_generation (:1662).

WHERE THE DATA ACTUALLY LIVES - three live homes, none of them where the
generator looks:

REAL:      belief_gap_regions is [] on 3120/3120 calls. The lookup is

             3. MissionDecision has no `parameters` field, so even on selection

NOT (D) REDUNDANT: the concept IS duplicated - GlobalMonitor._belief_gap_indicators
           computes it correctly from the same belief - so deletion is

NOT (A) ACTIONABLE: there is no measurable consequence, and no fix confined to
