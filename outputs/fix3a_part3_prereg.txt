AMENDMENT 3 - THE REVIEW OF PART 2 (written after the review, BEFORE any Part 3 run)
  One review agent (1 of the 3 allowed) read the Part 2 diff at 8497449a. Its findings and what was done:
  1  DEFECT, A1-R STILL CYCLES IN A STATIC FIRE. Only the steps inside the near field planned with the
     clearance; at d0 = 7 the unchanged planner stepped straight back to 6 and undid the BFS detour
     (repro: searcher (25, 19), fire (25, 25), target (25, 40) - a two-cell livelock), and inside the
     field the greedy step and the pocket-escape cells (which change every step) allowed contour cycles.
     FIXED - THE FIRE-MODE LATCH (uav_executor._route_within_fire_field): once a burning cell is within 6
     on a route, the route stays in fire mode, latched on its target (wind state "fire_route_target"),
     until the target changes or is reached (Manhattan <= 2). Fire mode plans ONLY by a BFS over a fixed
     admissible set - burning distance >= T = min(d0, 6), no fire, no smoke (a second pass plans through
     smoke, never steps into it), no other UAV; no greedy step, no pocket cells. In a static fire with
     static UAVs T never decreases and each step shortens the path in a fixed set: no cycle. No path:
     inside the near field a strictly-farther retreat, else a wait (outside it, a latched route waits -
     it does not walk away one cell per step). The dock-fix lesson again: a recovery that engages only
     where it is needed hands the steer back to the rule that undoes it.
