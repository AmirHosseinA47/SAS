THE RULES (STATIONARY_YIELD_FIX, one switch, ships 1; read only while UAV_HOLD_STATIONARY is on; 0 =
the item-2 behaviour of 611c2a4 exactly - identity mf2rX == mf2cX 16/16):
  1. NO EDGE ESCAPE: a yield no longer escapes for standing on the grid's outer ring or for an off-grid
     heading (a stay cannot leave the grid). The tracker hazard escape and the stuck-count escape (the
     ruled backstop) remain. A YIELD STEP RECORDS NO DRIFT: a refused move on a yield step (a step
     aside or backstop escape into a cell that got taken) is right-of-way coordination, which
     COLLISION_RISK already reports - not a deviation from the UAV's own course.
  2. STEP ASIDE: a yielder whose cell is the cell a partner needs - the target of the partner's
     committed move of this step (partners have lower ids and are dispatched first), else the target of
     its last move if that was refused - moves to an in-bounds, UAV-free, hazard-free (not burning, not
     smoke), non-depot neighbour that is not a partner's cell or needed cell (farthest from the needed
     cells, then from the partners, then from the grid edge). None -> it stays (the backstop remains).
     Order of a yield step: tracker hazard escape > step aside > stuck-count escape > stay.
  3. NO YIELD AT A DEPOT - the exact rule: a pair produces no yield when EITHER UAV is on a return leg,
     docked, or stands in the DEPOT APPROACH AREA = every cell within Manhattan 3 of a depot cell. 3 =
     the depot's doorstep ring (1) + the collision radius (2): every UAV that can be in a collision pair
     with a UAV on the doorstep. A launch leg (berth -> first cell outside the area) lies inside the
     area, so it is covered. WHY: returns and launches converge on a few fixed cells (the berths sit at
     each depot's inner corner); a stationary UAV there is an obstacle on the only lanes in and out,
     and UAVs that launched together held each other in place (the C/south freeze). Near a depot the
     move rule (one UAV per cell) and the depot's own berth / dock-fix logic sequence the traffic;
     COLLISION_RISK still fires there (a real condition) but no longer holds anyone. SYMMETRIC on
     purpose: the literal "does not hold another UAV" (the partner side) would still let a UAV inside
     the area stop for one outside, and a stop inside the area is the obstacle.
  Tests: tests/test_fix2_yield_fix.py, 37; each rule fails its tests when reverted in memory (8
  reverts) - including, since ded5280e, the step aside's exemption from the searcher gate (a searcher
  steps aside with a burning cell 4 away; without the exemption the gate turns it into its own retreat).

ROW B - FIRE SOURCE (GLOBAL_ANALYZER_FIRE_SOURCE_FIX). The global analyzer now reads the fire belief:
  HIGH_PRIORITY_FIRE_REGION, STALE_HIGH_PRIORITY_REGION and FIRE_SPREAD_ACCELERATING fire at the
  predicted rates, and nothing changes - at GLOBAL_PLANNER_MODE 0 no planner consumes them. The paper
  may say the global layer detects fire regions; it may not say the system adapts to them.

ITEM 1 - REAL ALARMS (FAILSAFE_REAL_ALARMS). The fail-safe mode is now driven by real conditions:
  normal on 80% of steps (was 0.3%); collision risk on the 5-10% of UAV-steps where two airborne UAVs
  are within 2 cells; sustained drift on 1-2%; the link alarm never. Every fail-safe mode frequency
  published before fix2 is superseded. The fleet search mode now needs a fleet-level condition - "a
  fire burns that no UAV observes" - which in this model is ground truth, not an inference (R7): say
  so, and do not describe it as belief-driven. The planning echo (a reasonless decision re-entering
  the mode as a reason) is closed.

ITEM 2 - A HOLD HOLDS (UAV_HOLD_STATIONARY). 'hold' was a moving step produced by false alarms; it is
  now a stationary right-of-way yield of the higher-id UAV in a close airborne pair, at most 3 steps in
  a row, never in front of a return leg. The paper's fail-safe "safe hold" means this yield, with its
  measured exceptions (FOLLOW-UP 1): a fire hazard, a return leg, 3 steps without movement. With
  STATIONARY_YIELD_FIX (FOLLOW-UP 3) it never escapes for the grid edge, records no drift, steps aside
  when it stands on the cell its partner needs, and does not happen at a depot; the yield clusters at
  the depot (FOLLOW-UP 2) are gone. A pair that travels together still slows the higher id (yield
  stalls away from depots). The 'fire_flank_hold' label is a lateral patrol step, not a hold.
  PAPER FINDING (recorded as ruled): making the fail-safe hold genuinely stationary exposed a depot
  coordination deadlock that the old moving hold had masked.

ITEM 3a - WIND-AWARE Y-COMMIT (SEARCHER_WIND_COVERAGE_FIX). Correct and tested (T7), but inert on the
  evaluated configurations (value-identical to off): no outcome may be attributed to it.

ITEM 3b - THE GATE'S NEAR FIELD (SEARCHER_GATE_NEAR_FIELD). The searcher hazard gate now acts only
  within 6 cells of a strict hazard, on the gate and on the pathfinding route. Wind-aware search
  operates for the first time (T7 and T8 pass; the B/west edge loop is gone); drone time on burning
  cells roughly halves; on 16 fresh CRN seeds victim deaths do not rise materially (+1, a dispatch
  cascade that traded a firefighter death for a victim death). Open for the maintainer: 2 fewer
  rescues and 1 more never-detected victim on those seeds, the rescue changes all under north wind -
  a screen, not a result. D-7 ACCEPTED (ships ON); the measurement round must report results BY WIND
  DIRECTION to settle the north-wind screen. Earlier results were produced by a searcher that, in
  effect, fled every hazard on the map and ignored the wind.

ITEM 4 - STAGGERED LAUNCH (STAGGERED_LAUNCH_BATTERY) - SHIPS OFF. Both the even and the compact stagger
  remove the no-searcher gap and every whole-fleet-out step in C and D, but the gap they close falls
