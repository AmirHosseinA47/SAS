  --dim-hook deny     WildFireModel.HEIGHT/.WIDTH become class-level properties
                      whose setter stores the value and whose getter RAISES
                      AttributeError: every getattr(model, "HEIGHT", 50) returns
                      50 and _grid_dimension returns None - the 8520706 read
                      path, reproduced by the same mechanism.       (control)
  --dim-hook record   same properties, getter returns the value and attributes
                      every read to file:function:line.
  --dim-observe       wraps the two helpers (calls / non-dead results per
                      caller) and six consumers; each consumer call is evaluated
                      twice - the real call, returned, and a counterfactual with
                      the reads denied, compared and discarded. Nested wrapped
                      calls inside a counterfactual pass straight through.
  --dim-pin a,b,c     _grid_dimension returns None when called from the named
                      functions (the one-site-live ablation arms).
  Implementation outputs/_dim_hooks.py (the battery round's class-property
  pattern). Read-only by construction; PROVEN inert by byte-identity: the

12. RECOMMENDATION
================================================================================
  Ship the two lines as a correctness fix, on the following record:
  - the model's size is now readable; every consumer in Part 1's inventory
    reads it (6.2); the 100x100 blocker W1 is removed;
  - the control arm proves the change is the cause of every 50x50 difference;
  - what changes at 50x50 is the removal of three always-on searcher overrides
    and the addition of an edge test for fire-tracker holds; the woken
    behaviour is what the code always said it did;
  - its effect on outcomes over 23 seeds is neutral-to-negative on victims
    (rescued +2 / +0, dead -1 / +5) and neutral on firefighters (0 / -1), and
    the only gain is one seed's worth. It is not an improvement and must not
    be recorded as one.
  - the 50x50 gates recorded before it (latbase, phfix3, the val50 numbers in
    gridscale_part1.txt) stop being the comparison point; outputs/_ffr_dimfix_*
    is the new baseline for the canonical 13 and outputs/_ffr_dimfreshfix_* for
    the fresh 10, and the route_blocked gate needs a fresh baseline prefix on
    the fixed source before it is used as a comparison again.

  The two failed gate items, and what I would do about each:
  - test_no_crash_zero_victims / searcher hazard exposure. This is not the
    plumbing; it is the searcher hazard gate being, for the first time, what
    its code says: edge-only. The interior hazard avoidance the searchers had
    was an accident of the dead helper. I would NOT loosen the test - it
    encodes a bound the maintainer chose - and I would not re-add the override.
    The honest options are (a) ship the fix and open a follow-up round on
    searcher hazard avoidance in the interior, with this test and the 23-pair
    burning-cell exposure as its gate, accepting one red test until then; or
    (b) hold the fix until that round lands. I recommend (a): the defect blocks
    the 100x100 move the maintainer has already decided on, and the exposure
    cost is measured, bounded (+1.1 and +0.7 points of searcher steps) and
    attributable to one gate (C7).
  - route_blocked D/east 909. A unit unassigned by the incident pathway while
    flagged, whose victim then dies, is not covered by D6 (which handles a unit
    still bound to the dying victim). One helper-level clearing in the same
    place D6 clears, for a flagged-and-unassigned unit when the last live
    victim goes terminal and the unit is not fire-enclosed, would close it. Out
    of this round's scope by the brief; reachable by any re-rolled dispatch,
    not only this one.

  A separate question this round exposes but does not answer: the three
  searcher gates were always-on for the whole history of the repository, and
  the searcher tuning of every prior round (wind-aware search, corridor,
  sweep, pocket escape) was done against searchers whose chosen direction was
  being overridden on more than half of their hazard-gate calls. Whether that
  tuning still fits searchers that now follow their own plans is a round of
  its own - and the hazard-exposure follow-up above is its first item.
