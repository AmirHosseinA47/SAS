THE CONSTRAINT
  - 3b's TARGET CHOICE MUST BE REACHABILITY-AWARE. A searcher must not be sent to a target it cannot reach
    under the guards it steers by: fire clearance, smoke, the edge band, other UAVs.
  - 3b's GATES MUST INCLUDE RUNS THAT NEVER FINISH (N) AND OSCILLATION EPISODES OF EVERY KIND (O). The broad
    measure is <= 2 cells with >= 4 changes in 10 steps, plus the pocket-style episode, anywhere on the grid.
    Count and longest are reported for every arm, next to deaths and exposure.

WHY (evidence: outputs/fix3a_report.txt, outputs/fix3a_r2_report.txt, outputs/fix3a_final_record.txt)
  1. THE REMAINING NEAR-FIRE OSCILLATION COMES FROM TARGET SELECTION THAT CANNOT SEE REACHABILITY.
     - In the shipped state (fix3a Part 2, a1373935, measured in Part 3) there are 1-6 short two-cell
       oscillations per arm, none near a depot.
     - The mechanism: the searcher hazard gate vetoes an ordinary search / sweep step toward a target the route
       cannot reach, and the planner draws the searcher back next step. Of 372 episode steps, 147 are gate
       vetoes.
     - The one run that never finishes (C/S 9610) is the same pattern as a wait. The searcher holds a target
       behind a fire for about 95 steps, and the victim is found at step 337 instead of 188.
  2. ROUND 2 SHOWED THAT ABSORBING THE VETOES SILENTLY DOES NOT HELP; IT MAKES IT WORSE.
     - Round 2 routed every gate veto into one owner, the latched route (structurally exact: 0 memoryless
       steps, 0 edge-filter violations). The generator lost its only feedback: the retreat's label had
       forced a retarget and blacklisted the area.
     - So it kept choosing unreachable targets. The result was 28-36 give-ups per arm, repeat-region
       give-ups, later detections, and a victim never detected on D/east 303.
     - The generator's relocation re-issues neighbours of an unreachable region: on C/S 9610 it produced 3-5
       cells apart, a fresh wait each time.
     - A controller-side fix cannot supply what the target choice does not know. The target generator (the
       region targeting and target_regions of session 3b) is where reachability must enter.
  3. THE GATES THAT MISSED IT BEFORE.
     - Session 2's D-7 condition checked deaths and exposure, not livelock or unfinished runs, which is how
       3b(ii) merged with 3 never-finishing runs.
     - fix3a's own prototypes counted only the depot-pocket kind of oscillation.
     - Both steering rounds of session 3a moved the oscillation elsewhere rather than removing it:
       - Part 2 moved it from the depots to the gate path;
       - round 2 moved it into the route's own no-path rules, and the tests that would have shown it were
         not run at the gate's measure before pre-registration.

WHAT 3b SHOULD PRE-REGISTER FROM THE START
  - (N) = 0 and (O) = 0 (every kind) as gates on every arm that changes searcher movement or targets.
  - A prototype screen measured by the SAME (O) measure before the pre-registration.
  - Reachability measured directly: the share of issued targets with no admissible path from the searcher at
    issue time, and the give-up / retarget count - before and after.
  - The edge ring: every never-detected victim so far had fled into the outer ring (edge distance <= 3).
    The edge filter is not the cause (radius 8 reaches the ring), but targets that bring searchers within 8
    of the ring are part of the coverage question.
  - "Every kind" reaches beyond searchers. The (O) measure counts searcher episodes only, but D/south 505's
    route_blocked loss contains a firefighter episode: a 180-step approach near the fire, 106 revisits, and a
    12-step two-cell episode (16, 15) <-> (17, 15) (outputs/fix3a_final_record.txt L3). 3b does not change
    firefighter movement, so it is not a 3b gate. The same measure applied to firefighters should be reported
    next to it, so that a searcher change which shifts dispatch order cannot hide a firefighter livelock.
