Scope: I read the code only. Nothing was run or edited, and I opened nothing under outputs/. Paths below are relative to E:\Projects\SAS. There are four findings.

(1) After the executor returns a direction, three more places can refuse or change the move.
- execute() (src_extension/execution/uav_executor.py:432-469) exempts three kinds of searcher step from the hazard gate: rtb_waypoint, yield_step_aside, and routed steps. A step counts as routed if its label is victim_search_escape_bfs, or if the label contains "retarget_to_interior" and _last_escape_method is non-empty. A "hold" label skips every filter.
- The fail-safe search-mode path (_execute_role_preserving_search, :501-537) applies _apply_final_direction_safety to every label, routed or not. This also covers FIRE_WAIT.
- In agents.py, UAV.advance first runs _apply_return_to_base. With the shipped settings (BASE_STATION_RETURN_MECHANISM=2, FREE_CELL_DOCKING=1) it overwrites selected_dir and clears the stay flag (agents.py:818-842, 1005-1017). UAV.move then refuses the move in these cases: docked, no direction applied this step, a stay, off-grid, or another UAV on the target cell in the live grid (agents.py:1078-1106, 402-408). None of the agents.py guards exempts routed labels. None checks fire.

(2) The edge filter is a pure predicate on (current cell, next cell).
- With SEARCHER_CORNER_ESCAPE on: p(c) = max(0, 4 - min(cx, 49-cx)) + max(0, 4 - min(cy, 49-cy)). A move c->n is allowed iff p(c) = 0 or p(n) < p(c).
- With it off: d(c) = min(cx, cy, 49-cx, 49-cy). A move is allowed iff d(c) >= 4 or d(n) > d(c).
- The on rule allows everything the off rule allows, plus some moves where both axes are within 3 of an edge.
- It can be applied as a directed-edge constraint inside a BFS whose state is the cell, without changing what it allows per step.
- Consequences: from p=0 any move is legal, so a searcher can step to edge distance 3, then must step back out. Cells with p >= 2 can never be entered from outside the band.
- Today the fire-mode BFS and _bfs_escape_direction do not apply the filter, so a routed step can walk into the band. Only the route's retreat/wait fallback applies it (uav_executor.py:2249).

(3) The lookahead is a straight-line test of 3 cells, and the hazard buffer shrinks as hazard_buffer_level rises (level 0 gives buffer 2, level 1 gives 1, level 2 gives 0). "Strict smoke" means belief SMOKE_OBSCURED cells. The per-cell hazard test also counts the true Fire-agent smoke; the distance measures do not.

(4) The veto-on-planner pattern is still present. Main example: the gate's near-field retreat (uav_executor.py:2817-2838) replaces any non-routed searcher step within 6 of burning or belief-smoke cells. It keeps the planner's label, so the planner never learns of the veto.
- The latch "fire_route_target" is only read inside _route_within_fire_field. The route is only called from the escape-target, forced-retarget and fallback branches.
- The planner-wind, plain wind-target, sweep and known-victim branches ignore the latch and go through the gate.
- The route's near test uses burning cells only; the gate's uses fire plus smoke. They disagree.

Smaller vetoes remain, with file:line in the entries below:
- the BFS first-step smoke check that jumps to wait;
- the fail-safe final-safety pass;
- the return-to-base override;
- live-grid occupancy refusal;

On B/N 9671: the filter does not make edge cells undetectable. Detection is Euclidean distance <= 8 from any UAV (wildfire_model.py:1419-1420), and edge distance 3 or 4 is reachable under either rule. Whether the hole exists in the shipped arm needs run data.

OPEN QUESTIONS
- How often does a searcher get executed twice in one step (the fail-safe dispatch with search_mode_active, then the local dispatch)? And how often does the fail-safe result stand, with _apply_final_direction_safety applied to routed or FIRE_WAIT steps? This needs run data. Either way, the W counter for (b) must be keyed on the step index (model.evaluation_timesteps_counter), not on the number of calls.

- Which near-field test should decide the handoff in (a)? The gate uses burning plus belief smoke within 6 (uav_executor.py:2827); the route uses burning only (d0, :2185 and :2201). The route's clearance T also uses burning only.
- Should the fire-mode BFS take on the edge filter as a directed transition constraint? That would make band targets with no reachable p <= 1 cell unreachable, so they would be dropped after W. Without it, routed steps can enter the band and the next non-routed owner will force the searcher back out.

- In the BFS first-step smoke veto (uav_executor.py:2236-2238) the loop breaks instead of continuing. Should it replan without that cell? As written it can produce waits that are not really 'no path' cases, which would inflate W counts.
