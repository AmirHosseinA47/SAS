1. THE HORIZON AND WHY (as pre-registered), AND HOW THE MARGIN WAS USED
================================================================================
  360 STEPS, with --set BATCH_SIZE=360.
    - WildFireModel.step() calls sys.exit(0) once it passes BATCH_SIZE + 1 steps
      (wildfire_model.py:5808). Every harness run passes BATCH_SIZE=300, so 301 steps
      is the ceiling.
    - BATCH_SIZE is read nowhere else in the simulation. The only other readers are the
      dashboard's informational batch_size field and the panel that displays it
      (whole-source grep, quarantine excluded).
  THE WORK LEFT AT 240 (the pre-registered bound)
    - For every victim still ASSIGNED at 240: the manhattan distance from the assigned
      unit to the victim, plus the victim's distance to the nearest edge. For a unit
      already carrying the victim: the unit's distance to the nearest edge.
    - Largest per arm:
        control           45 cells (south/half/1048395951 victim_0)
        shipped           31
        positioning-only  33
    - 120 extra steps = 2.7x / 3.9x / 3.6x those maxima.
  HOW IT WENT - THE BOUND IS A LOWER BOUND
    - All 19 rescues completed after 240, in all arms, needed more steps than their
      bound: 1.06x to 42x.
    - The bound assumes the nearest edge. A rescue actually completes at a FIXED exit
      cell chosen at pickup, and units retreat, detour and (section 0, point 4) stall.
      The prereg's "nearest grid edge, where a rescue completes" was imprecise on this.
    - The control's 45-cell case did NOT finish in the 120 steps. Contact came at step
      356; at 360 its carrier was stepping between (45,44) and (46,44), 3 cells from
      the edge.
    - The shipped arm's 31-cell case (east/half/133894353) used 87 of the 120.
    - In the shipped and positioning-only arms, every rescue in progress at 240
      finished.
