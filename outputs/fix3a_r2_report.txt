5. DIAGNOSIS - WHY ROUND 2 FAILS (outputs/_fx3r_osc_mech.txt; traces in section 8)
================================================================================
5.1 BAND - the route's two no-path rules take turns (7-9 of the (O) episodes in most gated arms).
  - Inside the near field with no path, the route retreats to the neighbour STRICTLY FARTHER from the fire.
    That neighbour can be inside the edge band.
  - There, with no path again, the band exit (review fix C4) steps OUT toward lower penetration, which is back
    toward the fire.
  - Example: B/S 9606 alternates (4, 25) <-> (3, 25) for 17 steps.
  - Both steps belong to the one owner, but they optimise two different quantities (burning distance, band
    penetration): two owners again, inside the route.
  - The same pair recreates depot pockets, because the corner depots lie in the band.
5.2 FIRE - no-path alternations with a moving front (2-7 episodes per arm).
  - The burning distance flickers by +-1 as cells ignite and burn out.
  - A strictly-farther retreat at the near-field boundary then takes turns with the wait or with the next
    retreat.
5.3 THE PLANNER NO LONGER HEARS THE VETO.
  - Under the legacy gate, a retreat's label ("victim_search_hazard_retreat") was the target generator's only
    signal. The wind-state sync forced a retarget and blacklisted the area around the searcher.
  - Under (a) the veto is absorbed by the route and the signal is gone. The generator keeps issuing targets
    the route cannot reach. The route waits, then gives up.
  - The cost: 28-36 give-ups per arm, repeat-region give-ups 7-10, ~2/3 of waits far from any hazard, later
    detections (A1: 2 victims never detected, 3 runs unfinished; D/east 303 never detected).
  - It is also the trajectory-test failure: under variable wind the planner's target no longer depends on the
    wind within 40 steps.
  - This is the same "veto layered on a planner that cannot see it", one level up: the TARGET GENERATOR
    cannot see the route's refusals.

6. OPTIONS FOR THE MAINTAINER (nothing below is implemented; round 2 stops here)
================================================================================
  (i) ONE OBJECTIVE FOR THE NO-PATH STEP. The retreat never enters or deepens the band. The band exit never
      lowers the burning distance inside the near field. Candidates are ranked by one lexicographic potential
      (band penetration first, then burning distance), so no pair of no-path steps can cycle. This targets
      5.1 and part of 5.2.
  (ii) MAKE THE REFUSAL VISIBLE TO THE PLANNER. A hand-off and a no-path step emit the planner signal the
      legacy retreat label gave (force the retarget, blacklist the unreachable goal's region - not the
      searcher's own cell). This targets 5.3. A reachability-aware target choice would be the complete answer,
      but it is the generator's (session 3b's) territory.
  (iii) FALL BACK: keep fix3a Part 2's searcher (A1 as in Part 3) and ship A2 + B. The Part 3 report's option
      (c) still applies: A2 and B pass every gate in both rounds.
  (iv) Whatever is chosen, fix the TEST-ISOLATION defect separately: the wind-trajectory tests depend on
      global state left by earlier tests (variable wind, team size). They should set their own configuration.
  MY RECOMMENDATION: (i) + (ii) as one round-3 design, pre-registered on the same sample with the gates
  unchanged, with a PROTOTYPE SCREEN that measures (O) by the broad measure BEFORE the pre-registration. That
  screen is what round 2 lacked for its own rules, and 5.1 and 5.3 would have shown in it.

7. THE LESSON (ruled) AND WHAT ROUND 2 ADDS
================================================================================
  RULED: A1 removed one veto-against-planner livelock but left the same pattern on the steps the route does
  not control. THE RULE THAT FOLLOWS: A GUARD MUST BE PLANNED WITHIN, EVERYWHERE IT ACTS.
  ADDED BY THIS ROUND:
  - ONE OWNER NEEDS ONE OBJECTIVE. The owner's no-path rules (a fire retreat and a band exit) optimised
    different quantities and livelocked each other. "Planned within the guard" means one potential over all
    the guards (fire distance, edge band), not one planner per guard.
  - REMOVING A VETO REMOVES A SIGNAL. The memoryless retreat was also the planner's only feedback. When a
    lower layer absorbs a refusal, the layer above must be told, or it keeps asking for what cannot be done.
  - A FIX FROM A REVIEW IS A NEW RULE. The band exit (C4) was reviewed and unit-tested alone. Its interaction
    with the retreat is the dominant failure. Every rule added after a prototype needs its own screen by the
    gate's measure, not only a unit test.
  - GLOBAL STATE LEAKS BETWEEN TESTS. Two regression tests passed for months only in their own order.
