======================================================================
"Without dcD's settings" = arm Z. A number that reproduces in Z is not caused
by dcD's settings.
  G1  each seed s with rescued(D,s) < rescued(ihrest,s):
        CARRIED iff rescued(Z,s) <= rescued(D,s)   (the regression against
                ihrest is present, at least as deep, without dcD's settings)
        NEW     otherwise
  G2  if D has zero recoveries: CARRIED iff Z also has zero; else NEW
  G3  each unit (seed, wind, ff id) route_blocked and alive at step 240 in D:
        CARRIED iff the same unit is route_blocked and alive at 240 in Z
        NEW     otherwise
      (G3 counts every such unit, as the gate does. Section 5's horizon/latch
      classification is reported beside it and does NOT change G3's label.)
SECONDARY ATTRIBUTION, reported for every failing element, never changing the
label: (i) does it reproduce in K (dock fix off, same commit)? (ii) is it the
depot round's recorded failure - identical per seed to dcrbD (fix absent,
9f77178)? This answers "is it the same [FAIL] as before" before anything is
attributed to dcD.
PRECONDITION: MET iff each of G1, G2, G3 passes or every failing element is
CARRIED. Otherwise NOT MET: the failure is reported, nothing is fixed.

======================================================================
5. LATCHED UNITS - HORIZON ARTIFACT OR GENUINE LATCH
======================================================================
Population: every unit route_blocked and alive at step 240 in ANY gate arm.
Reported per unit: arm, seed, wind, unit, the step the final route_blocked
episode began (last transition into route_blocked, from the shard's per-step
`fires` and the harness ff_steps), every earlier episode and recovery, position,
the run's terminal_step.
Classification, by EXTENSION: the same tuple and configuration through the
harness with --steps 300 (tag drl<arm>). The extension is valid only if its
fire_digests 1-240 and ff_steps rows 1-240 equal the 240-step harness run's.
  HORIZON ARTIFACT  the unit leaves route_blocked for a non-dead status at some
                    step 241-300
  GENUINE LATCH     the unit is route_blocked and alive at every step 241-300
  INDETERMINATE     it dies while blocked in 241-300, or the extension is not
                    valid - reported with its shape, not folded into a class
The two classes are counted separately and never summed into one figure.
Shape descriptors (descriptive only): episode length at 240; recoveries in the
20 steps before the episode; whether the unit held an assignment at 240.
