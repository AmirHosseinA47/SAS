SUMMARY
This is an inventory of every code path that can set, override or re-filter a victim searcher's direction or stay for a hazard reason, at the shipped settings: VICTIM_SEARCHER_HAZARD_RETREAT_RANGE=99 (common_fixed_variables.py:288), SEARCHER_GATE_NEAR_FIELD=1 (:157), SEARCHER_ROUTE_FIRE_FIELD=1 (:166), UAV_HOLD_STATIONARY=1 (:124), STATIONARY_YIELD_FIX=1 (:130), BASE_STATION_RETURN_MECHANISM=2 (:692). All citations are to files under E:\Projects\SAS\. Nothing under outputs/ was opened.

**Where the hazard steering actually happens.** It is all in src_extension/execution/uav_executor.py:
- The searcher gate, _apply_victim_searcher_hazard_gate (2764-2883), has 5 veto branches and 2 off-grid guard sites.
- It is called at 5 places inside _resolve_direction_intent / _apply_retarget_with_pathfinding_fallback (1400, 1420, 1470, 1597, 2321), and a second time in execute() at 458.
- The memoryless retreat, _retreat_to_safe_interior_direction (2703-2762), has 4 live callers, all inside the gate (2775, 2790, 2834, 2873). A 5th caller (2105) is the fix2 3b(ii) veto, which is dead while A1-R is on.
- The fix3a latched route, _route_within_fire_field (2127-2266), is reached only from 3 places: the pocket escape_target branch (1389) and the forced-coverage/interior retarget branches (1467 and 1492, via _apply_retarget_with_pathfinding_fallback 2313).
- Every other searcher step gets its direction from the memoryless greedy _choose_best_direction and then goes through the gate, which has no memory. That covers the known-victim branch 1071-1333, the planner wind target 1418-1437, the unforced wind target 1470-1477 and the lawnmower sweep 1591-1602. So the latch owns only the retarget/escape steps, and only while it returns non-None.

**Where the route hands the step back to the memoryless path.** It returns None in 3 cases, and each time the caller falls back to _choose_best_direction plus the gate (1397-1405, 2321-2325):
- 2204: no greedy step while unlatched.
- 2207: the greedy step would enter a hazard.
- 2212-2214: the target is within Manhattan 2. The latch is also cleared here.

It also drops the latch whenever the goal changes (2196-2198). Gate retreats force that, because any label containing "hazard_retreat" blacklists the area around the searcher and forces a retarget (2946-2955).

**How the gate and the route measure hazard differently.**
- The gate's near field measures distance to fire OR smoke: _min_strict_hazard_distance, 2827 → 2668-2685.
- The route's d0 measures distance to burning cells only (2160, 2185).
- So a veto caused by smoke within 6 would reach the route with d0 > 6, unlatched, and get the greedy planner.

**What the fire-mode search does not plan within.** Its BFS blocks fire, smoke, cells inside the clearance and other UAVs (2231-2232). It does not apply the edge-band filter (_victim_edge_blocked_direction 2552-2585) or the lookahead hazard buffer (2587-2615). Only its no-path "legal neighbour" list applies the edge filter (2249). Routed steps are exempt from the gate, so this causes no veto conflict today. But if edge-blocked vetoes are sent to the route, the route itself could choose an edge-blocked step.

**The FIRE_WAIT has no bound** (2265-2266). The only existing thing that ends one is an indirect target change: pocket_streak reaching 20 or sweep_no_move_streak reaching 5 sets force_coverage_escape (local_adaptation_generator.py:1490-1495), which can produce a new target and reset the latch.

**Machinery that does not steer searchers for hazard reasons:**
- safety_checker.py only classifies modes and reasons; it moves nothing.
- UAV.move / advance in agents.py has no hazard logic. It refuses a move only for off-grid or occupied cells (1103). The return-to-base leg overrides selected_dir without looking at fire (651-842, 918-1017).
- wildfire_model.py writes directions only when the pipeline is off (2539-2551).
- The planner rewrites every searcher "hold" into a wind-aware search (local_uav_path_planner.py:200-213, 234-242).
- _dispatch_fail_safe executes no UAV in practice: fail-safe params carry no uav_id key.
- _apply_final_direction_safety is unreachable for searchers in practice.

**Hold and yield steps.**
- The only surviving searcher holds are the COLLISION_RISK yield and a non-emergency hold_position. They either stay without any hazard check (the gate is skipped for "hold" at 452) or produce hold_escape. hold_escape is memoryless and skips hazard cells, then passes through the gate.
- yield_step_aside only steps onto hazard-free cells (656) and is exempt from the gate (447-451).

**What change (a) must re-route through the latched planner:**
1. All 5 gate veto branches, wherever the gate is called.
2. The greedy fallbacks after the route returns None.
3. The known-victim branch, including victim_escape_committed and victim_stuck_escape.
4. The planner, unforced-wind and sweep branches.
5. Possibly hold_escape and yield steps near fire, which are not hazard-triggered.

OPEN QUESTIONS
- Is the edge-blocked gate branch G2 (uav_executor.py:2789-2794) in scope for (a)? It fires anywhere in the edge band, not only near fire. The route's BFS (2231-2232) does not plan within the edge filter, so sending G2 vetoes to the route could produce steps the edge filter forbids.
- Which measure should decide 'near fire' for (a): the gate's fire-or-smoke distance (2827) or the route's burning-only d0 (2185, ruling R-2)? With the current code, a veto triggered by smoke within 6 would reach the route unlatched and be planned greedily (2201-2211).
- What target should the known-victim branch latch on? Its target is the victim position (1047-1070), and victim_escape_committed (1111-1183) is a second latched memory that the gate currently vetoes. Should it be replaced by the route, or kept?
- Does (a) also cover the non-hazard memoryless steps a searcher can take near fire: hold_escape (579, 595, 608, then gated at 458), yield_step_aside (606, hazard-filtered and exempt) and the hazard-blind 'hold' stay (452)?
- How do W and the existing counters interact during a FIRE_WAIT? The stay advances sweep_no_move_streak (>=5 sets force_coverage_escape at local_adaptation_generator.py:1490-1493), pocket_streak (>=20), and _uav_stuck_counts (>=2 sets victim_stuck_escape, >=3 sets hold_escape). Whether a committed escape_target survives those, and so how long a latched wait can actually last, needs a run trace, not code reading.
- Stale _last_escape_method: it persists on the per-UAV cached executor (decision_dispatcher.py:216-223) and is not reset on the route-reached return (2212-2214). Could a gate-fallback label containing 'retarget' then skip the second gate at 453-457? It looks benign today (the first gate already ran), but it matters if (a) keys route ownership on this attribute.
- _dispatch_fail_safe (decision_dispatcher.py:284-299) is dormant only because fail-safe params carry no uav_id or target_entity. I checked failsafe_adaptation_generator.py and fail_safe_planner.py only; any other producer of FailSafeDecision.actions would revive the searcher path to _apply_final_direction_safety (534).
