WHY "HOW BIG IS THE BONUS RELATIVE TO THE GAP" IS THE WRONG QUESTION
  rescue_planner.py:242
      return max(feasible, key=lambda entry: (_preference_adjustment(entry, signals),
                                              entry.score)).option
  The bonus is NOT added to the utility score. It is the PRIMARY element of a
  lexicographic sort key; entry.score is only the tiebreak. Any strictly positive
  preference bonus therefore beats ANY utility gap, however large. _DELAY_CANCEL_BONUS
  could be 0.75 or 0.0001 with identical effect.
  => An additional, independent reason not to tune the constant: its magnitude is never
     compared against anything. The brief's instruction to leave it alone is correct,
     and tuning it could not have rescued the mechanism anyway.
