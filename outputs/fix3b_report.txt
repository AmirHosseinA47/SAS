3. THE G-O FAILURE: THE HAND-BACK WHEN NOTHING IS LEFT TO SEARCH (outputs/_fb3_episode_diag.txt)
================================================================================
  FACT 1. All 21 broad episodes of the Bayes arms (BD set 1 / 2, BF set 2, BF uniform spawn) start AFTER THE
    LAST VICTIM'S FIRST DETECTION ("undetected at start" = 0 in every row).
    The current searcher's episodes are the opposite: 8 of 10 start while a victim is undetected (L2).
  FACT 2. When every victim is detected, N_unf = 0 and lambda = 0. No candidate has mass, so the strategy
    falls back to the current chain, and stays there until step 360. Its share of airborne searcher steps:
    - while an undetected victim remains, the strategy steers 81-90% (BF 82.7% / 90.0%, BD 87.5% / 90.3%,
      BF uniform spawn 81.4%);
    - over the whole run, only 40-51% (27% under uniform spawn).
  FACT 3. LO never runs out of targets (tile ages keep growing after detection). It never hands back, and it
    has no post-detection episode: 0 and 1 episodes, against CUR's 2 and 3.
  FACT 4 (the livelock). In fb3bf2 C/S the hand-back happens at step ~40 (every victim detected at 37).
    - From step 297 to 360 searcher 2503 executes 'victim_search_wind_aware_sweep' on every step,
      alternating between two cells. Its pocket_streak climbs to 86, and no release fires.
    - The current searcher reaches this sweep state continuously from launch and rarely livelocks after
      detection (2 post-detection episodes in 48 runs).
    - The hand-back enters the sweep from a state the current chain never produces: its wind-search state
      was frozen while the strategy steered (sync skipped by design).
  WHAT IT IS. A design gap in the strategies' END STATE. Section 3.6's fallback ("no feasible target -> the
    current behaviour") also fires when the search is COMPLETE, and then for the rest of the run. The
    targeting itself never oscillated: 0 episodes contain a targeting step.
  PROPOSED FIX - for a ruling, not implemented:
    (i) ONE OWNER TO THE END (recommended). When N_unf = 0 or the belief has no mass, a Bayes arm continues
        on the least-observed rule over the same last-cover map, with the same BFS, battery filter and
        commitment. LO itself showed 0 / 1 episodes on these seeds, and it is the arms' own baseline. The
        searcher never re-enters the current chain after its own steering, and it keeps covering (useful
        if N is wrong).
    (ii) Hand back with the current chain's state RESET to its launch state. Cheaper, but untested, and the
        sweep might livelock from a reset state too.
    (iii) Mission complete -> return to a depot / hold. Realistic, but a new behaviour for the exemplar.
  A re-screen of BD + BF on sets 1-2 + uniform spawn (80 runs, about 1.5 h), with the SAME gates, would
  follow the ruling. Lesson again: a fallback is part of the design and needs its own screen - the
  session-3a lesson ("a lower layer that absorbs a refusal must tell the layer above"), here in reverse.

7. WHAT EACH FINDING MEANS FOR THE PAPER
================================================================================
  - IDENTITY. The method ships OFF, provably value-identical to the session-3a system. Every earlier number
    stands as a number of the shipped system.
  - REACHABILITY IS EXACT BY CONSTRUCTION, AND IT WORKS WHERE IT OWNS THE STEP.
    - 100% of 4 215 issued targets had an admissible path.
    - 0 oscillation episodes contain a targeting step.
    - Exposure after targeting steps is about 0.
    - The baseline that never hands back (LO) passes both oscillation gates and reduces searcher exposure
      to 0.02-0.10%.
    This supports session 3b's constraint where the strategy owns the searcher. The causal test (the
    reachability ablation) is still to be run in the measurement round.
  - THE HAND-BACK IS THE NEW FAILURE MODE.
    - A strategy that falls back to a heuristic it does not own inherits that heuristic's livelocks.
    - It also reaches states the heuristic never reached on its own.
    This is the same lesson as session 3a, from the other side: one owner to the end. For the paper it is a
    design rule for MAPE-K plan components that delegate: the delegating component must cover its own end
    state.
  - THE RING. On the fixed ring no strategy beats the current searcher. Under uniform spawn the Bayes-flee
    arm leans better at noise level. That is consistent with 15.1, but 16 pairs cannot show it.
  - BURN-OVER MISSPECIFICATION. An operator-plausible burn-over (0.5) erased the belief's support for a
    victim who escaped (C/S). Model mismatch arms are not optional: the sensitivity is a primary result.
  - FIREFIGHTER OSCILLATION rises with searcher changes. A searcher-side method must report firefighter
    livelock next to its own (session3b_first_constraint.txt's warning, now measured).
  - THE 240-STEP HORIZON flips outcomes by search order (D/south 202), as in session 3a. The measurement
    round's horizon sensitivity (10.7) is necessary.
  - COMPUTE. The method costs under 1% of a model step at the median (2-4 ms belief, 15-17 ms planning).
