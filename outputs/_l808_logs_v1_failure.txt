the --out filename. Every run computed for 5-14 minutes and then died at the
final write.

outputs/_l808_logs/ carry U+F00D in their names (the CR, mangled), which is why

are NOT committed: a filename containing a private-use-area codepoint is not
safe to check out across platforms, and the 28 .out files are zero-length
anyway. The 28 non-empty .err files are reproduced below in full, because they
are the actual evidence for section 10.
