================================================================================
FIX3B - CRN AUDIT (maintainer's request, before Part 2)            outputs/fix3b_crn_audit.txt
Which earlier results were described as CRN or "fire pinned", and which tool produced them.
================================================================================
WHY THIS AUDIT
  _fx3_probe (and the _sd_probe / _mf2_probe chain under it) accepts --set FM2P_CRN=1, writes it into the
  run's params and NEVER applies it: no code in that chain replaces the fire draw (0 FM2P references).
  So any result produced through that chain and labelled CRN would be a stock result under a CRN label.

METHOD (read-only; scripts kept in outputs/ for re-running)
  1. outputs/_fb3_crn_scan.py
     Every top-level outputs/*.txt|*.md (non-recursive; UTF-16 / UTF-8 decoded) was searched for
     "FM2P_CRN", "CRN", "fire pinned", "pinned fire", "pins the fire" and "common random". 59 files
     matched. The claims in the round reports were read in context.
  2. outputs/_fb3_crn_cmds.py
     Every recorded command line that carries FM2P_CRN, from *.argv, queues, *.jsonl, *.sh and state
     files: 863 lines. The script or instrument that ran each one:
     - 128 lines name _fm2_probe_harness.py directly (the mf2hc* argv files and the _mf2_q_p3c queue);
     - 2 lines name _cl_probe.py;
     - 732 lines are pool queue lines (kind|tag|repo|...). Their instrument is chosen by the pool:
       - _cl_pool.sh refuses a CRN line unless the instrument is _cl_obs_crn.py or _cl_probe.py
         (guards at :217-225);
       - _pl_pool.sh maps kind 'fc' to _pl_obs_crn.py (:256-257);
       - _ug_pool.sh and _dfp_pool.sh take HARNESS from the environment, defaulting to the STOCK
         harness. So for those two waves the queue line alone does NOT prove CRN; check 3 decides.
     NO command line carrying FM2P_CRN names _fx3_probe.py, _sd_probe.py or _mf2_probe.py.
  3. outputs/_fb3_crn_json.py - the decisive check, on the output.
     _fm2_probe_harness.py writes an "fm2p" block whose counters.crn_draws counts the replaced fire
     draws. A run is REAL CRN iff that block exists and crn_draws > 0. Every outputs/*.json mentioning
     FM2P_CRN or fm2p was checked. For the sysdebug counterfactuals (_sd_cf.py --cf-crn, its own
     identical construction), the "cf" block's config.crn and counters.crn_draws were checked.

RESULT: EVERY RESULT DESCRIBED AS CRN WAS PRODUCED BY A CRN INSTRUMENT AND CARRIES REAL CRN DRAWS.

THE _fx3_probe CHAIN (fix3a Part 3, round 2, merge runs; sysdebug's plain _sd_probe arms)
  0 of its runs carry FM2P_CRN, and no fix3a report describes any result as CRN or "fire pinned" (0
  matches in fix3a_*.txt). Those results are STOCK, as reported. The risk was prospective: a future run
  passing --set FM2P_CRN=1 to _fx3_probe would have been mislabelled. Part 2 closes that: the probe used
  from now on REFUSES FM2P_CRN unless its CRN flag is on (fix3b_part1.txt section 8).

CAVEATS - NOT CRN-INSTRUMENT ERRORS, BUT THEY MUST NOT BE QUOTED AS PLAIN CRN MEASUREMENTS
  C1 firemech2_report.txt:30-35, :320 - "+239 of +1,379 under common random numbers" is a PROJECTION.
     It is the round-2 CRN probe arm's measured +248, with one run replaced by its control, projected
     onto the shipped strict predicate. The report says so ("Round 3 ran no CRN arm"). Quote it as a
     projection from a CRN arm, not as a CRN measurement of the shipped gate.
  C2 firemech2_report.txt:309 / depotfireproof_report.txt:658, :804 - "PINNED REPLAY" (outputs/
     _fm2_fire_replay.py, which records draws and replays them) is a DIFFERENT instrument from closed-loop
     CRN, and the two disagree on firebreak's physical size (+1,209 of +1,783 vs about zero). Those
     reports leave it open. A "pinned replay" number is not a CRN number; the fire-replay tool's own --crn
     mode is unvalidated against a closed-loop CRN run (_fm2_fire_replay notes :192-197, :231-232).
  C3 firemech2_report.txt:86-87 - Part 1's two pre-registered CRN rows (canonical +166, fresh +73) are
     real CRN (f2c* probe arms) but were not re-measured in tree.
  C4 ungated_horizon_report.txt:135, :333 - the 360-step ungated results are STOCK only; CRN exists at
     240 only. Do not quote a 360-step ungated CRN number.
  C5 CRN arms compare only with CRN arms (memory note): every CRN figure above is paired within its own
     CRN set. None was paired with a stock arm in its report.

FOR THE PAPER
  - Every result in this project labelled CRN is a closed-loop CRN result. The fix3a / sysdebug stock
    results were never labelled CRN.
  - Two labels need care when quoted: projections from a CRN arm (C1), and the separate "pinned replay"
    instrument (C2).
  - From fix3b on, CRN runs go through a probe that refuses the flag unless it applies it, and each CRN
    run's JSON carries crn_draws > 0, which the analyzers check.
