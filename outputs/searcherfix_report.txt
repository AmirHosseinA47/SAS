4. THE ANTI-OSCILLATION MECHANISM, TESTED DIRECTLY
================================================================================
Part 1 predicted that breaking the direction latch would produce STRAIGHT-LINE
motion rather than alternation, because the same starvation that re-emitted an
illegal direction will now re-emit a LEGAL one. The D5 pre-check bounded the
residual 2-cycle risk at about 2 in 10. Measured over the 12 steps after each
guard firing:

    pin                    distinct cells / 12   longest 2-cell alternation
    east/default/101              12                      0     straight line
    east/half/909                 12                      0     straight line
    south/half/404                10                      3     brief wobble
    east/default/303               7                      7     ALTERNATES

  east/default/101: (39,48)->(39,47)->(39,46)->(39,45)->(39,44)->(39,43)->...
  east/half/909:    (48,38)->(47,38)->(46,38)->(45,38)->(44,38)->(43,38)->...

THREE OF FOUR WALK AWAY IN A STRAIGHT LINE. ONE OF FOUR DOES ALTERNATE - and it
matters where: east/default/303 walks four cells inward off the rim and then
ping-pongs at (45,22)<->(45,23), IN THE INTERIOR. That is ordinary planner
behaviour on a freed searcher, not the guard cycling: the guard cannot fire
there, because no direction from an interior cell leaves the grid. The
prediction held in the form it was made - the guard itself is acyclic - and the
one alternation is inside the bound the pre-check set.

5. A DEFECT IN MY OWN INSTRUMENTATION, AND A CLAIM I WITHDREW
================================================================================
I pre-registered a distinguishing action label per guard site as the direct
per-step hook the diagnosis's LIMIT 1 asked for. IT DOES NOT WORK, and the first
number I computed from it was meaningless.

WHAT HAPPENS. The gate runs TWICE in a step - once inside
_resolve_direction_intent (call sites 1277/1297/1347/1474) and again at the
primary dispatch site 425. The inner call substitutes and emits the new label.
The outer call then receives an ALREADY-LEGAL direction, is correctly a no-op -
the idempotency Part 1 s.2.4 required - and returns the HARDCODED original
label, erasing the evidence. Measured: guard firings recorded in uav_actions = 0,
while off-grid refusals went 82 -> 0 and four trajectories demonstrably changed.

WHAT I WITHDREW. I first reported "32 new tight reversals, 0 attributable to a
guard leg". That was an ABSENCE OF SIGNAL, not evidence of innocence, and
stating it as a result would have been misleading. It is replaced by POSITIONAL
attribution, which is sound here precisely because only four runs differ: all 32
new tight reversals are inside those four runs, and 32 were removed in the same
four. Net zero.

THE FIX FOR THE HOOK, not applied this round: at both sites, when the guard is a
no-op but the INCOMING action already records a firing, preserve it rather than
overwrite. Two lines. It changes no behaviour, only the recorded label, but it
would require re-running the 23-run sfON arm to take effect in the corpus, and
positional attribution already answers the question the hook was for. Recorded
as a small follow-up rather than spent on now.

6. THE RESULT THE ROUND DOES NOT GET: DETECTION TIMING
================================================================================
The brief names detection timing as the cost the fix exists to recover, citing
partial rho +0.465 / +0.442 / +0.438 across three pools and tertile means
73 -> 89 -> 116 steps. MEASURED HERE: det_max_first_seen is IDENTICAL in all 23
tuples. Not "small", not "noisy" - identical, 23 ties.

WHY, and this is mechanical rather than statistical. The fix perturbs only FOUR
of 23 runs (section 3). In one of those four - south/half/404 - the last victim
first entered a searcher's box at step 55, THIRTY-TWO STEPS BEFORE the pin began
at 87; no fix to the pin can move a sighting that already happened. In the other
three the trajectories differ for 155-178 steps and the last-victim sighting
still lands on the same step. With four perturbed runs there is no power to move
a between-run correlation.

WHAT THIS SAYS ABOUT THE DIAGNOSIS'S CLAIM. The diagnosis was explicit that its
correlation might be a COMMON CAUSE rather than a cost: "a searcher pinned near
the rim both accumulates exposure and is slow to reach the far corner, and this
corpus cannot separate those." This round is the first evidence that bears on
that question directly - a within-seed intervention rather than a between-run
association - AND IT IS CONSISTENT WITH THE COMMON-CAUSE READING. Removing the
pins did not make the last victim be seen any sooner.
THE ROUND THEREFORE DOES NOT CLAIM THE COST IS RECOVERED.

THE OTHER PRE-REGISTERED COVERAGE MEASURE, on the four perturbed runs only:
    run                mean inst. coverage        searcher footprint
    south/half/404      0.19181 -> 0.18018 (-)     346 -> 314  (-32)
    east/default/303    0.09129 -> 0.09417 (+)     183 -> 211  (+28)
    east/default/101    0.09489 -> 0.09889 (+)     159 -> 184  (+25)
    east/half/909       0.18286 -> 0.20307 (+)     350 -> 357   (+7)
    MEAN                0.14021 -> 0.14408 (+)     259 -> 266   (+7)
  Three of four improve, one worsens, n = 4. Directionally positive and NOT
  significant. The only part that is mechanically certain is that a pinned step
  contributes zero new coverage by construction, which is now 82 steps of
  recovered search effort - but "recovered effort" is not the same claim as
  "better outcome", and the outcomes did not move.

10. ERRORS I MADE THIS ROUND, AND WHAT THEY COST
================================================================================
 1. THE TEST-CONSTANT RENAME. I changed only the DEFINITION line of the
    NORTH/SOUTH constants, with a comment claiming the assertions were on the
    integers and so unchanged in meaning. FALSE - they use the NAMES. Six tests
    broke. Fixed by swapping the definition and every use site together, so each
    assertion still pins the integer it always pinned; the comment was corrected.
    Cost: caught immediately by the targeted run, zero impact on results.
 2. THE LABEL HOOK. Section 5. I pre-registered an instrument that does not
    survive the gate's double invocation, and I reported one number from it
    before noticing. Withdrawn and replaced with positional attribution.
 3. THE PYTEST KILL. Two pytest wrappers ran concurrently; I killed one PID
    believing it was the redundant run, and the OTHER wrapper exited 127 at
    about the same moment. A fork failure under memory pressure is the likely
    cause - free physical memory was 3.5 GB with ten simulations running, and
    this project's own pool header records exactly that failure mode - but I
    could not reconstruct with certainty which OS PID belonged to which wrapper,
    so I do not claim the kill was harmless. Cost: none to the gate; the
    surviving run delivered a clean, provenance-checked baseline and the run
    that was lost was the duplicate.

11. LIMITS - what this round does NOT establish
================================================================================
 1. THE FIX'S BENEFIT. Detection timing did not move, and coverage moved only
    directionally on n = 4. The round establishes that the defect is gone and
    that nothing got worse. It does NOT establish that removing it helps.
 2. n = 4. Only four runs in 23 contain a pin at this commit. Every statement
    about what the fix DOES rests on four runs; every statement about what it
    does NOT break rests on all 23.
 3. WEST AND NORTH WINDS remain unmeasured, in this round and in the whole
    corpus. Six of the ten original pins were on the east edge.
 4. THE UAV-BLOCKED MODE (the berth jam) is untouched by design and remains the
    larger dwell population: 72 searcher runs and 115 tracker runs at switch-ON,
    against zero off-grid. If anyone wants fewer stuck UAVs, that is where they
    now are.
 5. TWO PRE-EXISTING DEFECTS were found and deliberately NOT fixed, per the
    maintainer's ruling that closing that class is its own scoped round:
    _hazard_retreat_range carries the int(0.5) == 0 bug (--set ...=0.5 silently
    selects range 0, the WORST measured setting at 3.45% against the shipped
    2.59%), and _direction_in_bounds has a dimension-swapped fallback (it
    compares x against WIDTH on a MultiGrid(HEIGHT, WIDTH) grid; dead on a real
    model and inert while the grid is square). With the mission gate and
    BASE_STATION_FIREPROOF, int(0.5) is now the THIRD confirmed instance, and
    the fireproof report already names eleven base-station accessors and five
    fire-mechanic ones with the same bare-cast shape.
 6. _exploration_fallback (uav_executor.py:4450) returns the previous
    selected_dir UNVALIDATED when starved. It is the ORIGIN of the off-grid
    direction and a third unguarded return. It is not guarded here because it is
    reached by all roles and would change tracker behaviour; recorded as a
    candidate for its own round.
