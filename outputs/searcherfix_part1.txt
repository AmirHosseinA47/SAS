2. THE MECHANISM, RE-DERIVED - AND IT IS SHARPER THAN THE DIAGNOSIS STATES
================================================================================

The diagnosis says "the hazard geometry does not change from step to step, so
the same illegal direction is re-emitted every step". That is true but it is not
the whole mechanism, and the difference matters for the fix.

2.1 THE MARGIN-3 FILTER IS STRUCTURALLY STARVED AT A BOUNDARY
  `_victim_edge_blocked_direction` (2113-2128) blocks a direction whenever
      dist_before <= 3  AND  dist_after <= dist_before
  where dist is min(x, y, x_max-x, y_max-y). Inside the 4-cell boundary band
  this admits ONLY a strictly-inward move.
  Replicated exactly in a scratch script on a 50x50 grid, at the ten recorded
  pin cells:

      cell      survivors of the edge filter   in-bounds dirs   off-grid dirs
      (27, 0)   N                              E,W,N            S
      (49,12)   W                              S,W,N            E
      (49,24)   W                              S,W,N            E
      (39,49)   S                              E,S,W            N
      ( 0, 0)   NONE                           E,N              S,W
      (49,42)   W                              S,W,N            E
      (49,38)   W                              S,W,N            E
      (23, 0)   N                              E,W,N            S
      (49, 3)   W                              S,W,N            E
      (49, 1)   W                              S,W,N            E

  NINE of the ten leave exactly ONE candidate. The corner (0,0) leaves ZERO -
  `_retreat_to_safe_interior_direction` is STRUCTURALLY INCAPABLE of returning
  anything there, whatever the fire is doing, because a 4-connected move cannot
  increase the minimum boundary distance from a corner. The same total dead zone
  covers every cell where min(x, x_max-x) == min(y, y_max-y) <= 3, i.e. all four
  corners and the near-corner diagonal cells (1,1), (2,2), (3,3) and mirrors.
  Verified: (3,3) -> NONE; (4,4) -> all four survive.

  THE CONSEQUENCE THAT DRIVES THE DESIGN. A pin does not require "every
  neighbour burning". It requires only that the ONE admitted candidate is
  hazardous - or, at a corner, nothing at all. AND: any fix that reuses the
  retreat, or the :2374 ranking loop, will find NOTHING at exactly the moment
  the guard is needed, because both apply this same filter. The guard must use
  a legality-only candidate test that ignores the margin-3 filter.

2.2 THE CLOSED LATCH - why the SAME direction re-fires, byte for byte
  Traced in source and confirmed end to end:

   (1) uav_executor.py:344 (and :912)
         chosen_dir = int(getattr(agent, "selected_dir", 0))
       chosen_dir is initialised from the agent's PREVIOUS direction.
   (2) :949 `_choose_best_direction` filters candidates on `_direction_in_bounds`
       (:4275), wind-blocked, lookahead and hazard level. At a hazard-surrounded
       boundary cell every candidate is filtered out.
   (3) :4364 falls through to `_exploration_fallback`, whose own filters
       (:4388 bounds, :4392 `_victim_search_cell_is_safe`) also reject
       everything, leaving `scored` empty - and then:
         uav_executor.py:4450    return int(getattr(agent, "selected_dir", 0))
       THE PREVIOUS DIRECTION, RETURNED UNVALIDATED. This is the origin of the
       off-grid `chosen_dir`, and it is a THIRD unvalidated return the diagnosis
       does not name.
   (4) the gate's retreat returns None, so SITE A / SITE B hand that direction
       straight back.
   (5) uav_executor.py:436 -> :144
         agent.selected_dir = int(chosen_dir)
       with no bounds test of any kind.
   (6) agents.py:852 refuses it; the position is unchanged; go to (1).

  A CLOSED FOUR-NODE LATCH. This is why the recorded direction is IDENTICAL on
  every step of a pin (south on every step 86-112 at (27,0)) - pure geometry
  would permit the planner to re-pick; the latch guarantees it cannot.

  WHY THIS MATTERS FOR THE FIX. The latch is broken at exactly one point by the
  guard: once a LEGAL direction is committed at (5), the same starvation next
  step re-emits THAT LEGAL direction. Repetition of a legal direction is
  straight-line motion, not alternation. The mechanism that makes the bug a
  livelock is the same mechanism that makes the guarded behaviour a straight
  line. This is the strongest single argument for the guard - see section 4 for
  its precise limits, which the adversarial pass narrowed.

2.3 WHY IT IS SEARCHER-ONLY - a structural proof, not the measured zero
  uav_executor.py:416-434 is a three-armed dispatch:
      :417  if base_return_leg (action == "rtb_waypoint"):  pass
      :419  elif victim searcher and action != "hold":      -> the gate (:425)
      :428  elif action != "hold" and not (hold_escape & fire_tracker):
                                                            -> _apply_final_direction_safety
  `_apply_final_direction_safety` (1832) -> `_safe_direction_or_escape`
  (1811-1830) ranks ALL FOUR directions with NO `continue` filters:
      `_direction_danger_level` (1731-1739) returns 3 for OUT-OF-BOUNDS,
      2 high fire, 1 smoke, 0 clear;
      the loop takes the strict minimum level, tie-broken by
      `_direction_escape_score` (1772).
  An off-grid direction scores 3 and is strictly worse than any in-bounds one,
  so it can be returned only if all four are off-grid - impossible on a grid of
  side >= 2. THAT HELPER CANNOT BE STARVED. This is the code-level reason for
  the diagnosis's measured "zero off-grid refusals in 23,422 tracker-steps".

  CORRECTION THE ADVERSARIAL PASS FORCED, and it narrows the claim:
  the dispatch has a THIRD arm and a hole. `rtb_waypoint` (:417-418) takes
  NEITHER the gate NOR `_apply_final_direction_safety`, for EITHER role; and an
  action of literally `"hold"` fails both :419 and :428, so it also takes
  neither. So the correct statement is:
      ON THE ORDINARY (non-return, non-hold) PATH, THE VICTIM SEARCHER IS THE
      ONLY ROLE WHOSE COMMITTED DIRECTION IS NEVER BOUNDS-VALIDATED.
  The `rtb_waypoint` and `hold` arms are unvalidated for every role. They do not
  bite today (their producers bounds-filter, and the measured off-grid refusals
  are 657/657 searchers carrying the gate's label), but they are real and are
  recorded here as OUT OF SCOPE for this round - see section 8.

3. REQUIREMENT 2 - THE GUARD. WHAT IT RETURNS, AND WHY
================================================================================

3.1 THE DECISION
  SHAPE (a): THE BEST LEGAL ALTERNATIVE, BY A RULE THAT ADDS NO PREFERENCE.
  Specifically: substitute the REVERSE of the offending direction,
  `(chosen_dir + 2) % 4`, using the file's existing bounds idiom.
  NOT a hold. NOT a hazard-scored pick.

3.2 WHY NOT A HOLD
  A hold is not available as a distinct action. agents.py:829-832 states it:
  "there is no 'stay' action anywhere in the model - all four selected_dir
  values are moves, and even the executor's own hold commits a direction and
  walks the agent off the pad." The only way to express a hold is to emit a
  direction that `move()` refuses - WHICH IS THE BUG. A hold would be
  positionally identical to the livelock, would preserve the zero-coverage step
  the round exists to remove, and would leave `selected_dir` stale (feeding the
  latch at 2.2 and corrupting `uav_last_failed_dir`). Rejected.

3.3 WHY THE REVERSE, AND WHY IT NEEDS NO SCORE
  A PROOF, not a preference. The guard fires only when the cell in direction d
  is out of bounds. That can happen only when the offending coordinate is at an
  extreme (0 or max) and d points outward. Moving by the negation takes that
  coordinate to 1 or max-1 - inside - with the other coordinate unchanged and
  already in bounds. Therefore:
      `(chosen_dir + 2) % 4` IS ALWAYS IN BOUNDS, AND ALWAYS STRICTLY INCREASES
      THE DISTANCE TO THE BOUNDARY THE UAV IS PRESSED AGAINST.
  This holds at corners too (it fixes one axis; a second firing fixes the other).
  Consequences:
    - no candidate scan that can come back empty - unlike every existing helper
      in the gate, which is exactly what starved at the boundary (2.1);
    - no tie to break, so no score that can flip between steps;
    - NO PREFERENCE OF ANY KIND IS ADDED. It is not local hazard avoidance
      (rejected #1): it reads no fire or smoke state. It is not a scorched
      penalty (rejected #2): it reads no ground history. It does not touch
      VICTIM_SEARCHER_HAZARD_RETREAT_RANGE, which stays at 99 (rejected #3).
  Worked example: (27,0), chosen_dir 1 (south, off-grid) -> 3 (north) -> (27,1).

3.4 THE DELIBERATE DEPARTURE FROM THE DIAGNOSIS - stated plainly
  The diagnosis's scoped rule is "substitute any legal in-bounds direction;
  prefer one onto non-burning ground when one exists", citing 65.7% of pinned
  steps having such an option.
  THAT PREFERENCE HAS ALMOST NOWHERE TO ACT, and where it acts it costs the
  proof. At every NON-CORNER boundary cell the strictly-inward direction is
  UNIQUE and equals `(chosen_dir+2)%4` (table in 2.1). So "prefer non-burning"
  can only be exercised by declining the inward move and stepping ALONG the
  edge - keeping the searcher on the outermost ring, where the guard can fire
  again next step and where the retreat stays starved. The preference buys one
  cleaner cell and sells the monotone property that makes the guard analysable.
  DECISION: buy the proof, not the preference, at the default rung. The cost is
  bounded and disclosed: UAVs are fire-immune (`_check_fire_casualties`,
  wildfire_model.py, has no UAV branch), and the status quo is standing on that
  cell for up to 26 steps with 92 of 160 pinned steps already on burning ground.
  The worst case is ONE extra burning step per firing, against a dwell the fix
  removes outright.
  BUT THE ALTERNATIVE IS NOT DISCARDED - it is made a measurable rung. See 3.6.

3.5 WHAT "LEGAL" MEANS, PRECISELY: IN-BOUNDS ONLY
  Not in-bounds AND unoccupied. Reasons:
    - there is no executor-side equivalent of `not_UAV_adjacent`. Verified: it
      is defined only at agents.py:393-399 and has no counterpart under
      src_extension/. Honouring the second conjunct of agents.py:852 needs new
      code reading `model.grid.get_cell_list_contents`;
    - occupancy within a step is order-dependent, and reading other UAVs'
      committed positions mid-step is a determinism hazard this round should not
      introduce;
    - the UAV-blocked mode is the BERTH JAM (diagnosis section 3.5), which
      affects trackers MORE than searchers and is explicitly a separate problem.
      Extending the guard to occupancy would widen this round into it.
  THE HONEST CONSEQUENCE, and the adversarial pass is right to insist on it:
      the guard guarantees the DIRECTION is legal; it does NOT guarantee the
      MOVE succeeds. If the substituted inward cell is occupied, agents.py:852
      still refuses, and the searcher still does not move - the same
      non-progress signature with the refusal reason changed from out-of-bounds
      to occupancy.
  This is why section 7 pre-registers a NEW-MODE gate item: searcher UAV-BLOCKED
  runs / burning-steps (13 / 68 today) must not rise. Off-grid refusals -> 0 is
  guaranteed by construction; pins -> 0 is guaranteed under the diagnosis's own
  pin definition (which requires an off-grid refusal); converting an off-grid
  pin into an occupancy pin would be a FAILURE and the gate must be able to see
  it.

3.6 THE RULE, STATED SO IT CAN BE IMPLEMENTED WITHOUT FURTHER DECISIONS

  New private helper on UAVExecutor:

      def _offgrid_guard_direction(self, agent: Any, direction: int) -> int:
          """Legality guard for the hazard gate's two unvalidated fall-throughs.
          Returns `direction` unchanged unless it leaves the grid."""
          level = self._offgrid_guard_level()
          if level <= 0:
              return direction                      # OFF: exact pass-through
          if not self._can_check_bounds(agent):
              return direction                      # cannot test: unchanged
          if self._direction_in_bounds(agent, direction):
              return direction                      # already legal: unchanged
          if level >= 2:
              return self._first_boundary_safe_direction(agent, direction)
          return (int(direction) + 2) % 4           # rung 1: the reverse

  and, at the two sites, behind the switch:

      :2321   return self._offgrid_guard_direction(agent, chosen_dir), <label>
      :2402   return self._offgrid_guard_direction(agent, best_dir),   <label>

  NOTES ON EACH PIECE, all forced by findings above:
   - `_can_check_bounds` (4730-4739) is REQUIRED. `_direction_in_bounds`
     (4770-4787) FAILS PERMISSIVE - it returns True when `pos` is None (4772)
     or the model is None (4777) - which is exactly why the executor's own
     idiom wraps it (see :1732-1733, :4720-4721). Calling it bare would make
     "the substitute is guaranteed legal" false in the degenerate case.
   - `_direction_in_bounds` is preferred over `_cell_in_bounds` because it
     resolves the model via `_resolve_model(agent)` (4776) rather than reading
     `self._model` directly (1702). ITS FALLBACK IS DEFECTIVE - see 8.2 - but
     that fallback is dead on a real model, where `grid.out_of_bounds` is
     callable and is the SAME predicate agents.py:852 uses.
   - rung 2 reuses `_first_boundary_safe_direction` (4719-4725), which already
     exists, is already applied by `_direction_toward` (4717), and scans
     (chosen, chosen+1, chosen+3, chosen+2) - the REVERSAL LAST. It is the
     file's own bounds-substitution idiom. Using it makes the fix a CONSISTENCY
     fix rather than a new rule.
   - the guard is idempotent (a legal direction passes through), which 2.4
     requires because the gate can run twice in a step.
   - it draws from no RNG and writes no state.

3.7 THE EAST-BIAS CONCERN, downgraded on evidence
  The diagnosis flags direction 0 (EAST) winning ties on first-wins ordering at
  :2285-2288 as a "plausible contributing mechanism, not proven". The
  adversarial pass narrowed it correctly: first-wins arbitrates only on an EXACT
  tie, and at retreat range 99 the score is `_min_strict_hazard_distance * 5.0`,
  which ties across candidates only when the hazard set is EMPTY
  (`_min_strict_hazard_distance` returns the constant 99.0 only at :2225-2226,
  `if not hazards`). At the recorded pins fire is adjacent, so an east-first
  scan would usually NOT decide. The concern is real but weak.
  THE RECOMMENDED RULE IS IMMUNE TO IT EITHER WAY: rung 1 has no scan and no
  tie; rung 2's scan is anchored on `chosen_dir`, not on absolute east.

4.1 WHAT THE TWO PRIOR ROUNDS ACTUALLY FOUND (verified, not paraphrased)
  _move_toward round (outputs/moveoscillation_part1.txt): 0 of 144 guard
  counterfactuals strictly better, 75 equal-or-worse, 62/62 episodes already
  repaired by the board or by `_survival_move`. Reversals near a barrier are
  usually the code's own improving pick.
  last_cell round (outputs/lastcell_part1.txt, lastcell_report.txt): the guard
  is LOAD-BEARING (~91% of its firings cost nothing); the defect was its
  UNCONDITIONAL application; the fix was a structural-redundancy gate, not a
  tuned threshold. Its recorded conclusion: "THE GUARD IS NOT THE PROBLEM. ITS
  UNCONDITIONAL APPLICATION IS."
  BOTH ROUNDS ARGUE AGAINST ADDING STATEFUL ANTI-OSCILLATION HERE.

4.2 WHAT THE GUARD PROVES BY ITSELF - stated precisely, after refutation
  CLAIM AS ORIGINALLY DRAFTED: "the guard is acyclic".
  THAT CLAIM DOES NOT SURVIVE COMPOSITION AND IS WITHDRAWN. The correct,
  defensible property is narrower:
      NO TWO CONSECUTIVE GUARD-EMITTED MOVES CAN REVISIT A CELL, because the
      reverse of an off-grid direction strictly increases the offending
      coordinate and the guard NEVER emits an outward direction.
  That is a genuine and useful property. It is NOT an anti-oscillation proof for
  the gate as a whole, and it is not presented as one.

4.3 THE ONE OSCILLATION SHAPE THAT IS REACHABLE - named, not hidden
  The guard pushes the searcher inward off the edge. At the inward cell the
  guard DOES NOT FIRE, because the upstream direction there is in bounds. The
  pre-existing, UNGUARDED, in-bounds fall-through at :2321 can then hand back a
  direction pointing back out - which is exactly how the worked-example searcher
  reached (27,0) from (27,1) at step 85 in the first place. Result: a two-cell
  alternation (edge cell <-> inward cell).
  A MEMORY INSIDE THE GUARD CANNOT PREVENT THIS, because the guard does not fire
  on the return leg at all. State here would be cost with no benefit - precisely
  the shape the last_cell round condemned.
  A SECOND SHAPE, from 3.5: if the substituted inward cell is occupied, the move
  is refused and the searcher repeats in place - non-progress with the refusal
  reason changed.

4.4 WHY NO STATE IS ADDED, AND WHAT CATCHES THE ALTERNATION INSTEAD
  Three existing mechanisms, all verified in source this round:
   (a) `sweep_no_move_streak` - local_adaptation_generator.py:1333-1337,
       incremented when the committed position is unchanged, RESET ON ANY
       MOVEMENT; escape at :1369 when it reaches WIND_SWEEP_NO_MOVE_ESCAPE = 5
       (:78). This is the FAST immobility escape.
   (b) `pocket_streak` - counts steps inside a +/-2 anchor box and does NOT
       reset on movement; at WIND_POCKET_CAMP_THRESHOLD it drives :2332-2334.
       This is the SLOW one, and it is the one that survives a 2-cycle.
   (c) `compute_oscillation_score` - src_extension/knowledge/
       local_path_context_model.py:68-91 - a LIVE, IN-SIMULATION detector
       scoring BOTH direction reversals at lag 2 and positional ping-pong at
       lag 2.
  THE HONEST ACCOUNTING, and this is a real behavioural consequence of the fix
  that must be disclosed rather than argued away:
    - today's FREEZE scores 0 on (c) - positions are all equal so the ping-pong
      term's `last_positions[index] != last_positions[index-1]` is False, and a
      constant direction has no opposite pair. A 2-cycle scores toward 1.0.
      SO THE FIX DOES NOT MERELY CHANGE AN OFFLINE SIGNATURE: IT TURNS ON A LIVE
      INPUT THE MODEL CURRENTLY CANNOT SEE. That is arguably the point - the
      model can finally perceive the problem - but it is a behaviour change and
      it is pre-registered for measurement in 7.4.
    - the fix DISARMS (a) on formerly-pinned steps, because the UAV now moves.
      It is the faster escape, and losing it is a genuine cost. (b) survives a
      2-cycle and is unaffected.
    - `_uav_stuck_counts` is likewise disarmed while the UAV moves. It has FOUR
      consumers, not two (uav_executor.py:583, :1076; wildfire_model.py:1737,
      :1840 -> LocalPathContextModel -> the UAV_STUCK trigger in
      local_uav_analyzer.py). Whether any of them changes outcomes is a
      MEASUREMENT question and is pre-registered; "no live repair mechanism is
      lost" is NOT established and is not claimed.
  NET POSITION: the guard's worst case (a 2-cycle) is caught by the same slow
  camping detector that today's freeze trips, at the same rate, AND it newly
  trips a live oscillation score that a freeze does not. It cannot be worse than
  the livelock on this axis. Its expected case resolves in one step.

4.5 HOW OSCILLATION WILL BE MEASURED - pre-registered, definitions fixed now
  Measured on RECORDED POSITIONS, never on intent. Recorded fields (verified in
  outputs/_ffr_harness.py:541-548): `uav_steps[t]` rows are
      [uid, cell, role, selected_dir, battery, base_state]
  SEMANTICS, corrected by the adversarial pass and load-bearing for the
  arithmetic: `selected_dir[t]` IS THE DIRECTION APPLIED IN STEP t (written
  pre-move at uav_executor.py:144, consumed in the same step inside `advance()`).
  An implementer who reads it as "the direction applied next step" will shift the
  pairing by one and compute the wrong firing set.

  FIRING SET (computed on the OFF arm; this is the changed-step set):
      for UAV row j at index t: p = uav_steps[t-1][j][1], d = uav_steps[t][j][3];
      FIRE(t,j) iff role == 'victim_searcher' and p + (_MOVE_X[d], _MOVE_Y[d])
      lies outside [0,HEIGHT) x [0,WIDTH).
      This is exactly the diagnosis's off-grid-refusal counter.
  REVERSAL: on realised positions. delta (0,0) is a HOLD - skipped WITHOUT
      clearing the previous direction; otherwise m_t is the index whose
      (_MOVE_X, _MOVE_Y) equals delta; REVERSAL iff (m_prev + 2) % 4 == m_t.
  TIGHT REVERSAL: additionally requires strict step adjacency,
      pos[t-1] == pos[t+1] != pos[t] with consecutive indices.
      Both "any gap" and "tight" are reported, per arm.
  LONGEST ALTERNATION: over each UAV's position sequence, the maximal run
      [i..j] with len(set(run)) <= 2 and at least 2 position changes inside it.
      Reported as a LENGTH (count and max), per arm, and separately for runs
      containing at least one CHANGED step.
  ATTRIBUTION IS MANDATORY: every NEW tight reversal must be opened and each of
      its two legs attributed by action label and by membership in FIRE. The
      last_cell round's entire +29 turned out to be one unit on an untouched
      path. A reversal counts against this fix only if at least one leg is a
      guard-substituted step.
  AGAINST THE TARGET, not merely against position: using
      `partition_steps[t]['targets'][uid]`, for every dwell/alternation run ask
      whether Manhattan distance to the hooked target ever strictly decreased.
      The figure to beat is the diagnosis's 94/94 never-decreased. A two-cell
      alternation with the same property is NOT a fix and the gate must fail.
  CAVEAT: `uav_steps` has no pre-step-1 frame, so step 1 is not evaluable for an
      intent or a reversal.

5.1 WHAT THE route_blocked LATCH ACTUALLY WAS (70e1b33, then 4795708)
  `ff_marker.status = "route_blocked"` is a PERSISTENT STATUS FIELD on a
  firefighter marker, read by dispatch gates as a property of the unit alone,
  whose only in-run clear path required a dispatch that the flag itself refused.
  A circular dependency: correctly released, permanently undispatchable. Fixed
  by an external release pass and then ROUTE_BLOCK_STALE_CLEAR = 1.
  THE SHAPE TO LOOK FOR: (i) a persistent field, (ii) that gates future
  eligibility, (iii) whose clear path is blocked by the field itself.

5.2 THE SEARCHER PATH HAS NO SUCH FIELD - enumerated
  Every piece of state a direction or action label feeds:
    `uav_last_failed_dir`     wildfire_model.py:1092-1095 - SET when the cell is
        unchanged, POPPED when it changes. Self-clearing on movement, which is
        the EXACT OPPOSITE of the route_blocked shape. Its only consumer is a
        -6.0 score penalty and a filter (`_is_failed_direction`, :4461).
        NOTE: during a pin that penalty is never even reached, because
        `_exploration_fallback`'s `scored` list is empty - the one mechanism
        that could have broken the latch is bypassed exactly when it is needed.
    `uav_visit_counts`        wildfire_model.py:1090 - increments the pinned
        cell every step, inflating `_visit_penalty` there. With the guard the
        visits spread. Benign; a real behavioural difference; recorded.
    `_uav_stuck_counts`       wildfire_model.py:3134-3158 - increments on a
        refused move, RESET TO 0 on a successful one. Self-clearing.
    `sweep_no_move_streak`    reset on any movement. Self-clearing.
    `pocket_streak`           does not reset on movement; expires by its own
        threshold logic. Not eligibility-gating.
    `saturated_until` / target blacklists - both expire on a cooldown
        (WIND_SATURATE_COOLDOWN_STEPS = 12, WIND_TARGET_BLACKLIST_COOLDOWN = 15).
    `_victim_escape_memory`   a committed escape direction + step counter,
        cleared when distance to target improves by 3 (uav_executor.py:974-987).
  ONE CORRECTION THE ADVERSARIAL PASS FORCED, accepted:
    `hazard_buffer_level` IS persistent with a SELF-EXEMPTING clear path -
    local_adaptation_generator.py:1392-1393 resets it only `if ... < 2`, and 2
    is exactly the value :2333 writes. So the blanket "no persistent field" is
    wrong. But it does not gate DISPATCH eligibility (searchers have no dispatch
    machinery); it permanently loosens the retreat buffer. It is therefore NOT
    the route_blocked shape, and the claim is narrowed to:
        NO STATE ON THE SEARCHER PATH GATES FUTURE DISPATCH ELIGIBILITY.

5.3 CAN THE GUARD LEAVE A SEARCHER WORSE OFF THAN THE LIVELOCK DID?
  ANSWER: NO, on every axis this round could enumerate - with two disclosed
  costs and one residual that Part 3 must measure.
   - It cannot create an unescapable state. Under the livelock the searcher ran
     its planner every step and escaped when the fire cleared (step 113, via
     `victim_search_escape_bfs`, which is exempt from the gate at :424). The
     guard does not touch the planner, the target hook, dispatch eligibility, or
     any clearing condition. Every escape route open today stays open.
   - Its worst case (a 2-cycle) trips the same slow camping detector the freeze
     trips, and additionally trips a live oscillation score the freeze does not
     (4.4). It is strictly more visible to the model, not less.
   - The guard destroys its own firing precondition ON ANY ACCEPTED MOVE. On an
     occupancy refusal it repeats - equivalent to today, not worse.
  COST 1 - BATTERY, quantified and corrected.
     agents.py:369-375: every step -0.1; if moved, a further -0.2. So a refused
     step costs 0.1 and a successful one 0.3.
     The worked example checks out exactly: 26 refused steps x 0.1 = 2.6,
     matching the recorded 74.2 -> 71.6.
     THE BOUND MUST BE TAKEN OVER REFUSALS, NOT PINS - the guard converts EVERY
     out-of-bounds refusal, not only the long same-cell ones. Using the
     diagnosis's own counts: 113 off-grid refusals at dcD -> at most
     113 x 0.2 = 22.6 extra battery points across that arm; 657 across the wider
     167-record population -> at most 131.4. PER-UAV maximum is NOT established
     and must be MEASURED, not asserted. Thresholds at risk: low 30.0, critical
     15.0 (agents.py:286-287). At BASE_STATION_MODE 3 a depot recharges at
     5.0/step, so a UAV that returns recovers quickly.
     This is a real cost of the fix and is pre-registered in 7.8.
  COST 2 - the loss of the fast `sweep_no_move_streak` escape on formerly-pinned
     steps (4.4). Pre-registered.
  RESIDUAL - the UAV_STUCK trigger and its three other consumers change state.
     Whether that changes any outcome is a measurement question, pre-registered.

6.3 THE ACCESSOR - ONLY AN EXACT INTEGRAL ZERO DISABLES
  The recurring defect this must not repeat: the fire mechanic's mission gate

  with its stated default, via `int(0.5) == 0`. The canonical correct pattern is
  `base_station_fireproof()` at agents.py:1064-1086. Copy its shape exactly:

      def _offgrid_guard_level(self) -> int:
          """VICTIM_SEARCHER_HAZARD_GATE_BOUNDS_FIX, read at call time from the
          cfv MODULE so a per-run override through apply_scenario_config is
          visible - the same contract as _hazard_retreat_range (:2098-2111).

          --set ...=0.5 arrives as the FLOAT 0.5, int(0.5) is 0, and the run

          fallback is the SHIPPED value, like every other accessor here, which

8. RISKS, OPEN QUESTIONS AND THINGS DELIBERATELY LEFT OUT

8.1 A PRE-EXISTING int(0.5) DEFECT IN THE VERY FUNCTION THIS ROUND TOUCHES
  `_hazard_retreat_range` (uav_executor.py:2098-2111) is
      max(0, int(getattr(_cfv, "VICTIM_SEARCHER_HAZARD_RETREAT_RANGE", 0)))
  inside a try/except returning 0. Executed:
      0   -> 0 edge-only        0.5  -> 0 edge-only (SILENT)
      '0' -> 0 edge-only        None -> 0 edge-only
      'off' -> 0 edge-only      99   -> 99 armed      '99' -> 99 armed
  So `--set VICTIM_SEARCHER_HAZARD_RETREAT_RANGE=0.5` silently selects range 0,
  which is the WORST setting measured (3.45% against the shipped 2.59%), with
  nothing anywhere saying so. This is a live instance of the exact defect class
  the brief warns about, sitting in the function this round edits. It is NOT
  fixed here - changing it would alter the meaning of an existing switch and
  belongs in its own round - but it is recorded, and the new accessor does not
  repeat it.

8.2 `_direction_in_bounds` HAS A DIMENSION-SWAPPED FALLBACK
  wildfire_model.py:169 builds `MultiGrid(HEIGHT, WIDTH, False)`, and :106-108
  states "x is the HEIGHT axis and y is the WIDTH axis ... getting these the
  wrong way round is silently inert only while the grid is square."
      `_cell_in_bounds` (1713):      0 <= cell[0] < height and cell[1] < width
      `_direction_in_bounds` (4787): 0 <= nx < width  and  ny < height
  The second is TRANSPOSED. Both fallbacks are DEAD on a real model, where
  `grid.out_of_bounds` is callable, and both are inert while WIDTH == HEIGHT ==
  50. This is the dimension-fix round's exact defect class, latent. Recorded;
  the guard uses `_direction_in_bounds` behind `_can_check_bounds` for its
  model-resolution behaviour, and does not depend on the defective fallback.

8.3 A THIRD UNVALIDATED RETURN, DELIBERATELY OUT OF SCOPE
  `_exploration_fallback` (uav_executor.py:4450) returns the agent's previous
  `selected_dir` unvalidated when starved. It is the ORIGIN of the off-grid
  direction (2.2). It is NOT guarded in this round because it is reached by all
  roles through `_choose_best_direction`, so guarding it would change tracker
  behaviour, break the "cannot fire for a tracker" property, and widen the
  value-identity surface far beyond a searcher-only fix. RECORDED AS A CANDIDATE
  FOR ITS OWN ROUND. The gate guard is sufficient because the gate is the last
  thing that touches the direction before it is committed.

8.4 THE `rtb_waypoint` AND `hold` DISPATCH ARMS (2.3) bypass both post-filters
  for every role. Not exercised by the pins; out of scope; recorded.

8.5 WHAT THIS FIX DOES NOT ADDRESS, restated from the diagnosis

  exposure (56 steps), and the depot component (47 steps, already handled by the

8.6 OPEN QUESTIONS THIS ROUND COULD NOT CLOSE
  (i) DOES THE 2-CYCLE ACTUALLY MATERIALISE? It requires the upstream
      `chosen_dir` at the inward cell to point back outward, which comes from
      `_resolve_direction_intent` and cannot be predicted from source. THE
      CHEAPEST BOUND IS OFFLINE AND SHOULD BE RUN BEFORE ANY WAVE: on the
      existing records, for each pin, read `uav_steps[t][j][3]` at the inward
      cell on the step BEFORE the pin began, and count how many of the pins have
      an outward intent there. That converts the risk from speculative to
      measured without a simulation.
  (ii) WEST AND NORTH WINDS ARE UNMEASURED in the whole corpus. Six of the ten
      pins are on the east edge; a west-wind check is genuinely informative.
  (iii) The searcher's gate-reach ratio (2.5) is inherited from a memory note
      and was not re-verified this round. It bounds how much the fix can touch.
