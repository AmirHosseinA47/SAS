THE QUESTION (maintainer)
  7 of the 11 victims the shipped ungated arm lost on the fresh 30 (U30) were still
  being rescued at step 240, and victim deaths FELL 13 -> 12. Is the -8/101 DELAY
  rather than loss?

THE HORIZON: 360 STEPS, with --set BATCH_SIZE=360. Why:
  1. A HARD CAP AT 301 STEPS.
     - WildFireModel.step() calls sys.exit(0) once
       evaluation_timesteps_counter - 1 == BATCH_SIZE (wildfire_model.py:5808).
     - Every harness run passes BATCH_SIZE=300, so a run cannot pass 301 steps. The
       process exits without writing its JSON.
     - BATCH_SIZE is read nowhere else in the simulation. A whole-tree grep finds only
       that exit check, plus an informational "batch_size" field in the dashboard state
       builder.
     - So raising it cannot change steps 1-240. That is CHECKED on every run (prefix
       identity, below), not assumed.
  2. THE WORK LEFT AT STEP 240.
     - For every victim still ASSIGNED at step 240 in the three arms, the remaining
       movement is the assigned unit's manhattan distance to the victim plus the
       victim's distance to the nearest grid edge, where a rescue completes (or, for a
       unit already carrying the victim out, its distance to the edge).
     - The largest remaining work: 45 cells (control, south/half/1048395951 victim_0).
       In the shipped arm the largest is 31; in the positioning-only arm, 33.
     - Units move at most 1 cell per step, and slower when they retreat from fire.
  3. 360 gives 120 extra steps:
       2.7x the largest remaining work anywhere
       3.9x the largest in the shipped arm
     That leaves room for retreats and detours. 300, the longest the default cap allows,
     would leave 60 steps: barely the worst case with no detour.

CHECKS, before any number is read
  P1 PROVENANCE: every run's repo, extra_params, seed, wind, roles and steps == its
     arm's. A mismatch refuses the run.
  P2 PREFIX IDENTITY: each uh run's first 240 steps must equal the recorded 240-step
     ug run of the same arm and tuple (uhC~ugC, uhD~ugD, uhY~ugY), on:
       every per-step series: fire_digests, victim_steps, ff_steps, uav_steps,
         uav_actions, partition_steps
       every event list restricted to step <= 240: assigns, unassigns, completions,
         recycles, firefight_log
     ANY difference means the longer horizon changed something before step 240. That
     is a STOP: report, no reading of outcomes.

THE DECISION RULE (maintainer's, verbatim)
  "if most of the 11 resolve as rescues and the cost falls inside the limit, the -8
   was a horizon artifact -> I will choose (a) and merge; if they do not, the cost is

  READ AS: "most of the 11" = AT LEAST 6 OF THE 11 are rescued by step 360, AND
