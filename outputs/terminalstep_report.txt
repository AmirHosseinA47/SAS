    Note on the steps=150 control. The brief permitted a reduced --steps
    fallback because no combo tested at HEAD still produces a non-terminal
    run (see 4.5). Reducing the budget is a CLI argument, not a code
    change, and it truncates rather than perturbs the run: the loop body,
    the RNG draw order and the terminal_step latch are identical for the
    first 150 steps regardless of budget. Verified rather than assumed -
    the two runs that terminate under steps=150 latch at 130 and 125, the
    exact values they latch at under steps=240:

        steps=240   101:223  202:210  303:130  404:125  505:211
        steps=150   101:-    202:-    303:130  404:125  505:-

    So the steps=150 control is a faithful truncation of the steps=240
    control, not a different simulation.
