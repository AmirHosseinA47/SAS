  THE CRUX, ANSWERED: ANY ENGAGEMENT BEFORE THE MISSION IS DECIDED CAN COST A RESCUE,
  AND NO SPATIAL OR PER-UNIT ARBITRATION PREVENTS IT. A better chooser (c) is a false
  lead. A spatial commitment limit (a) and a reserve (b) do not remove the loss. What
  removes it, by construction, is a commitment limit in TIME: no unit engages until
  every victim is rescued or dead.
    - The loss is a re-timing of the whole rescue chain. It starts in the first 14-16
      steps, when both idle units walk toward the fire before any victim is confirmed.
      There is no systematic latency cost to arbitrate away: calls are 5.4 cells
      CLOSER on average (section 1.4).
    - Measured counterfactuals, dry (fire untouched), 23 runs, rescued (OFF 68):
          only unit 0 engages 68     only unit 1 engages 68
          leash 3 cells       67     leash 8 cells       69     both units 66
      Every one flips individual victims in both directions:
        - one unit instead of two keeps the south/909 loss
        - leash 3 differs from OFF on 5 of the 9 items round 1 changed and adds a loss
      Under common random numbers the full policy's positioning costs 6 rescues
      (72 -> 66) and adds 6 unit deaths. The cost is real on average, and it is not
      bounded by any spatial rule tested.
    - The gate "engage only when no victim is unresolved" gave every victim's final
      status identical to its control on 161 of 161 gated runs (7 arms x 23: f2GD f2GW
      f2GF f2GES, and CRN f2cGW f2cGF f2cGES).
        - It never opened before terminal_step.
        - In the three wave-1 gate arms, unit trajectories were identical to OFF until
          the gate opened, at terminal_step + 1 on every run where it opened.
  SOUTH/404 (the write-caused loss) is the fire's shared random stream, not a
  firebreak acting on the rescue:
    - Ten clears at steps 9-16 delay the burn-out of one cell, (8,35). At the re-roll
      tick that cell is 42 cells from victim_0, whose survival decides the loss, and 13
      from victim_2.
    - The delay shifts the draw stream at tick 84, and the rest of the fire re-rolls.
    - Victim_0 then lives 48 steps longer and holds the last living unit, so victim_2
      is never reached.
    - Closed-loop bisection: all three distinct re-rolled fires that were run lose
      victim_2. The unperturbed fire rescues it.
  EXTINGUISH'S COIN FLIP is the same stream shift. An extinguished cell stops drawing
  two ticks after the write, and the fire outside the physical light cone differs from
  that tick (17 of 18 runs). With per-cell random numbers (CRN) the sign is ONE-SIDED:
  16 up / 0 down / 7 unchanged (pre-registered rule: "H1 + H4").
  WHAT THIS DOES AND DOES NOT CORRECT IN ROUND 1 (section 3.4): a pure draw-stream
  shift, with no write at all, moves final intact by a median 155 cells with NO
  systematic sign, so round 1's per-run consistency counts are weaker evidence than
  they look. But firebreak's ungated stock gain is NOT an artifact: replayed on the
  same fire with pinned draws it is +1,209 of its +1,783 over 17 tuples. Under CRN,
  closed loop, firebreak is instead about 0 - an unresolved disagreement between two
  validated instruments.

  RECOMMENDED DESIGN (for confirmation)
    POLICY  FF_FIREFIGHT_MISSION_GATE = 1: an idle unit may engage only when EVERY
            managed victim's status is "rescued" or "dead". The static audit showed
            the obvious helper (_count_unresolved_victims) is not outcome-neutral
            (section 4.2).
    CONFIG  extinguish + retreat suppression (E=1, K=1), firebreak OFF. It is the
            only configuration whose intact gain holds on BOTH seed sets in BOTH
            instruments under the gate.
    NO completion threshold. Under the gate a preemption cannot happen; ungated, the
            data say it trades a certain dispatch delay for a small fire gain (section 5).
  PRE-REGISTERED FOR PART 3 (projection from the probe arm, strict predicate applied):

    - The gate keeps about TWO THIRDS of the stock effect (+1,908 of +2,867) but only a

  Per victim, first ok assign (n=81), DRY - OFF:

  +4 in the fourth. The loss is not "the unit was far away at the call".

3.2 WHY: THE DRAW STREAM SHIFTS TWO TICKS AFTER THE WRITE (mechanism H1)
  - An extinguished cell still draws on the first fire tick after the write, turns
    burnt there, and stops drawing on the second. From that tick every later cell (uid
    order) reads a different number.
  - Measured on 20 fmES writing runs:
      the stream shifts at that tick on 18/20; the 2 exceptions extinguished a cell
      with 1 fuel left, which burns out on the same tick as in OFF anyway
      on 17 of the 18, the fire already differs OUTSIDE the physical light cone at the
      shift tick (median 61 cells outside, 20 inside)
      one tick earlier, the write's own physical difference was 0-4 cells
  - Instruments were validated before use:
      0 non-ignition interval boundaries off a fire tick in fmOFF (an extinguish ends
      its own cell's interval on the write step by construction: 359 such boundaries in
      fmES, 0 in fmF)
      0 out-of-cone differences before the reconstructed shift
      a negative control with teeth: 22 of 27 runs show below-shift-uid cells one tick
      later
  - FIREBREAK: a never-burned clear keeps drawing. Its stream shifts only when some
    burn-out timing changes: on the 17 fmF runs whose first write is a never-burned
    clear, 36-133 steps after it (median 52), after a median 26 clears. Scorched clears
    shift within 4-5 steps, like extinguish; scorched-first runs are only 3 tuples (the
    median of 19.5 clears pools all 20 fmF writing runs).
  - Before the shift the physics is ONE-SIGNED in every arm: 0 cells burn in an arm
    that do not burn in OFF.
  - SINGLE-WRITE REPLAYS (I2):
      - One extinguish, all later writes removed, moves final intact by a median 125
        cells, against 195 for the whole arm (canonical, 13 runs: 137 vs 133; fresh,
        the 7 runs that extinguish at all: 113 vs 246). The two are not independent:
        the arm contains the same first extinguish and inherits its re-roll
        (Pearson 0.65).
      - With pinned draws the same single extinguish leaves final intact unchanged on
        16/20. It still changes other cells' burn timing on 17/20 (verifier's
        correction); that timing change is what triggers the shift.
  - No per-run feature predicts fmES's sign at corrected significance (14 tested, n=16
    changed runs). An early first extinguish leans up (rho -0.50, p 0.043), but that is
    underpowered, not evidence of unpredictability.
  - H3 as "extinguishing cells that were about to burn out" is refuted:
      46 of the 443 extinguishes whose cell's initial fuel is recoverable - pooling the
      arms in which that cell burns out naturally and is never written - had exactly 1
      fuel left (490 extinguishes in all; narrower recovery rules give 344/36, 394/43)
      16 of the first 20 extinguished cells re-ignited later in OFF; the other 3 with a
      known fate burnt out in the same spell
      69% of extinguishes come after terminal_step, but lateness does not predict the
      sign (AUC 0.47)
  - H2 (physical front redirection) cannot be separated on stock data: a redirected
    cell needs a burn-out timing change, which shifts the stream one tick later.

3.4 HOW MUCH OF A STOCK-MODEL INTACT CHANGE IS PHYSICAL?
  Two same-realisation instruments answer this. Both are validated: the k = 0 replay
  reproduces fmOFF 240/240 on all 20 tuples, with 0 mismatches between the consumed
  values, the regenerated stream and an independently recorded stream (30,688,953
  values checked). Full output: outputs/_fm2_rng_offset.txt.
  (i) A PURE DRAW-STREAM OFFSET (no write at all). From fire tick T, every draw is taken
      k positions later (k > 0) or earlier (k < 0) in the run's own stream. 200 arms:
      T in {30, 60, 90, 93}, k in {+1, -1, +3, -3}, 20 tuples.
      - IT IS ENORMOUS: |intact difference| median 155, mean 206; the SIGNED difference
        has sd 276. The final has_burned symmetric difference has median 284.5 cells.
        Only 10 of 200 arms leave intact unchanged.
      - IT HAS NO DIRECTION. Per (T, k) the mean ranges -49 to +144, and the paired
        contrast d(+|k|) - d(-|k|), which cancels the run's own luck, gives
          T=30 |k|=1  mean  -10.8  p 0.91      T=90 |k|=1  mean   -7.5  p 0.88
          T=30 |k|=3  mean  -76.2  p 0.29      T=93 |k|=1  mean  +58.2  p 0.34
          T=60 |k|=1  mean +115.8  p 0.047
        My working hypothesis - that consecutive uids are neighbours, so an offset
        biases spread along the grid axis - is REFUTED. Two nominal p < 0.05 results
        stand: the T=60 contrast above, and a wind interaction (T=90, c_south - c_east,
        0/10 runs, p 0.002). With 5 contrasts (10 counting the per-wind splits) one
        nominal 0.047 is expected; neither is explained, and both are flagged rather
        than interpreted.
      => A single-draw shift is worth a couple of hundred intact cells of NOISE, with
         no systematic sign.
  (ii) PINNED FULL SCHEDULES (physics only, same fire, same recorded writes). 17 fmF
      tuples with a write; every replay matches the arm's fire 240/240 before pinning.
        stock (the arm)     +1,783        pinned (physics only)   +1,209
        residual            +574 (mean +33.8 per run, 10 up / 6 down / 1 zero)
      The residual is statistically indistinguishable from the pure re-roll baseline of
      the same tuples: difference mean -7.5, p 0.89.
      => FIREBREAK'S UNGATED STOCK GAIN IS MOSTLY PHYSICAL on this realisation, about
         two thirds of it; the rest is the re-roll. It is NOT an artifact.
      The same test on fmES is NOT interpretable: 68% of its recorded extinguishes do
      not land in the pinned fire (an extinguish needs its cell burning at that step),
      against 7.8% of firebreak clears. Extinguish's instrument stays the closed-loop
      CRN arm.
  WHAT THE TWO INSTRUMENTS AGREE AND DISAGREE ON
    - AGREE: stock per-run intact differences carry a noise term of sd about 200-350
      cells. Round 1's per-run "consistency" counts are therefore weaker evidence than
      they look, and a per-run sign is not a reliable read of a physical effect.
    - AGREE: extinguish has a real, one-signed physical effect (CRN 16 up / 0 down).
    - DISAGREE ON FIREBREAK'S SIZE: pinned-on-the-stock-realisation says +1,209 over 17
      tuples (about +71 per run); the CRN closed loop says about 0 (ungated -1 counted,

    - It does not change this round's choice: under the gate, firebreak-bearing
      configurations fail to rise on both samples in BOTH instruments (4.4), while

4.2 THE POLICY: A MISSION GATE, WITH THE STRICT PREDICATE
  RULE
    An idle unit may engage only when every managed victim's status is "rescued" or
    "dead". Before that, _firefight_prepare returns None, which is exactly the
    feature-off path for that unit. After that, every idle unit engages as in round 1.
  WHY NOT THE PROBE'S HELPER (_count_unresolved_victims == 0)
    Static audit: two independent auditors plus reconciliation, all verdict B. Code
    cites are in the audit.
    - HOLDS
        The helper count is absorbing: once 0, it stays 0. Every resolved state keeps a
        sticky flag, and the only writers that clear flags (assign, finalize) refuse
        flagged victims.
        So the RESCUED COUNT is frozen from the moment it opens.
    - FAILS, three paths
        P1  An "unreachable" victim is alive, still flees, and still burns
            (_check_fire_casualties skips only dead/rescued). Fire writes after the gate
            opens can turn unreachable into dead.
        P2  A unit can stay bound to a victim marked unreachable (route_blocked claimants
            are not released). If it dies, the victim is relabelled "confirmed", which
            the evaluation counts as candidate.
        P3  After that relabel the dashboard's all_victims_terminal and terminal_step can
            still change.
    - MEASURED EXPOSURE
        On 4 of the 20 runs where the probe gate opened, it opened at steps 211-216 with
        an unreachable (never-detected) victim still on the grid:
          east/def/101, east/half/707, south/half/606, south/half/1010
        No outcome changed on this sample, but the hole is live in scenario D.
  THE STRICT PREDICATE (the audit's S1) CLOSES P1-P3
    "rescued" and "dead" are both absorbing whatever any unit does:
      rescued  rescued=True, marker off-grid, status guarded
      dead     finalize refuses it, reset is guarded, casualty rewrite is idempotent
    So no evaluated victim status, all_victims_terminal or terminal_step can change
    after it opens.
    COST (measured)
      - It never opens on those same 4 runs. It also never opens on south/half/101,
        where the helper opens at s159 but both units are already dead, nor on
        south/half/202 and south/half/808, where neither predicate opens. On the
        remaining 16 runs the two open at the same step (16 + 4 + 1 + 2 = 23).
      - Work given up: engaged rows 2,751 -> 2,650 (-3.7%) and writes 1,383 -> 1,325
        for EFS; 343 -> 324 extinguishes for ES.
      - Intact effect of dropping the strict-closed runs' post-gate work (the projection
        in section 0 applies it): stock +24 (that work had netted -24); CRN -9. The
        strict-closed set is computed per instrument and is NOT the same runs: 7 in
        stock (4 of them with any work), 5 under CRN (1 with any work).
    The added clause "no firefighter bound" is redundant for victim outcomes (audit).
  WHAT IS STILL NOT NEUTRAL, BY DESIGN
    Firefighter deaths and the fire itself after the gate opens. Those are the
    mechanic's effect, and they are measured (4.4, 4.5).

6.1 THE NEW SWITCH
  common_fixed_variables.py
    FF_FIREFIGHT_MISSION_GATE = 1
  Accessor ff_firefight_mission_gate() in agents.py, read at CALL time like the other
  FF_FIREFIGHT_* accessors.
    1 = the strict gate (4.2)
    0 = round-1 policy: any idle unit, any time
    anything else (junk, None) -> 1
  Why the junk fallback is 1 and not 0:
    - The round-1 rule "fallbacks are the OFF value" exists so a typo cannot ARM the
      feature. NOTE: that rule is written into two comment blocks (agents.py:1060-1063,
      common_fixed_variables.py:188-190) and into the name of an existing test,
      test_every_switch_ships_off_and_every_fallback_is_off. Both comments become
      inaccurate for this family the moment the switch lands, so the change must update
      them and record the exception deliberately.
    - This switch cannot arm anything; the feature still needs EXTINGUISH or FIREBREAK.
    - Its conservative value is 1: a typo can only make the feature more

          dispatch.

      draws extinguish is one-signed (16 up / 0 down).
