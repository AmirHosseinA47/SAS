PAPER FINDINGS (session 3a)
================================================================================
  1. The depot pockets were a CONTROLLER defect, not a depot effect: a safety veto layered on a route planner
     that could not see it produced a livelock. Burnable depots still showed it. A1 removed them: 0 depot-pocket
     oscillations in every arm.
  2. Every result at the session-2 defaults (a5a496db) includes 3 runs that never finish and must not be used.
  3. B2 is the first launch stagger in this project that closes the no-searcher gap without extra returns.
     Confirmed three times: 517 -> 0 (Part 3), 584 -> 0 (round 2), with searcher returns down (1.62-1.75).
     B3 is a safety net: it does not fire on top of B2, and alone it never lets a delayed return arrive below
     LOW.
  4. With free-cell docking, docking and charging are positional, and doorstep standoffs no longer occur.
  5. THE REMAINING NEAR-FIRE OSCILLATION COMES FROM TARGET SELECTION THAT CANNOT SEE REACHABILITY. Round 2
     showed that absorbing the gate's vetoes silently makes the target generator keep choosing unreachable
     targets: more give-ups, later detections, a victim never found. This is session 3b's first design
     constraint (outputs/session3b_first_constraint.txt): 3b's target choice must be REACHABILITY-AWARE, and 3b's
     gates must include runs that never finish and oscillation episodes of every kind.
  6. THE EDGE-COVERAGE QUESTION: the edge filter does not create the hole. The detection radius (8, any UAV)
     reaches the outer ring from the band's inner lane, and the shipped arm observes MORE of the ring than
     a5a496db (94.5% vs 87.2% of ring cells) while its searchers spend less than half as long in it. The
     never-detected victims had FLED into the ring. The B/N 9671 victim started at (40, 25) and reached (49, 20)
     by step ~141; in round 2's B arms it was found at steps 64-68, before it got there. It is a search-order and
     timing question, not a filter defect.
  7. One owner for near-fire steering (round 2) is implementable exactly, with 0 memoryless steps and 0
     edge-filter violations, where Part 2's route broke the edge filter about 300 times per 16 runs. It does not
     remove oscillation on its own: an owner with two objectives livelocks, and a lower layer that absorbs a
     refusal must tell the layer above.

LESSONS (ruled and learned)
  - Session 2's D-7 condition checked deaths and exposure but not livelock or unfinished runs. EVERY MOVEMENT FIX
    IS GATED ON (N) AND (O) AS WELL.
  - A1 removed one veto-against-planner livelock but left the same pattern on the steps the route does not
    control. A GUARD MUST BE PLANNED WITHIN, EVERYWHERE IT ACTS - with one objective, and with the target
    generator told what the guard refuses.
  - A prototype screen must measure the gate it will be judged by. A fix from a review is a new rule and needs
    its own screen.
  - Tests must set their own configuration: two wind-trajectory tests passed or failed by test order until this
    session.
