  otherwise empty AND `current_dist == 0` - as a four-line terminal fallback

  VALUE: the cell being VACATED, written on the line before the move.  The
  semantics are "do not step straight back to where this unit just came
  from".  The memory is ONE MOVE deep - not one step, and not a history.

  LIFETIME: until the next survival-move-driven move overwrites it, or a
  reset clears it.  Reset sites, all five:
    597   `advance` standby branch     requires the unit already required-safe
    761   top of `_survival_move`      requires an ideal standoff
    832   at-cap and a required-safe candidate was taken
    859   post-move, landed on an ideal standoff
    863   post-move, landed required-safe while at cap or stalled, and idle
  plus the 786 re-anchor.  `_revalidate_idle_retreat_stall` deliberately does
  NOT reset it (agents.py:984-988): it clears the stall flag alone, precisely
  to keep the anti-oscillation memory alive on the path 62b4fbe added.

  DOES IT HAVE 62b4fbe's RESET-HOLE PROBLEM?  NO - A DIFFERENT ONE.
  62b4fbe's defect was that `_idle_retreat_stalled` could only be cleared by
  conditions requiring the unit to already be safe - the exact complement of
  the condition that set it - so the flag was a one-way door.  `last_cell` is
  not a one-way door: it is overwritten on every survival move, and 786
  clears it whenever an idle unit is found beyond its leash.  Measured over
  every `_retreat_candidates` call, arm `none` (= stock):

                                             13-run sample   10 fresh seeds
      scans with last_cell set                    284             235
        it IS the true immediate predecessor      224             191
        it is STALE - the unit's last cell change
          came from another mover (`_move_toward`
          never rewrites it)                       60              44
          ...and the stale value is still a free
             neighbour, so it actually bites       10               2
      stale among the section-4 sole-exit events    0 of 26

  Staleness is real (about 21% of scans) but tiny in effect (10 and 2 scans),
  and it is NOT the mechanism under investigation: in all 26 events where the
  guard closed the last door, `last_cell` was the cell the unit had genuinely
  just left.  THE GUARD IS BEING APPLIED CORRECTLY AND IS STILL FATAL.  That
  is the finding, and it is why the fix is a condition and not a reset.

  ONE PERSISTENCE ASYMMETRY WORTH RECORDING.  Because the value is written
  only on a move, a unit that STOPS moving freezes its `last_cell` and keeps
  refusing that one cell for as long as it stands still.  Age in steps
  (steps since the unit last changed cell - an upper bound on the age):

      13-run sample   mean 1.81   >1 step in 78 of 284 scans   max 10+
      10 fresh seeds  mean 1.68   >1 step in 61 of 235 scans   max 9

  A one-move guard becomes an N-step guard exactly when the unit is stuck,
  which is exactly when being stuck is dangerous.  E/default 202 refuses
  (29,49) on steps 213, 214 and 215; S/half 808 refuses (0,21) on 120, 121
  and 122.  A consequence of the design rather than a bug in it, but it is
  why the refusal gets so many chances to be lethal.

PART 1.3 - WHAT THE GUARD IS FOR, AND WHAT IT IS WORTH
================================================================================
DERIVED FROM THE CODE.  The retreat scan is greedy on (fire distance, risk)
over the immediate neighbourhood.  Greedy hill-climbing on a field that moves
under the agent reverses: at A the unit prefers B; once at B, the shifted
fire field can make A preferable again.  With no memory the unit alternates
A,B,A,B forever, never actually retreating, while `_idle_retreat_steps` never
runs the leash out.  Excluding the cell just vacated forces every survival
move to somewhere new, converting an oscillation into a walk.  The window is
one move deep - the minimum that breaks a period-2 cycle and too shallow to
break a period-3 one, consistent with period-2 being what it was written for.

MEASURED, NOT ASSUMED.  Two independent measurements say the guard is
load-bearing rather than decorative:

  (i) It fires constantly and almost always harmlessly.  On the 13-run sample
      the last_cell test removed a neighbour in 157 scans.  In 143 of those,
      other candidates remained - it merely redirected the unit.  In only 14
      did it empty the set.  Fresh seeds: 129 removals, 108 redirects, 21
      empties.  So ~91% / ~84% of the guard's work costs nothing at all.

  (ii) Relaxing it WITHOUT a gate brings the oscillation straight back.
      Arm `c` (section 6) re-admits `last_cell` whenever the set would
      otherwise be empty, at ANY fire distance.  On the 13-run sample that is
      27 re-admissions and 5 of them reverse on the VERY NEXT STEP - a
      textbook A->B->A.  22% of ungated re-admissions immediately become the
      thing the guard exists to prevent.  Tight step-adjacent reversals go
      3 -> 11 and the longest alternating run goes 2 -> 3.

  THE GUARD IS NOT THE PROBLEM.  ITS UNCONDITIONAL APPLICATION IS.

PART 2.5 - THE DESIGN, AND WHY THE GATE IS NOT A TUNED THRESHOLD
================================================================================
THE SHAPE.  Re-admit `last_cell` as a LAST RESORT: only when the candidate
set is otherwise empty, and only when `current_dist == 0`.  Every other
filter - fire and leash - still applies to it, because the re-admission is
expressed as a re-run of the same chain with `last_cell = None`.

WHY cur_dist == 0 IS THE RIGHT CONDITION, FROM THE CODE AND NOT FROM THE
NUMBERS.  This is the crux, so it is argued before any result is quoted.

  `current_dist` is `self._min_fire_distance(cell, fire_cells)` at both call
  sites (agents.py:801 and 970).  `_min_fire_distance` (719-725) is the
  minimum Manhattan distance to a cell in `fire_cells`, and `_fire_cells`
  (615-627) contains exactly the actively burning cells.  So

      current_dist == 0   <=>   the unit's own cell is actively burning.

  Now look at the filter ORDER at 881-886.  The FIRE test is one line ABOVE
  the last_cell test.  So on the NEXT scan, after a unit steps off a burning
  cell, the cell it just vacated is refused by the FIRE filter - a filter
  that outranks the guard and that the guard's absence cannot affect.

  THEREFORE: when cur_dist == 0, the anti-oscillation guard is STRUCTURALLY
  REDUNDANT.  The step back that it exists to forbid is already forbidden, by
  a higher-priority filter, and stays forbidden until that cell burns out.
  Suspending the guard in that case removes nothing, because in that case it
  protects nothing.

  This is why the gate is not a threshold picked to make a number move.  It
  is the exact condition under which the guard cannot do its job anyway.  The
  brief asked whether "oscillation DURING an active fire emergency is
  actually a realistic risk worth guarding against"; the answer is stronger
  than a judgement call - at cur_dist == 0 it is not a risk at all, because
  the fire filter already covers it.

  AND THE PREDICTION IS CONFIRMED.  Of arm `c`'s 27 UNGATED re-admissions,
  the 4 at cur_dist == 0 produced ZERO reversals and the 23 at cur_dist >= 1
  produced ALL 5.  The structural argument and the measurement agree.

WHY (b) AND NOT (a).  Even at cur_dist == 0 the unit can have other free
neighbours - E/half 202 step 60 has two.  Option (a) drops the filter and
lets `last_cell` COMPETE on the greedy score, where it can win and drag the
unit backwards.  Option (b) reaches for it only when nothing else survived,
so wherever a choice exists the preference ordering is byte-for-byte
unchanged.  (b)'s behaviour change is a strict subset of (a)'s, and section 6
shows the difference between them is a firefighter's life.

WHY (b) AND NOT (c) - i.e. WHY NOT ALSO RESCUE THE FOUR UNASSIGN CASES.
Three independent reasons, in decreasing order of force:
  1. At cur_dist >= 1 the structural argument above evaporates: the vacated
     cell is NOT burning, so the fire filter does not block the return, and
     re-admission is exactly the oscillation the guard was written for.
     Measured: 5 immediate A->B->A reversals out of 23 such re-admissions.
  2. The four unassign cases do not need it.  `_move_toward` already moves
     each of those units onto the refused cell in the same step (section 4.3
     / correction 2).  (c) does not save them; on E/half 404 it just makes
     the unit ping-pong (31,38)->(31,39)->(31,38) and die on schedule.
  3. Measured cost: on the 13-run sample (c) loses a rescue and gains a
     firefighter death on S/half 101 - r3/d1/ff1 becomes r2/d1/ff2.

THE CODE.  Four lines and a comment, appended AFTER the existing loop.  No
existing line changes:

    -        return candidates
    +        if not candidates and last_cell is not None and current_dist == 0:
    +            # The unit is standing on a burning cell and every neighbour it
    +            # could still step to was ruled out by the anti-oscillation
    +            # memory.  That memory exists to stop a unit ping-ponging
    +            # between two free cells, and it cannot serve that purpose
    +            # here: the cell being vacated is on fire, so the fire test
    +            # above - which outranks it - refuses the step back anyway
    +            # until that cell burns out.  Re-run the same chain without
    +            # it, so `last_cell` is considered only when nothing else
    +            # survived and every other filter, the leash included, still
    +            # applies to it.
    +            return self._retreat_candidates(
    +                cell, origin, None, fire_cells, current_dist
    +            )
    +        return candidates

  The recursion terminates at depth 2: inside the re-run `last_cell is None`,
  so the condition is False.  Expressing it as a re-run rather than as a
  deferred-entry list is deliberate - it makes "the same chain minus one
  filter" true by construction instead of by a duplicated dict literal that
  could drift from the one above it.

PART 2.6 - CONFIRMING THIS IS ADDITIVE TO 62b4fbe, NOT A RE-OPENING OF IT
================================================================================
The brief asks to stop and report if the fix requires restructuring the
helper 62b4fbe introduced.  It does not.

  * `_retreat_candidates` keeps its signature, so BOTH call sites - the
    normal scan at 802 and `_revalidate_idle_retreat_stall` at 972 - pick the
    behaviour up with no edit at either site.  That is precisely the property
    62b4fbe's extraction was for ("so the two can never drift apart about
    what 'nowhere to go' means"), and this change is consumed through it
    rather than around it.
  * Lines 879-902 are untouched: three filters, same order, same dict.  The
    diff is an insertion before the existing `return`.
  * THE LEASH IS NOT TOUCHED.  The re-run passes the same `origin`, so
    `from_origin > IDLE_RETREAT_MAX_CELLS` still applies to the re-admitted
    cell.  The C3 leash death from idleretreat_report.txt section 4.2 stays
    exactly as it was, by design and not by accident.
  * THE STALL-LATCH RECHECK IS NOT TOUCHED.  `_revalidate_idle_retreat_stall`
    is unchanged; it simply receives a non-empty list in one more situation,
    and its own `_pick_improving_retreat` test still decides whether to move.
  * `_move_toward`'s continuation-after-route_blocked is not touched.
  * `_assigned_one_step_retreat` has no last_cell term at all and is not
    touched.
  * No constant is added or changed.  `current_dist` is already a parameter;
    the condition introduces no new state, no new field and no new threshold.

PART 2.8 - WHAT THE FIX WILL AND WILL NOT DO
================================================================================
WILL
  * Get a unit off a burning cell when the only remaining exit is the cell it
    just left.  4 such events in 23 runs (1 baseline + 3 fresh).
  * Do so without any possibility of ping-pong, for the structural reason in
    section 5, confirmed on the C4 trajectory itself.
  * Leave 12 of the 13 baseline runs bit-identical, which is also why the
    route_blocked gate is expected to hold - but it will be re-run, not
    assumed.

WILL NOT
  * Help the four unassign-inflow cases.  They are at cur_dist == 1 and are
    already moved by `_move_toward` in the same step.  The brief's gate
    offers "the C4 case AND/OR the unassign-inflow cases"; this fix delivers
    the first and explicitly declines the second, with the measured reason
    that the version which reaches them is worse.
  * Help the 11 cur_dist == 1 sole-exit events, or the C3 leash death.
  * Change anything for an enclosed unit - 43 of the 61 empty-candidate scans
    on the baseline sample have zero free neighbours and no filter is
    implicated at all.
  * Guarantee survival.  On the fresh seeds, two of the three cur_dist == 0
    events are units that die in the same step as the refusal; whether a
    single step is enough to save them is what the arm-b fresh run answers.
