0. THE SHORT VERSION
--------------------
  * The three "near edge" gates are all comparisons against _distance_from_boundary (D), which
    returned 0.0 on every real run before e76c806. Two of them (C5 <= 5, C3 < 2) change a LABEL
    and a MODE; one of them (C7 <= 2, inside the searcher hazard gate) replaces the searcher's
    chosen direction with the output of _retreat_to_safe_interior_direction. That replacement is
    a RETREAT (a direction substitution), not a filter, a retarget or a scoring penalty - and
    with D dead its scoring collapsed to "the strictly-safe neighbour furthest from the nearest
    fire/smoke cell, ties by direction index". Section 1.
  * Of the 2,628 interior decisions the fix removes, 1,635 are the C5 flag (a label/mode change
    on the executor-target path), 973 are C7 gate rows - which are 498 UNIQUE searcher steps,
    because the gate runs twice per step - and 20 are C6 re-rankings. At the decision, the
    override moved the searcher AWAY from the nearest fire on 73.5% of those 498 steps (the
    searcher's own choice: 43.6%), reversed the live choice outright on 22%, and made no
    difference to whether the cell it arrived on was burning (2.8% vs 3.0%). 61% of its interior
    firings happened with the nearest fire NINE OR MORE cells away. Section 2.
  * So the accident was not "do not step into fire" - the gate's strict checks still do that,
    identically, on both trees. It was a constant, target-blind repulsion from the fire that kept
    searchers on average 1.7 cells further from it (mean fire distance 12.5 vs 10.8 canonical)
    and out of its 3-cell neighbourhood 30% less often (23.2% vs 30.0% of steps). The exposure
    cost of the fix is that far-field drift disappearing, not a near-field decision going wrong.
    USEFUL BY ACCIDENT, AND FOR A DIFFERENT REASON THAN IT WAS WRITTEN FOR. Section 2.6.
  * Original intent, from the code (there is no history - every line arrived in the import commit
    a4a01b4): the three gates are EDGE handling, and edge-only is what they were written to be.
    Hazard safety for searchers was designed as strict filtering (no fire/smoke on the next cell,
    a 2-cell buffer, a 3-cell lookahead), and that filtering is measured insufficient on its own
    because the searcher is sent DOWNWIND of the fire front - to where the fire is going - so the
    front walks into a searcher that only checks the current fire. Section 3.
  * Replaying the accident's components one at a time on the fixed tree (harness-side dead reads
    per consumer, no source change) against the failing test: the C7 override alone brings
    test_no_crash_zero_victims from 4 strict steps back to the baseline's 1, with the baseline's
    trajectory metrics exactly; the C5 flag and the C3 hold read change nothing (4 -> 4). And the
    override with the LIVE retreat scoring passes the bound but collapses coverage (22 unique
    cells in 50 steps vs 40) - it walks the searcher to the grid centre and parks it. Section 2.5.
  * DESIGN (section 4): one constant, VICTIM_SEARCHER_HAZARD_RETREAT_RANGE (cells; 0 = off = the
    e76c806 edge-only gate; 1..98 = retreat when the nearest fire/smoke is within that range;
    >= 99 = always), read at call time so --set overrides it. When it is on, the retreat scores
    by hazard distance only (the accident's scoring), and edge handling stays with the
    edge-blocked filter and the planner's margins, which are live and already do that job.
    RECOMMENDED: 99 - the accidental behaviour made intentional - as the primary arm, because it
    is the only variant with evidence behind it, and 6 as a measured secondary. Reasons in 4.2.
    Decision rule for Part 3 stated in advance in 4.6.
  * ATTRIBUTION, 78 runs on the canonical 13 (section 4.7): replaying the C7 override with the
    accident's hazard-only retreat scoring on the fixed tree gives 8520706's rescued / dead /
    firefighter deaths on 13 of 13 seeds (35/12/8; the fix's 37/11/8 with its E/505 +2 and E/303
    write-off all revert) and its burning-cell exposure (2.08% vs 2.12%; fix 3.22%), and is
    byte-identical to the held round's P-only ablation on 4 seeds. The gate with LIVE retreat
    scoring recovers only half the exposure (2.54%), loses 4% of covered cells and pulls
    planner-path searchers 5 cells nearer the fire. The always-on flag (C5) re-rolls four seeds
    with exposure 3.39% - no hazard benefit. The hold read (C3) is byte-identical to the fix on
    13/13 - inert. Both re-recordings of base and fix are byte-identical to the held round's
    arms 13/13 (the new per-step label recording is read-only). All three trees spend ~80% of
    searcher steps in the forced-retarget pathfinding mode; the accident's benefit is where the
    searcher stands when each mode runs, not which mode runs.

    def _distance_from_boundary(self, x: int, y: int, model: Any | None) -> float:
        if model is None:
            return 0.0
        x_max = self._grid_dimension(model, ("grid_height", "HEIGHT", "height"))
        y_max = self._grid_dimension(model, ("grid_width", "WIDTH", "width"))

1.5  THE OVERRIDE - _retreat_to_safe_interior_direction (:2178-2225), quoted in full:

    def _retreat_to_safe_interior_direction(self, agent: Any) -> int | None:
        model = self._resolve_model(agent)
        pos = getattr(agent, "pos", None)
        if pos is None:
            return None
        fire_cells = self._collect_strict_active_fire_cells(model)
        smoke_cells = self._collect_strict_smoke_cells(model)
        best_dir: int | None = None
        best_score = -float("inf")
        pos = getattr(agent, "pos", None)
        for direction in range(4):
            if self._victim_wind_blocked_direction(agent, direction):
                continue
            cell = self._next_cell_for_direction(agent, direction)
            if cell is None or self._strict_victim_hazard_level(cell) > 0:
                continue
            buffer = self._hazard_buffer_for_agent(agent)
            if self._cell_within_victim_hazard_buffer(
                cell, fire_cells=fire_cells, smoke_cells=smoke_cells, buffer=buffer,
            ):
                continue
            if not self._strict_path_lookahead_safe(agent, direction):
                continue
            score = self._distance_from_boundary(cell[0], cell[1], model) * 14.0
            hazard_dist = self._min_strict_hazard_distance(cell, fire_cells, smoke_cells)
            score += hazard_dist * 8.0
            if pos is not None:
                curr_dist = self._distance_from_boundary(int(pos[0]), int(pos[1]), model)
                next_dist = self._distance_from_boundary(cell[0], cell[1], model)
                if next_dist > curr_dist:
                    score += 18.0
            if score > best_score:
                best_score = score
                best_dir = direction
        if best_dir is not None:
            return best_dir
        for direction in range(4):
            if self._victim_wind_blocked_direction(agent, direction):
                continue
            cell = self._next_cell_for_direction(agent, direction)
            if cell is None or self._strict_victim_hazard_level(cell) > 0:
                continue
            score = self._distance_from_boundary(cell[0], cell[1], model) * 10.0
            score += self._min_strict_hazard_distance(cell, fire_cells, smoke_cells) * 6.0
            if score > best_score:
                best_score = score
                best_dir = direction
        return best_dir

     What it is: a one-step RETREAT. Candidates are the four neighbours, filtered by the same hard
     rules the gate uses (not edge-blocked, no strict hazard, outside the buffer, 3-cell lookahead
     clean); the survivor with the highest score wins, first-max on ties in order E(0) N(1) W(2)
     S(3); if nothing survives, the same without buffer/lookahead. It never reads the target.
       LIVE scoring   14*D(next) + 8*hazard_dist(next) + 18 if the step increases D;
                      fallback 10*D + 6*hazard_dist.
       DEAD scoring   8*hazard_dist(next); fallback 6*hazard_dist. The +18 never fires (0 > 0).
     Two facts follow. (i) With D dead, the function was a pure fire/smoke repulsion step - the
     "safe interior" half of its name had fallen off. (ii) With D live and the function called on
     EVERY step, the boundary terms dominate (a step changes D by at most 1 = 14 points, the
     inward bonus is 18, a step changes hazard distance by at most 1 = 8 points), so the searcher
     would walk to the grid centre and stay. Both are measured in 2.5.

     hazard_dist is _min_strict_hazard_distance (:2140-2157): manhattan distance to the nearest
     cell of (strict active fire cells U strict smoke cells) - the REAL fire from the schedule's
     Fire agents (is_burning) and the visibility model's smoke_obscured cells - 99 when there is
     none. The searcher has perfect current-fire knowledge; it has no knowledge of where the
     front will be next step.

2.3  THE C5 FLAG (1,635 decisions): what it changed and what it did not
     The flag turns the executor-target path from _choose_best_direction + gate into pathfinding
     with the label retarget_to_interior (1.2). Movement consequence measured directly (2.5):
     none on the failing test (4 strict steps with C5 replayed, 4 without). Its visible
     consequence in the dead world was the LABEL - the downwind wind-aware test asserts the plain
     label in the interior and failed 7/7 for the whole campaign because of C5 (held round 9).
     A second-order consequence worth stating: in the dead world C5 and C7 competed. Every
     executor-target step was pathfound, and a pathfound step skips the gate, so the C7 override
     could only reach the planner-target path (76% of its firings above), the sweep path and
     the pathfinding fallback. With C5 live, more searcher steps reach the gate - which is why
     the always-on replay of C7 on the fixed tree is not the same as 8520706, only its C7
     component (4.7 measures the difference).

2.6  VERDICT ON "USEFUL BY ACCIDENT, OR USEFUL FOR A DIFFERENT REASON"
     Both, in this precise sense:
       - The trigger was written for EDGE proximity (D <= 2) and the action was written as a
         retreat to the safe interior (14*D + 18 inward + 8*hazard). The dead read made the
         trigger unconditional and stripped the action to its hazard half. Neither the author's
         trigger nor the author's action ran; what ran was "on every gated step, if a strictly
         safe neighbour exists, step to the one furthest from any fire or smoke".
       - That behaviour reduced exposure by keeping searchers away from the fire IN GENERAL
         (61% of firings at 9+ cells; mean distance +1.7 cells; the 3-cell neighbourhood entered
         30% less), not by preventing steps into fire at the moment of danger (2.8% vs 3.0% of
         override firings landed on a burning cell either way; within 2 cells of fire it changed
         the next cell's fire distance in 13 of 75 firings). The near-field protection the code
         was designed to give - the strict checks (1), (5), (6), (7) - is the same on both trees.
       - It was target-blind: the searcher's own choice at the gate is a step toward its target
         (that is what _choose_best_direction returns), and on 55% of the ~898 gated steps the
         override substituted a different direction (the 498 interior + 13 band changed steps),
         reversing the choice outright on 22% of those. Coverage in cells did not suffer (2.4),
         because the wind-aware target
         machinery re-targets on same-target streaks (10 steps) and the searcher alternates
         between pathfinding toward a target (flag set, gate skipped) and being repelled from
         the fire (flag clear, gate on). What suffered is time-to-target - the held round's
         attribution in section 7: first sightings barely move, dispatch ORDER moves.
     For the design this means: the thing to restore is a far-field bias away from the fire on
     the gated steps, and the honest name for it is hazard repulsion, not hazard avoidance.

3. WAS EDGE-ONLY THE ORIGINAL INTENT?
-------------------------------------
3.1  History: `git log -S` for each of the seven names (the three gates, the retreat, the helper,
     _grid_dimension, the retarget_to_interior label) returns exactly one commit, a4a01b4
     2026-07-27 "Add core project files and update README". There is no design note, no docs/
     entry (docs/ covers the system boundary and the managing/managed split; grep for
     boundary/near-edge/interior/hazard gate/retreat finds nothing), no README mention, and no
     comment in the executor near any of the seven. The intent has to be read from the code.
3.2  From the code: the three gates test distance-to-edge and nothing else, and their outputs
     are named for the edge: _victim_near_edge_escape_required, near_boundary,
     retarget_to_interior, _retreat_to_safe_INTERIOR_direction with a 14*D + 18-inward scoring.
     The hazard concern has its own, separate machinery in the same gate - strict level, buffer,
     lookahead - and its own label (hazard_retreat). The two concerns are woven into one
     function but the author kept them distinguishable at every line. EDGE SAFETY was the intent
     of the three gates; HAZARD SAFETY was intended as strict filtering. Edge-only is therefore
     exactly what the gates were written to be, and e76c806 gives the author's design its first
     real run.
3.3  From the tests (the only specification that exists), all written against the dead helper
     and passing with it:
       - test_wind_aware_victim_search.py::test_victim_search_wind_aware_action_when_downwind_
         exists: a searcher in the interior with a wind target is labelled the PLAIN
         "victim_search_wind_aware". Interior = no interior retarget. It failed for the whole
         campaign because C5 was always-on and passes now (held round 9). Edge-only for C5 is
         specified.
       - test_victim_searcher_scenario_matrix.py::test_no_crash_zero_victims and
         ::test_scenario_a_no_5x5_camping_east: strict fire/smoke steps <= 2 in 50 steps, plus
         camping/coverage bounds. A HAZARD-EXPOSURE bound on the searcher, with no mechanism
         named. It is the maintainer's only stated requirement on interior hazard safety, and
         its number was calibrated on the accident (it passes at 1 with the override, fails at 4
         without).
       - test_wind_aware_corridor_search.py:289-292 and test_executor_routing_and_burnt.py:89
         accept hazard_retreat and retarget_to_interior as legitimate labels of forced retargets
         and route-around-smoke cases - the strict machinery, not the override.
3.4  Is edge distance a proxy for fire? No, and the geometry says why the strict checks are not
     enough on their own. Ignition is SYSTEM_RANDOM.randint(margin, HEIGHT - margin - 1) per axis
     with margin = 10 (wildfire_model.py:1992-1995) - the interior, never within 10 of an edge.
     Scenario D's victims spawn at (40,25), (25,48), (10,25) and (25,3) on every seed - 9, 1, 10
     and 3 cells from the nearest edge (outputs/_dim_edgeband.txt) - and the firefighters on a
     ring of radius 0.4 x 50 (:590-604). The searcher's wind-aware target is "a safe cell downwind of the
     active fire front" (_compute_wind_aware_search_target, the fire centroid plus the wind
     vector): the searcher is sent to where the fire is GOING, and the fire follows it there. A
     3-cell lookahead against the CURRENT fire cannot see the front arrive, and the front is
     fast: measured on the 13 canonical seeds (burn_intervals, manhattan reach of the burn scar
     from the ignition cell), it is 6-9 cells out by step 10, 22-30 by step 30 and 27-48 by step
     60 - about a cell per step early on, the searcher's own speed - so a searcher that stops
     moving away is overtaken. The accidental bias was a crude, target-blind stand-in for "keep a
     margin from a front that moves", and edge distance never had anything to do with it.
3.5  Answer: the original intent of the three gates was edge safety, and edge-only is correct for
     THAT intent. The original intent for hazard safety was strict avoidance of the current
     fire, which is preserved and measured insufficient. Nothing in the code or tests ever
     specified a far-field margin from the fire; the maintainer's test bound is the closest thing
     to a specification and it encodes the accident's outcome.

4. DESIGN
---------
4.1  Options evaluated
  (a) keep the override always-on, intentionally: remove the D <= 2.0 gate and document the
      retreat as hazard repulsion. TWO readings, only one of which is the accident:
      (a-literal) always-on with the LIVE retreat scoring - measured in 2.5: the +18 inward
        bonus and 14*D dominate 8*hazard, the searcher walks to the centre and parks (22 unique
        cells in 50 steps). REJECTED by measurement; it is also the "constrain movement, lose the
        unit's own good ground" shape of #9-A1.
      (a-replica) always-on with the accident's HAZARD-ONLY scoring (boundary terms dropped, C8
        dropped): reproduces the baseline's test metrics exactly (2.5) and is the only variant
        with full-run evidence (4.7, the C7+C6 arm). The brief's objection - "keeps a gate that
        does nothing" - does not apply to this shape: the edge gate is REMOVED (replaced by the
        unconditional retreat, or by a parameter-dependent branch, 4.3), the retreat's dead
        boundary terms are removed with it, and edge handling is left to the filters that already
        do it live (1.7). No dead code remains; what remains is a documented behaviour with a
        documented switch.
  (b) restore it in the interior via a hazard condition: retreat when the nearest fire/smoke is
      within K cells (or a risk score). The firefighters' _firefighter_cell_risk (agents.py:1052)
      is not reusable here - it is a per-cell score (1e6 on fire, +100 adjacent, +10 smoke) that
      the scorched round found is consulted only in a tier-4 fallback, and the searcher already
      has richer helpers (_min_strict_hazard_distance, buffer, lookahead). The honest form of (b)
      is a range trigger on _min_strict_hazard_distance. Its parameters are unmeasured, and 2.2
      predicts it will under-deliver: 61% of the accident's benefit-carrying firings were at 9+
      cells, and inside 2 cells the override barely changed the next cell. A K of 6 (buffer 2 +
      lookahead 3 + 1) keeps 36% of the accident's firings. It is more defensible as a rule - it
      says why it fires - but it is a different behaviour, and every prior round in this family
      that added a hazard-triggered constraint lost on fresh seeds.
  (c) two behaviours, different parameters: the e76c806 edge gate as it is (D <= 2, live
      scoring) plus an interior hazard rule. This is (b) with the edge gate kept; the edge gate
      is nearly redundant with _victim_edge_blocked_direction (within margin 3 only inward
      directions survive the filter, so the gate can only re-rank inward moves), and at
      D 3-5 the live retreat's inward bias is what the a-literal probe shows to be harmful when
      called often. (c) adds a second parameter set to measure for a component that does almost
      nothing. Folded into (b) as "the D <= 2 branch stays reachable when the range is finite".
  (d) something else: a front-aware term (the fire probability map, already penalised two cells
      ahead in _choose_best_direction at fire_prob >= 0.5) or a larger lookahead. Both change the
      searcher's target-seeking itself rather than restoring what was lost, are untuned, and
      belong to the follow-up the held round named (whether the searcher tuning still fits
      searchers that follow their own plans). Not this round.

4.2  RECOMMENDATION: (a-replica) as the shipped default, (b) at K = 6 as a measured secondary
     arm in the same wave, under ONE constant. Why (a-replica) and not (b):
       - it is the behaviour every outcome in the campaign's record was produced under, and the
         behaviour every prior searcher round tuned against; shipping it with the dimension fix
         changes nothing the maintainer has already accepted, while making it visible, named
         and switchable;
       - it has evidence: the test reproduces the baseline number exactly, and the full-run
         C7+C6 replay (4.7) shows its 23-seed outcomes and exposure before a line of source is
         written. (b) has a prediction and a precedent against it;
       - the brief's requirement is that the burning-cell exposure return to the 8520706 level
         on BOTH samples and the test pass 5/5. (a-replica) is the only candidate for which that
         is expected rather than hoped;
       - the objection that (a) is "the pattern this project spent 25 rounds removing" is
         answered by what those rounds removed: gates that could never fire and inputs that were
         never read. (a-replica) removes exactly such a gate (D <= 2 in C7) and such an input
         (the retreat's dead boundary terms) and replaces them with a stated rule and a switch.
     If the fresh seeds contradict (a-replica) and not (b), the round ships (b); the rule is in
     4.6. The constant makes both the same code path, so the choice is a default, not a rewrite.

4.3  THE SOURCE CHANGE (Part 2, not yet made)
     common_fixed_variables.py, next to the feature-2 constants (:142-143), with a comment in the

5. RISKS, AND THE THREE PRECEDENTS
----------------------------------
  - This is a movement constraint near fire, so it is in the #9-A1 / last_cell arm A /
    _reset_idle_retreat_state family, and the fresh seeds decide it, not the canonical 13. The
    difference from those three: they ADDED a constraint to a unit that did not have it and lost
    ground the unit was using well; this restores a constraint the searchers ran with for the
    whole record. The A/B is still required and is planned (4.6).
  - #9-A1 specifically: the scorched penalty evicted units from the safest ground in the model.
    The hazard-only retreat cannot do that to a searcher - its candidates are already filtered by
    the strict rules, and it scores by distance from fire, so it prefers burnt-out ground behind
    a front (far from any burning cell) over unburnt ground ahead of it. That is the right
    preference for a UAV.
  - Outcome re-roll: every outcome change in the held round was a dispatch-order consequence of
    searcher paths diverging at step 1-2. Restoring the C7 component moves the paths again;
    the +2 / -1 / 0 / +5 pattern will be re-rolled and must be reported per seed, as the brief
    asks (Part 3 item 4).
  - Coverage: the override is target-blind on the gated steps; cells covered were unaffected
    in 23 runs (2.4) but dispatch timing was. The mode-mix recording (4.7) will show how many
    steps the rule actually takes over on the live tree, where C5 no longer diverts steps into
    pathfinding.
  - The two trajectory tests (east_vs_west, north_vs_south) are order-dependent on both trees
    (held round 9) and are not attributable to any arm; they will be run in isolation as before.
  - E/707 (fresh): a searcher pinned on an edge in both worlds; nothing here touches it, and it
    should not be counted for or against the restoration.
