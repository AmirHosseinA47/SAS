0. CHANGE LOG (every change to this file after the first draft, and why)
  2026-09-26  D-10 CRN GATE: PER SEED -> PER SET. Changed on the maintainer's
              ruling, after I flagged the per-seed rule's false-fail rate. It was
              made BEFORE the first commit of this file and BEFORE any Part 3 run.
    - The first draft applied the maintainer's first answer literally: ANY CRN seed
      with fewer rescues fails. The draft itself flagged that a fix which merely
      perturbs runs would then fail about 40% per set and about 70% across three.
    - The maintainer's ruling, verbatim in substance: "I meant the CRN gate PER SET,
      not per seed. My stated reason - 'CRN has no re-roll, so a per-seed decrease
      is real' - was wrong: CRN pins the FIRE, not the firefighter's path, so a fix
      that changes movement still shifts victims between seeds without any real
      loss. Applied per seed the gate ... measures noise rather than harm."
    - REVISED CRN GATE: per-set totals, ZERO tolerance. A CRN set whose total
      rescued falls FAILS. Individual CRN seeds moving in both directions are
      expected and are not a failure on their own. The per-seed detail is
      reported beside the totals, so a one-sided pattern is visible if it exists.
    - STOCK is unchanged: the derived tolerance of 2 per set.
    - Sections 1 (D-10), 5 (G3), 6 (expectations) and 7 (stop rules) were edited
      to match. outputs/_cl_tooling_spec.txt B/H6 were edited the same way.

4. D-10: THE STOCK RESCUED TOLERANCE (outputs/_cl_tolerance.py -> _cl_tolerance.txt)
  THE NOISE. The stock gate's noise is the fire re-roll. Its recorded measure is
  the pairs whose decisions are identical and whose fire differs:
    uhD vs uhY  (U30, 360 steps)            1 victim flip in 30 tuples
    fmEFS vs fmDRY  (C13 + F10, 240 steps)  2 flips in 23 tuples
  So phi = 3 / 53 = 0.0566 flips per tuple: the accepted rate.
  THE SHAPE. The ungated round's: an accepted rate scaled to the sample size. For
  a set of n tuples, the null net loss is Poisson(phi n / 2) - Poisson(phi n / 2).
  T(n) is the smallest t with P(loss > t) <= 0.0345, which makes the three stock
  comparisons together falsely fail a null fix at most ~10%.
      C13  T = 2      U30  T = 2      N30  T = 2
  With these T the family-wise null false-fail is 0.059.
  RULE: a STOCK rescued loss on a set (clC rescued - arm rescued, at step 360)
  greater than 2 FAILS G3. A loss of 1 or 2 passes the tolerance and is listed
  victim by victim.

  G2 STALLS

       - M1c on N30 strictly lower than clC, with N30 stock + N30 CRN counted

     M1a, M1b and M1c are always reported side by side.
  G3 RESCUED at step 360, per set (C13, U30, N30), per instrument, arm vs clC.
       STOCK: loss > 2 -> FAIL. A loss of 1-2 is listed per victim.
       CRN:   the set TOTAL, zero tolerance: arm total rescued < clC total rescued

     whether the victim's own carry changed.

  G4 FF_DEATHS not higher than clC on any set or instrument, unless every extra

  G5 ROUTE_BLOCKED: _ffr_rbcompare.py --new clG<arm> --old ihrest.

  G7 THE FLIP GATE (carryleg_part1.txt section 7).
  ENGAGEMENT
     - An arm value-identical to clC everywhere is NOT ENGAGED.
     - SERVED not engaged -> "UNTRIGGERED IN PART 3".
     - Credit comes from outcomes only.
  SHIPPING
     - A fix ships iff:
         - it passes G1-G5 on its single arm;
         - it is ENGAGED, or, for SERVED, the D-9 probe shows it acting correctly;
         - the combination of passing fixes passes G1-G5;
         - the flipped tree passes G6 and G7.
     - If MODE 1 and MODE 2 both pass, MODE 2 ships.
     - A failing fix is reported, not bundled.
