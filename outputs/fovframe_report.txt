1. WHAT SHIPPED
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
All five decisions were implemented as confirmed. One is worth restating because
it is the opposite of the usual instinct: the firefighter got NO frame.

1.1  UAV OBSERVATION FRAME - the exact 17x17, on the dashboard only
     One snapped strokeRect per UAV around the TRUE Chebyshev block the UAV
     observes: cells [x-R, x+R] x [y-R, y+R], 289 cells at R = 8. That 289 is
     also N_OBSERVATIONS (common_fixed_variables.py:476-477) and the DQN's input
     width, so the square is the model's own canonical observation shape, not an
     approximation of a disc.
     Clamped to the non-torus grid. Clamping is load-bearing, not defensive: an
     edge-clipped block occurs on 31.7% (seed 101) and 39.6% (seed 202) of the
     240 steps in a real run.
     Inserted at serve_dashboard.py:704, after the depot overlay and before the
     trails loop - the only slot where the overlay is visible over terrain yet
     can occlude no unit marker and no assignment line.

1.2  VICTIM FLEE RADIUS - a manhattan diamond, per the maintainer's ruling
     VICTIM_FLEE_TRIGGER_DISTANCE = 3, drawn as the exact cell set |dx|+|dy| <= 3
     (25 cells, rows 1/3/5/7/5/3/1) emitted edge-by-edge as a staircase. Not a
     square - a square of the same half-width puts 24 of its 49 cells outside the
     real range with corners at manhattan 6, double the trigger. Not a smooth
     diamond either: that would slice cells and reintroduce a smaller version of
     the same error.
     Radius reads through the model's own accessor, so <= 0 kills the overlay
     with the feature. Per-victim `flee` mirrors Victim.advance()'s guards G3
     (terminal status) and G4 (firefighter custody), so a dead, rescued or
     in-custody victim draws nothing - the rule does not run for it. Verified
     live: at step 30 of seed 101, four of five victims draw a diamond and the
     fifth does not.

1.3  FIREFIGHTER - no frame, a column instead
     nearest_fire_dist, which the model already computes every step and discards
     into movement_reason.key_factors, is now a "fire d" column in the
     firefighter table. Manhattan cells to the nearest ACTIVELY BURNING cell,
     dash when off-grid or when nothing is burning. Coloured red at <= 1 and
     amber at <= 3, the two thresholds that actually gate the unit's retreat.
     No shape is drawn, because rescue is same-cell (radius 0), dispatch is
     unbounded, and the one real range is state-dependent (3 idle / 1 assigned)
     and manhattan. A frame would have asserted a sensing capability the model
     does not have.

1.4  COLOUR - two-tone, which keeps the exemplar's black and fixes it
     A 1px #FFFFFF core inside a #000000 casing, on the Math.round(k*cs)+0.5
     half-pixel convention the gridlines already use.

1.5  MESA - untouched, and the difference is stated not hidden
     Canvas_Grid_Visualization.py:111-127 keeps its 19x19 ring, its four open
     corners and its 0.5-cell overshoot. The two surfaces now differ by one ring
     BY DECISION. The mesa frame's real defect (the second `if` at :124 should be
     `elif`, which is why its corners render open) is left in place and recorded;
     the string h/w there is NOT a defect and was left alone on evidence.

3. THE POSITIVE CONTROL - the brief's instrument would have proved nothing
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is the most important result of the round, and it is a correction.

The brief required "the cell-colour digest (or its equivalent) MUST differ, with
exact accounting of which cells changed. Without it, 'identical simulation' could
mean the change was never exercised."

THE CELL-COLOUR DIGEST CANNOT DIFFER HERE. serve_dashboard.py:117-119 populates
`cells` inside a loop that skips every non-Fire agent, and the muting pass at
:148-159 iterates fire_cells only. No UAV, victim or firefighter can contribute a
byte. Measured on a real run: len(cells) == 2500 == the number of Fire agents
exactly, with UAVs on the grid. A dashboard FOV overlay is drawn in JS from the
uavs/victims payload, after the cells fill.

So the brief's control would have read IDENTICAL - which is precisely the "the
change was never exercised" signal it exists to prevent. Used as specified it
produces a false negative that looks like a failed positive control.

WHAT WAS DONE INSTEAD:

  cellcolor_sha256   DEMOTED to a NEGATIVE control, and it is IDENTICAL on all
                     three combos (34dc6786 / 34dc6786 / ee573808). This round
                     touches a renderer but no cell decider, so identical is the
                     correct result. The demotion has direct precedent, quoted
                     rather than invented: outputs/_rh_control.py:26-28 records
                     the mirror-image case.

  LAYER A, payload   fovpayload_sha256  36aca69d -> ccc56bd6, DIFFERS.
                     publishes_fov_radius  pre False -> post True
                     publishes_flee        pre False -> post True
                     publishes_ffdist      pre False -> post True
                     Absence pre-patch is what makes it a valid "before".

  LAYER C, pixels    The decisive one. The SHIPPED drawMap is sliced out of
                     serve_dashboard.py (not reimplemented), given the four
                     globals it reads, fed one captured frame, and rendered in
                     headless Chromium at 7f951cb and at HEAD.
                       digest  552b0029 -> d47d8f79
                       10,189 of 313,600 pixels changed (3.2490%)
                       gained #000000 x6536 (casing), #ffffff x3318 (core),
                       remainder antialias blends

EXACT ACCOUNTING, which is what the brief actually asked for. Rather than assert
"the digest moved", outputs/_fov_pixelaudit.py recomputes the intended geometry
independently in Python and checks TWO-WAY containment against the measured
pixel diff:

  A. every CHANGED pixel lies on a predicted stroke   -> no stray repaint
  B. every predicted stroke has changed pixels        -> nothing silently missing

  case            segments  changed  on-geometry  STRAY  SILENT   result
  nominal              124   10,189       10,189      0       0   PASS
  edge-clipped          58    5,266        5,266      0       0   PASS

Zero stray pixels means the delta is EXACTLY the overlay: nothing else on the
canvas moved. Zero silent segments means every stroke the patch intends actually
painted. A is the falsifiable half - one off-geometry pixel would mean the patch
moved something it should not have.

The edge-clipped case is a synthetic arm with UAVs parked at three corners and an
edge and victims against the boundary, because clipping fires on 31-40% of real
steps and the nominal frame did not exercise it.

4. FRAMES VISIBLE AND DISTINGUISHABLE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Black, the exemplar's colour, FAILS on this palette - two independent ways
     dE2000 = 0.0000, in every vision condition, against five distinct semantic
     marks at six code sites: dead victim (serve_dashboard.py:77), dead
     firefighter (:176), the UAV role-colour fallback (:72), the UAV marker's own
     stroke (:718), the gridline base (:682) and BLACK_AND_WHITE_COLORS[10].
     And 63.40% of the 23,572 frame cells a real run produces sit on ground where
     black gives under WCAG 3:1 - worst 1.48:1 on burnt #2b2b2b.

4.2  No flat colour fixes it
     An exhaustive sweep of 140,608 sRGB values, scored as the worst case over
     {normal, protanopia, deuteranopia, tritanopia, achromatic}, tops out at
     2.73 dE2000 against the 50 nominal co-occurring colours and 1.03 against the
     239 effective ones. Both at or below the JND. An independent second
     implementation over 14 named candidates agrees: best flat candidate 1.74.
     Cause, measured: this palette holds three complete luminance ramps
     (VEGETATION 12, FIRE 12, BLACK_AND_WHITE 11) and so occupies 50 distinct L*
     values across the full 0-100 range at a mean spacing of 2.04. There is no
     free luminance slot. A filled area can escape into chroma - that is how
     #895e00 won the scorched round at 27.34 - but a 2.24px line at ~8.8 cycles/deg
     is past useful chromatic sensitivity and is detected on luminance alone.

4.3  The two-tone, and why it is not just "better"
     worst-case vs the 50 nominal colours     38.37 dE2000   (14.0x the ceiling)
     worst-case vs the 239 effective colours  36.55 dE2000   (35.5x)
     worst-case in probability mode           40.32 dE2000
     internal contrast core vs casing        100.00 dE2000 / 21.00:1
     min best-tone WCAG over real frame cells  4.79:1
     frame cells below WCAG 3:1                0.00%   (black: 63.40%)
     The guarantee is palette-INDEPENDENT: for any background lightness in
     [0,100] the better tone is at least 50.0 L* away. It therefore survives the
     dead #2f4a1a mute being repaired, a new scenario palette, or another ground
     state being added - which no palette-derived single colour would.

4.4  Confirmed visually, not only numerically

                                    7f951cb and at HEAD

                                    a frame crosses the dark high-probability
                                    region the white core takes over. No probMode
                                    suppression is needed, as predicted.

     Fixed twice over. This round's artifacts are written under distinct tags

     previous round's, and _fov_compare.py now prints the age and provenance of

     committed artifacts were restored untouched after the run overwrote them.
     Recorded because the failure mode is the repo's own documented
     instrument-drift hazard, and this time it was caught by the result looking
     wrong rather than by checking the instrument first.

7.2  THE COMMITTED CONTROL ARTIFACTS DO NOT REPRODUCE IN THIS CONTAINER
     Running the SAME combo and seed as 3b1ffbf's recorded post arm gives a

     values (avg_norm_ig 0.7713 -> 0.7749, uncertain_cells 1539 -> 1480), and a

8. WHAT THE FRAME DOES AND DOES NOT CLAIM
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Carried forward from Part 1 because the brief's framing would have made this
report inaccurate, and because it is the sentence a reader of the dashboard needs.

    The square frame is the EXACT footprint of the UAV's cell/fire observation -
    Chebyshev radius 8, 289 cells, the same 289 that is the DQN's input width.
    It is ALSO the exact bounding box of a separate, narrower test: victim
    detection is Euclidean over 197 cells. For that one purpose the frame
    overstates by 92 cells, 31.83%, all of them in the corners (4 at ring 6, 28
    at ring 7, 60 at ring 8).

The brief's instruction to state "the frame shows the bounding box, not the true
footprint" is therefore only half right, and was not adopted as written: it is a
true footprint for the primary consumer and a bounding box for the secondary one.
Nor is the square a UI convention approximating a disc - README.md:139 says
"technically it is not a radius, since observed areas have square shapes".

Four of the brief's premises were corrected on evidence in Part 1 and all four
are carried here: the ring is not an off-by-one (1.5 / Part 1 section 1); the
square is exact for Chebyshev sensing (this section); firefighters and victims
needed different answers from the one requested (1.2, 1.3); and black fails on
this palette (4.1).
