CONCLUSION
  1. The executor permits assigning ONE victim to TWO firefighters. The assign
     guard only blocks giving a DIFFERENT victim to an already-assigned unit.
  2. Nothing unassigns the second unit when the first completes the rescue.
     _handle_rescue_incident unassigns on victim_dead but not on rescue_complete.
  3. The second unit therefore walks to a stale target_pos and starts exiting
     against a victim that is no longer on the grid.

  Guard 2 did not create this. It changed which unit survived long enough to
  reach the stale target. In the pre-guard arms ff_unit_0 died first, so only
  one claimant remained and the sample never exposed it.

  It does NOT inflate the rescue count - _finalize_rescued_victim returns early
  when the victim is already rescued (3 completions, 2 rescued in that seed).
  The cost is wasted capacity: ff_unit_1 spent steps 60-148 bound to an
  already-rescued victim, then took a rescue absence it had not earned.

  OUT OF SCOPE for feature 2 - this is rescue-assignment authority, not victim
  movement. Recommended for its own round.
