CRITICAL: 5 of these 10 misses occurred at all-UAV coverage >= 0.85,
the highest at 0.9288. A victim can go undetected while ~93% of the grid
has been observed at some point. Aggregate coverage fraction therefore
does NOT discriminate success from failure, in either metric.

WHICH METRIC TO MEASURE THE NEXT FIX ON: all-UAV union is the correct
BOUND (it is what wildfire_model._detect_victims_in_uav_radius actually
integrates over), so it should be reported. But neither aggregate
fraction should be used as the OPTIMISATION TARGET, because at 0.84 mean
all-UAV coverage the residual misses live in specific uncovered cells,
not in the size of the covered set. The discriminating quantity is
WHICH cells stay uncovered - i.e. whether the victim's own
neighbourhood is ever entered - not how many cells are covered.
Keep never_detected as the primary metric and report both coverage
numbers as diagnostics alongside it.
