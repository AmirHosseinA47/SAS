WHERE THE CLAIM IS UNDERSTATED — two corrections, both on the direction the claim already predicts, but far stronger than "if anything is suboptimal".

(1) NOT MERELY "NOT PREFERRED" — ACTIVELY DEPRIORITIZED. Burnt ground is not neutral in the scoring; it systematically ACCUMULATES the model's penalties, because a burnt cell by construction lies immediately behind the fire front. _firefighter_cell_risk (agents.py:665-673) charges +100 for _cell_adjacent_to_fire and +10 for _cell_has_active_smoke, and the freshly-burnt rim is typically both: 4-adjacent to the still-burning front, and carrying residual smoke for ~4 model steps (agents.py:91-95 ticks smoke_step(False) every step, above the FIRE_SPREAD_SPEED gate at agents.py:97, draining a dispelling_counter that stands at 3 when the cell turns burnt). So the scar rim scores risk=110. Unburnt fuel ahead of the front scores risk=0 AND has a larger `dist`, since _min_fire_distance (agents.py:719-725) measures Manhattan distance to CURRENTLY BURNING cells only. _pick_improving_retreat maximises (improvement, dist, -risk) at agents.py:946-955, so the ranking is doubly biased: it steers a retreating firefighter OUT of the permanently-safe black and INTO unburnt fuel the front will reach. The front advances up to 3 cells per FIRE_SPREAD_SPEED=3 tick (radius-3 spread) while a firefighter moves 1 cell/step — a matched race that ends at the grid boundary. This is the real-wildfire "escape into the black" rule inverted.

(2) NOT "NOT PREFERRED" — HARD REFUSED, with a lethal path. Two predicates do not merely score the scar rim down, they exclude it outright: _cell_meets_required_idle_safety (agents.py:727-736) returns False on fire-adjacency OR smoke, and _assigned_one_step_retreat (agents.py:1014-1020) `continue`s on all three. The lethal chain is concrete: once _idle_retreat_stalled latches, agents.py:789-792 makes _assigned_one_step_retreat the ONLY mover for an assigned firefighter on every subsequent step.
