1. WHAT WAS IMPLEMENTED
================================================================================
Exactly the design confirmed in outputs/idleretreat_part1.txt, applied by
outputs/_ir_patch.py (idempotent, CRLF-preserving, --check/--apply/--revert).

  4a  THE LATCH. agents.py:772-775, the unconditional early return, becomes:

        if bool(getattr(self, "_idle_retreat_stalled", False)):
            if self.target_pos:
                self._assigned_one_step_retreat(fire_cells)
                return
            self._revalidate_idle_retreat_stall(cell, origin, fire_cells)
            return

      Assigned units are behaviourally unchanged (the original
      `if target_pos and _assigned_one_step_retreat(): return` / `return`
      is the same statement, restructured only to give the idle arm a body).
      `_revalidate_idle_retreat_stall` re-runs the SAME filter chain and moves
      only on a genuine improvement, then clears the stall flag alone.

  4b  THE LEASH. agents.py:764-770 gains a second anchoring condition:

        if origin is None or (
            not self.target_pos
            and abs(cell[0]-origin[0]) + abs(cell[1]-origin[1])
            > IDLE_RETREAT_MAX_CELLS
        ):

      For an idle unit the leashed scan is the only mover inside
      `_survival_move`, so d > 6 proves an external mechanism moved it and the
      origin belongs to a finished manoeuvre. The `not self.target_pos` guard
      is load-bearing: `_assigned_one_step_retreat` moves assigned units with
      no leash test, so for them d > 6 proves nothing.

  THREE NEW METHODS, inserted before `_assigned_one_step_retreat`:
      _retreat_candidates          the filter chain, extracted verbatim and
                                   now shared by the normal scan and the
                                   revalidation pass
      _pick_improving_retreat      tiers 1-2 of the existing choice (ideal
                                   standoff, else strictly improving),
                                   extracted verbatim. Tier 3 - the
                                   take-the-least-bad-cell-anyway fallback -
                                   is deliberately NOT in the helper; the
                                   normal path keeps it, revalidation does not
      _revalidate_idle_retreat_stall   the recheck

  NOT TOUCHED, as scoped: `_move_toward`, route_blocked, rescue_planner.py,
  exit_target selection, `_recycle_firefighter_after_exit`, and every
  assigned-unit path.

  THE PATCH FAITHFULLY IMPLEMENTS WHAT PART 2 VALIDATED. The Part 2 what-if
  probe ran the same logic as a monkeypatch. Running the PATCHED SOURCE on the
  same two seeds reproduces those trajectories exactly:
      D/east  seed 101   480 trace rows compared, 0 differing
      D/south seed 101   480 trace rows compared, 0 differing
  So the Part 2 evidence transfers to the shipped code without reinterpretation.

  BUCKET D IS THE DEFECT, AND IT IS GONE: 9 -> 0. Nine of thirteen pre-fix

  THE RESIDUAL IS 5 DEATHS (C1+C2+C3+C4), and it must not be read as a single

4.1  C1 - THREE DEATHS, GENUINELY ENCLOSED. NOTHING COULD BE DONE.
       E/d 101 ff_unit_0  step  42  (41,41)   0 free neighbours
       E/h 404 ff_unit_0  step  33  (31,38)   0 free neighbours
       S/h 101 ff_unit_1  step 135  (1,0)     0 free neighbours
     Every in-bounds neighbour was burning at the lethal step. No candidate
     filter, no latch and no design choice is implicated. This is the
     irreducible floor the investigation's synthetic labs predicted.
     C1 rose from 1 to 3, which is not a regression: units that used to die
     frozen with an escape available now die after moving, in places where
     they really are surrounded. The composition of the residual shifted
     toward "nothing could be done", which is the intended direction.

4.2  C3 - ONE DEATH, KILLED BY THE LEASH AT ITS LEGITIMATE LIMIT.
     E/d seed 202 ff_unit_0, died step 225 at (34,49).
     This is EXACTLY the case predicted in the design as "accepted residual 2"
     and explicitly instrumented for rather than assumed away. The trace:

       step  pos       origin    d   free cand xlsh cur_d  moved
        210  (29,49)   (28,49)   1     2    1    0    1     yes
        216  (30,49)   (28,49)   2     2    2    0    0     yes   <- out of fire
        219  (32,49)   (28,49)   4     1    1    0    0     yes   <- out of fire
        222  (33,49)   (28,49)   5     2    2    0    0     yes   <- out of fire
        225  (34,49)   (28,49)   6     1    0    1    0     NO    <- burns

     The origin is NOT stale. The unit walked d = 1,2,3,4,5,6 by its own legal
     leashed retreat moves along the x=49 wall, escaping the fire three times.
     At step 225 it stands ON fire at exactly d = 6, its single free neighbour
     is at d = 7, and the leash refuses it. The 4b re-anchor correctly did not
     fire, because the origin was never invalid - the leash was working as
     designed, and it killed the unit.
     Note the fix still bought this unit 9 steps and three escapes: pre-fix it
     died at step 216 at (30,49).

4.3  C4 - ONE DEATH, KILLED BY THE ANTI-OSCILLATION GUARD.
     E/h seed 505 ff_unit_0, died step 63 at (41,22).
     Pre-fix this unit died at step 51 at (45,23). Post-fix the recheck walked
     it (45,23) -> (45,22) -> (44,22) -> (43,22) -> (42,22) -> (41,22), out of
     the fire twice on the way. At step 63 it stands ON fire with exactly one
     free neighbour - (42,22), the cell it came from at step 57, which is
     `_idle_retreat_last_cell`. The last_cell filter excludes it, the
     candidate set is empty, and the unit burns.
     The anti-oscillation guard that this round deliberately preserved is what
     closed the last door. The fix bought it 12 steps.

4.4  WHAT C3 AND C4 HAVE IN COMMON, AND WHAT I AM NOT DOING ABOUT IT
     Both are the same shape: a filter that exists for a NON-EMERGENCY purpose
     - bound the excursion, prevent ping-pong - refusing the last available
     step off a BURNING cell. In both cases cur_dist was 0.

     There is an obvious and principled follow-up: when the unit's own cell is
     on fire (cur_dist == 0), neither containment nor anti-oscillation is the
     operative concern, and both the leash and the last_cell exclusion could
     be suspended for that one step. On this sample that would address 2 of
     the 7 remaining deaths.

     I HAVE NOT IMPLEMENTED IT. It is a third change, outside the two pieces
     confirmed for this round, and implementing it now would be tuning the
     code to improve this round's own numbers after seeing them. It is
     recorded here as an evidenced proposal with two concrete cases attached,
     for a separate decision.

4.5  BUCKET A - TWO DEATHS OUTSIDE THIS FIX'S SCOPE, UNCHANGED
     E/h 101 ff_unit_0 step 30 (44,40) and S/h 404 ff_unit_1 step 57 (3,20)
     were not latched at death; the first dies while `moving_to_victim`, i.e.
     an assigned in-transit death on a path this round does not touch. Both
     are identical pre and post.

5. OSCILLATION
================================================================================
The gate requires no new oscillation, and the investigation warned that a
latch removal can look like a fix while the unit merely ping-pongs. Measured
three ways (outputs/_ir_osc.py):

                                                       pre-fix   post-fix
    survival-move moves (all units)                       285        372
    move-pair reversals, ANY step gap                      22         24
    TIGHT reversals (consecutive steps - real ping-pong)    3          5
    reversals where either move came from a LATCHED state   0          0
    DIRECT last_cell violations                            n/a         0

  THE DECISIVE ROW IS THE LAST ONE. The post-fix probe records
  `_idle_retreat_last_cell` before every call, so a violation is detected
  directly rather than inferred: a unit moving onto the cell the guard
  forbids. There are ZERO across all 13 runs. The anti-oscillation filter is
  intact.

  THE SECOND-DECISIVE ROW IS THE ONE ABOVE IT. Zero reversals originate from
  the path this round adds. All five tight reversals have
  `latched_pre = (False, False)` - both moves came from the existing unlatched
  normal path.

  ON THE +2. Tight reversals went 3 -> 5. Both new ones are on the untouched
  path, and their mechanism is the PRE-EXISTING hole documented in the design
  (agents.py:883-886: a tier-3 move landing on a required-safe cell calls
  `_reset_idle_retreat_state()`, which clears `last_cell` and permits a
  reversal on the next step). The fix produces 87 more moves overall, which
  gives that pre-existing hole more opportunities to fire. Reported as a real
  +2, attributed to existing code rather than waved away, and not fixed here
  because it is outside the agreed scope.

  Also unchanged and worth restating: `_assigned_one_step_retreat` has no
  anti-oscillation term at all and is untouched this round.

9. HOW MUCH OF THIS IS THE FIX, AND HOW MUCH IS LUCK
================================================================================
Stated plainly, because a -6 on 13 runs is a small sample.

  * The MECHANISM is certain, not statistical. Bucket D going 9 -> 0 is not a
    sampling result: it is the direct measurement that no latched unit with an
    improving cell now fails to take it. The pre-fix control shows the same
    classifier finding 9 such deaths.
  * The MAGNITUDE is a 13-run estimate and should not be quoted precisely.
    -6 deaths on 13 runs, +1 rescued on 13 runs.
  * PART 1 ALREADY PREDICTED THE SPLIT that this round confirms. Ten fresh
    seeds showed the latch finding replicating (78% vs 77%) while the
    leash-staleness finding did not appear at all. In this post-fix sample,
    4b's re-anchor is again a minor contributor: only one of the 25 escapes
    (D/south 101 step 103) depended on it, and one death (4.2) shows the leash
    binding legitimately. 4a is doing most of the work, exactly as Part 1 said
    it would.
  * WHAT THE FIX CANNOT DO remains what the investigation said it could not:
    three of the seven remaining deaths are units with every neighbour
    burning. Against an advancing front, a reactive greedy retreat gets
    outrun. Option (d) - not stranding units next to fire in the first place -
    is still the larger lever, and this round's numbers do not change that.
