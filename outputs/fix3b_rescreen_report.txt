9. WHAT EACH FINDING MEANS FOR THE PAPER
================================================================================
  - Gates pass with target zero: the safety argument of the paper (no new livelock, no new unfinished
    run, no route_blocked loss, identity at default) is now complete for the measurement round.
  - The hand-back finding becomes a design lesson: a planner that owns a subsystem only while it has
    something to say hands control to a chain that was never tuned for that state. "One owner to the
    end" is a general MAPE-K design rule worth one paragraph.
  - Owned-step share (85-95%): the comparison is strategy + residual current chain, not strategy alone;
    the paper states it and reports the share per arm.
  - Information map: the baseline the field would compare against (the current searcher) reads a
    privileged count. Any claim of "beats the current searcher" must say the current searcher is
    advantaged by P and by ring tuning; the fair comparison is uniform spawn, and a P-free variant of
    the current searcher (N - rescued - observed deaths) is a natural extra baseline (not built).
  - Exposure: the BFS keeps strategy-steered searchers out of fire and smoke entirely - a safety
    property that can be stated and measured, distinct from the detection endpoints.
  - Outcomes: nothing claimable at n = 16; the rescue channel is dominated by downstream dispatch and fire
    divergence, which the CRN design of the main round is built to remove.
  - R-5 flag: a threats-to-validity item (search order couples into a firefighter stall), not a claim.
  - Burn-over 0.1 was set after the screen: disclosed, with 0.5 in the sensitivity analysis.

  motion model put them elsewhere. Any p_bo > 0 can cross 0.5 this way; the value alone does not fix it.
