9. WHAT EACH FINDING MEANS FOR THE PAPER
================================================================================
  - Gates pass with target zero: the safety argument of the paper (no new livelock, no new unfinished
    run, no route_blocked loss, identity at default) is now complete for the measurement round.
  - The hand-back finding becomes a design lesson: a planner that owns a subsystem only while it has
    something to say hands control to a chain that was never tuned for that state. "One owner to the
    end" is a general MAPE-K design rule worth one paragraph.
  - Owned-step share (85-95%): the comparison is strategy + residual current chain, not strategy alone;
    the paper states it and reports the share per arm.
  - Information map: the baseline the field would compare against (the current searcher) reads a
    privileged count. Any claim of "beats the current searcher" must say the current searcher is
    advantaged by P and by ring tuning; the fair comparison is uniform spawn, and a P-free variant of
    the current searcher (N - rescued - observed deaths) is a natural extra baseline (not built).
  - Exposure: the BFS keeps strategy-steered searchers out of fire and smoke entirely - a safety
    property that can be stated and measured, distinct from the detection endpoints.
  - Outcomes: nothing claimable at n = 16; the rescue channel is dominated by downstream dispatch and fire
    divergence, which the CRN design of the main round is built to remove.
  - R-5 flag: a threats-to-validity item (search order couples into a firefighter stall), not a claim.
  - Burn-over 0.1 was set after the screen: disclosed, with 0.5 in the sensitivity analysis.

15.3 WHY NO BURN-OVER VALUE > 0 CAN MEET THE CRITERION (verified algebra + recorded data)
  - Order per step, post-move (wildfire_model.py:2736-2773): PREDICT (modes 2/3) -> MEASURE -> BURN-OVER,
    all on the TRUE burning set (A3).
    - predict conserves mass;
    - burn_over moves p_bo x (alive mass on burning cells) to DEAD, without renormalising;
    - measure zeroes the covered cells, then divides p AND dead by the same z = 1 - (covered alive mass).
  - So dead_mass = d_raw / (d_raw + a_raw), where d_raw is the raw mass burn-over has ever moved (never
    shrinks) and a_raw the raw alive mass (never grows). THE DEAD SHARE IS A RATCHET: 0 decreases in
    5 600 recorded sample pairs, across all 10 Bayes arms.
  - Negative information alone raises the share by 1/(1 - r) each step without adding DEAD mass.
    Coverage reaches 0.94-0.98 by step 200, so a_raw is driven towards 0 and ANY d_raw > 0 eventually
    takes the share past 0.5.
  - Because p is shared, the per-victim check is a RUN-LEVEL CLOCK: every alive victim still undetected
    when the run's share passes 0.5 fails, whatever the belief says about that victim's location.
  - CALIBRATION (post hoc, descriptive; correlated victim-steps, 10-15 distinct victims per high bin). A
    calibrated DEAD share FAILS a 0.5 threshold too: at D = 0.6 a calibrated belief has 40% of those
    victims alive.
    - BF-R arms: 15 alive victim-steps above 0.5 vs 38.7 expected under calibration (sum of 1 - D).
      Brier 0.075 vs climatology 0.212. UNDER-confident in every bin: truth is deader than D says.
    - BD-R arms: 78 vs 31.6 expected, Brier 0.097 vs 0.206. OVER-confident.
    - The 15 and the 78 equal the check's FAIL-sample counts.
    - Late in runs most undetected victims really are dead: at step 200, pooled BF-R arms have 11 dead vs
      2 alive undetected.
  - SIMULATOR TRUTH (verified): a victim still on a burning cell after its flee step dies with certainty
    (wildfire_model.py:5609-5641; the flee runs in advance(), agents.py:2385-2393). The belief applies
    burn-over AFTER its own flee step, on the same true burning set.
    - Measured against the simulator's rule, the matching value is 1, and 0 is the MOST misspecified
      about deaths.
    - The 16.2 reasoning ("burn-over mostly judges the belief's imprecise placement of the front") is
      only partly right. With the true burning set (A3) the front is exact. The imprecision is in the
      belief's MOTION model: p_go 0.8, the softmax, and the BD arms' fire-agnostic diffusion.

15.4 HOW THE DEAD MASS AFFECTS TARGETING (verified in code)
  - NO DECISION READS THE DEAD MASS. belief.dead is read only in measure's normalisation, burn_over and
    summary() (victim_search_belief.py:208-211, 226, 245). Of the derived quantities, only n_unfound
    drives a decision: the R-1 switch, searcher_targeting.py:185.
  - THE BAYES CHOICE IS SCALE-INVARIANT. Multiplying p (or lambda = n_unfound x p) by any positive
    constant leaves every target unchanged: the s > 0 filter (:293-295), the pre-rank s/max(L, L0)
    (:299), top-M, the exact gain and score g/max(L, L0) with its tie-break key (:310-313), the order of
    free searchers and the coordination conditioning. Proof by linearity, plus 60 synthetic planner calls
    with 0 mismatches. So the DEAD SHARE BY ITSELF CHANGES NO TARGET.
  - Burn-over reaches targeting only through:
    (a) THE SHAPE OF p NEAR THE FIRE. It removes alive mass on burning cells only. Under diffusion
        (mode 2), mass that stays next to the front is eaten. Under flee (mode 3), front cells drain
        about 63% per step by flight, so less is eaten.
    (b) THE SWEPT RULE, LOCALLY ONLY. A held target is dropped when Sp, its disc mass of p, falls below
        0.25 x its value at issue (:253). Burn-over lowers Sp only when the target's disc overlaps
        burning cells (worst case: 14 steps at 0.1, 3 steps at 0.5).
  - CORRECTION OF THIS REPORT'S EARLIER TEXT. The earlier section 15 said "a global loss of alive mass to
    DEAD looks like a sweep". That is WRONG.
    - measure divides an uncovered held target's mass by z <= 1, so Sp RISES under negative information.
    - Global DEAD growth only slows that rise, a second-order effect: on a synthetic never-covered
      target, the Sp ratio after 100 steps is 1.137 at p_bo 0.1 against 1.145 at 0.
    - It can never cause drop_swept.
  - What the collapse of scale does show (recorded):
    - G at issue tracks n_unfound x alive mass (Spearman 0.85 BD / 0.77 BF). Its median falls from
      0.155 / 0.151 (DEAD <= 0.5) to 0.0012 / ~0 (DEAD > 0.5).
    - In the BF arms, 171 of the 348 targets issued at DEAD > 0.5 had G < 5e-6. The planner keeps
      choosing targets on relative mass alone, as scale invariance implies.
    - The belief's expected number of unfound alive victims at those issues: median 0.035-0.045.
  - n_unfound counts victims that died unseen. In the 18 re-screen runs with an unseen death, R-1's
    continuation never triggered (lo_continuation_steps 0), so the Bayes rule plans to the end on that
    residual mass. Harmless for choice (scale invariance); relevant to the hand-back (section 2).

15.7 RECOMMENDATION (this REVISES the "(A)" this section recommended earlier - said plainly)
  - The R-3 quantity is a shared, monotone clock that a calibrated belief also fails, and targeting does
    not read it at all.
  - So every belief change that "passes" it does so either by construction (A, E, I), and the check then
    detects nothing, or by a value that may pass on these seeds (K, C, D, F), and the check then fits a
    parameter to itself.
  - Neither repairs what matters for search: where the belief puts alive mass relative to where alive
    victims are.
  RECOMMENDED SEQUENCE (the first step is needed under A and H alike, because A makes the old check
  vacuous):
    1. Implement the record-only instrument of H in outputs/_fb3_probe.py (truth stays out of the
       managing system). Re-run the 80 Bayes runs with it and require value identity with the recorded
       re-screen.
    2. From those runs, compare p_bo 0 / 0.1 / 0.5 on IDENTICAL observation streams (shadow beliefs or
       exact offline replay): belief-at-truth for alive undetected victims, phantom mass at 0, and
       calibration.
    3. Rule A or H on that evidence.
       - A wins if 0.1 measurably lowers belief-at-truth near the fire while the phantom mass at 0 stays
         small in burnt ground.
       - H wins otherwise.
       - If A: RE-SCREEN (the closed-loop gates must see the fire-edge risk). Then the SUITE once, then
         the single merge.
  IF YOU RULE NOW without step 2:
  - H if R-3 is a VALIDITY TEST of the belief: the criterion is the defect.
  - A if R-3 is a MODELLING PRINCIPLE ("the belief must not declare live victims dead"), with the
    instrument mandatory and p_bo 0.1 / 0.5 as the sensitivity.
  WHAT WOULD CHANGE THIS:
  - BF's calibration turning over-confident on a second seed set (n = 16 is a screen, not evidence).
  - The value-identity re-run failing (then the probe is not record-only and must be redone first).
  - A literature check settling how SAR practice treats survival.
