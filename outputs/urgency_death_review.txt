1.2 THE REPLAYS. outputs/_ud_replay.py re-runs a committed run line in-process through the unchanged probe, and
    adds two things: the fire board (burning and active-smoke cells, by agents.fire_board_sets) at every
    firefighter decision, and optional KNOCKOUTS - at a chosen (unit, step range) the fix's pure choice
    (_approach_path_choice for (a), _retreat_on_route_choice for (b)) returns None, so the model runs today's mover
    for that decision, the exact code path of a switch-0 arm. Nothing else changes.
    - Reproduction: a replay with no knockout (R0) must equal the committed record on every per-step row, the
      commands, the movement rows and events, eval and stdout. Checked first on set4/uniform/B_N arm M: exact.
      Every R0 is checked again in section 2.
    - Per firefighter death of arm M / MU that is not present in arm 0 (9 deaths in 8 runs): R0, KO-OWN, KO-LAST
      and KO-OTHERS (1.3); 31 replays, queue outputs/_ud_replay/_ud_rp_queue.jsonl.
    - Footprint replays (boards only) of every other arm-M cell where a fix acted (36 runs), used only by the
      follow-up design's guard diagnostic (urgency_part1d.txt 1d.2.6).

1.3 THE CAUSATION RULE (written 2026-10-07 07:55, before any knockout replay finished or was read; verbatim)
    QUESTION (maintainer): was each firefighter death in arm M CAUSED by a fix step?
    EVIDENCE PER DEATH (unit U dies at step t in arm X in {M, MU}, cell c):
      E0  arm 0 of cell c (the committed record): does U die at t, at another step, or survive?
      E1  the records of X: U's own LIVE fix events (mv_events, kind a / b), the leg it died on, route_blocked
          raises, unassigns, its last cells; the dying leg = from U's last bind up to its death, or, for an unbound
          unit, the leg that ended in the unassign that left it unbound.
      R0  replay of X's run line with boards (must reproduce the record exactly; else the death is UNRESOLVED).
      KO-OWN     replay with U's own (a)/(b) decisions knocked out at every step <= t (today's step instead).
      KO-LAST    replay with only U's LAST own live fix decision before t knocked out.
      KO-OTHERS  replay with every OTHER unit's (a)/(b) decisions knocked out at every step <= t (U's own live).
      "Survives" in a replay = U is alive at step t (its later fate is reported, not used).
    CLASSES (first that applies):
      C-NONE   U dies at the same step t in arm 0 and U's rows are identical between arm 0 and X up to t (the
               fixes did not touch this death). Also: U's death precedes the cell's first divergence.
      C-PROX   CAUSED BY ITS OWN FIX STEP (proximate): (i) U took >= 1 live fix step on its dying leg; AND (ii) U
               survives in KO-OWN; AND (iii) the route-closure chain holds: after U's last own fix step on the
               leg, the fire closed U's way (a route_blocked raise for U, or U's clean distance to its target became
               infinite) before U reached its target, and U died without being re-bound to a route it could pass.
               Sub-label "single step" when U also survives in KO-LAST.
      C-BUTFOR FIX-DEPENDENT, NOT PROXIMATE BY MECHANISM: (i) and (ii) hold but (iii) does not.
      C-DOWN   DOWNSTREAM: not the above, and U survives at t in arm 0 (or dies at another step): the death follows
               from the fixes' change of the run (other units' fix steps, the changed fire, binds or timing), not
               from U's own fix step. Sub-label "via others' fix steps" when U survives in KO-OTHERS.
      UNRESOLVED R0 does not reproduce, or the evidence conflicts; stated with the conflict.
    A death of the same unit at the same step in X and in arm 0 with differing rows before t is reported as C-NONE
    only if U's own rows are identical up to t; otherwise it is classed by the rules above.
    LIMIT, stated: a knockout changes the trajectory after the knocked-out decision; survival in a knockout is a
    but-for statement about that decision under this run's CRN fire, not a claim that the step was unsafe. (iii) is
    what makes a but-for result a STRANDING caused by the step.

1.4 THE REVIEWERS. One read-only workflow: 7 reviewers over disjoint groups of cells (every death of every arm in

    completeness and consistency of all 132 entries). The orchestrator wrote the tools, ran the replays, and
