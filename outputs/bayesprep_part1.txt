2. ITEM 2 - FRONT-PRIORITY (URGENCY-WEIGHTED) BAYESIAN SEARCH: SEARCHER_TARGETING = 5 (default 0)
================================================================================
2.1 WHAT IT IS
  - FP is the flee-aware arm (mode 3, with item 1's fixes) plus ONE change: in target preference only,
    each cell's expected unfound-alive mass is weighted by how soon the fire is expected to reach it:
        lambda_w(c) = lambda(c) * w(c),    w(c) = 1 + kappa * exp(-T(c) / tau).
  - lambda_w replaces lambda in exactly three places: the pre-rank S, the exact gain G, and the
    coordination's fictitious non-detection (claims zero lambda_w consistently).
  UNCHANGED, BY CONSTRUCTION:
    - the belief (prior, predict = the flee model, measure, burn-over 0.1);
    - reachability (the admissible BFS, clearance 6) and the battery filter;
    - coordination's procedure;
    - THE ONE-OWNER RULE: held targets are dropped only by reached / unreachable / battery / swept. The
      swept rule keeps reading the UNWEIGHTED p, so a moving front can never drop a held target through the
      weight;
    - MIN_DIST, the fallback, R-1 and the recall.
2.2 THE ESTIMATE OF T(c), THE FIRE ARRIVAL TIME (operator-side; NOT the simulator's rule)
    T(c) = min over believed-burning cells b of ( |v| - e * (v . u) ) / ( R_H * (1 - e) ),   v = c - b.
  - This is |v| / R(theta) with R(theta) = R_H (1 - e) / (1 - e cos theta): the elliptical fire with the
    ignition at the rear focus.
  - The minimum over the front is the Huygens envelope of elliptical wavelets for homogeneous conditions.
  - u = the downwind unit vector of the known constant wind (A4; the wind the managing system already
    reads through EnvironmentBridge / UAVExecutor._get_wind_direction; 'west' = fire travels to -x,
    cfv:35, :47-58).
  - The burning set = the planner's pre-move fire snapshot (_fix3b_true_fire, A3: what the belief already
    sees).
  - w = 1 on burning cells (their mass is ~0). No fire: w = 1, and FP equals BF exactly.
  - Cost: O(cells x burning cells) per step, about the flee model's distance pass (2-5 ms).
  WHY THIS IS NOT THE SIMULATOR'S RULE (agents.py:53-125, 239-266). The simulator:
    - is stochastic, per cell, over 28 Euclidean-3 offsets with weight d^-2;
    - boosts only the 3 sources DIRECTLY upwind on the same row or column (a line, not an ellipse) and cuts
      every other source to 10%;
    - ticks every 3 steps, capped at 0.75 per tick and at most 1 cell per step;
    - does not latch a burn (a scorched cell can re-ignite).
  The estimate is deterministic and elliptical, with a literature rate. It reads no simulator constant
  (FIRE_SPREAD_*, MU, fuel are never read; MU is not reachable from src_extension).
2.3 PARAMETERS. Each is cited (FORM from the literature; VALUE a pre-registered operator assumption where no
    value maps to cells / steps - the 15.3 rule).
  - U10 = 20 km/h: the assumed 10-m open wind. The simulator has no wind speed (MU is dimensionless, cfv:67).
    20 km/h is the lower bound of a "moderate breeze", Beaufort 4 (WMO). Sensitivity {10, 40}.
  - R_H = 0.1 x U10 = 2.0 km/h = 0.556 m/s, the head rate. "10% wind speed rule of thumb", Cruz & Alexander
    2019, Annals of Forest Science 76:44 (forests and shrublands, dry fuels).
  - Cells per step: 1 cell / step is the victims' movement quantum (A6). The fire is expressed relative to
    the person who flees, at a free walking speed of 1.34 m/s (Weidmann 1993, Transporttechnik der
    Fussgaenger, ETH Zurich IVT 90).
      -> R_H = 0.556 / 1.34 = 0.415 cells / step.
  - Shape: length-to-width z = 1 + 0.25 U_mf, with U_mf the effective midflame wind in mi/h, capped at 8
    (Andrews 2018, The Rothermel surface fire spread model..., USDA FS RMRS-GTR-371, following Anderson
    1983, INT-305). Eccentricity e = sqrt(z^2 - 1) / z.
      - U_mf = WAF x U_20ft, with WAF = 0.4 for unsheltered surface fuels (Albini & Baughman 1979, INT-221;
        Andrews 2012, RMRS-GTR-266).
      - U_20ft = U10 / 1.15 (Turner & Lawson 1978, BC-X-177).
      -> U_mf = 4.32 mi/h, z = 2.08, e = 0.877. Head : back = 15.3. Flank (theta = 90 deg) = 0.051
         cells / step; back = 0.027.
  - The polar ellipse from the rear focus: Van Wagner 1969 (Forestry Chronicle 45:103-104); Catchpole, de
    Mestre & Gill 1982 (Australian Forest Research 12:47-54). The Huygens envelope: Anderson, Catchpole,
    de Mestre & Parkes 1982 (J. Austral. Math. Soc. B 23:451-466); Richards 1990.
  - Using fire travel time to prioritise space: the evacuation TRIGGER BUFFER (Cova, Dennison, Kim & Moritz
    2005, Transactions in GIS 9(4):603-617; Dennison, Cova & Moritz 2007, Natural Hazards 41:181-199).
  - tau = 16 steps = 2 r / v_UAV: the time a searcher needs to cross one detection footprint (sensor radius
    8, UAV 1 cell / step; K5 and the simulator's quantum). No new free value.
    - For the head direction only R_H x tau matters, so the U10 sensitivity also covers tau.
  - kappa = 1: a person the fire is about to reach counts up to twice a person not under threat. This is a
    VALUE JUDGEMENT of the operator, with no literature value. Stated as such. Sensitivity kappa = 3.
  WHAT THE CITED SHAPE IMPLIES (stated plainly, not tuned away). At U10 20:
    - the head weights a cell 4 / 8 / 16 cells ahead at w = 1.55 / 1.30 / 1.09;
    - the flanks spread at about 0.05 cells / step at every U10 in {10, 20, 40}, so only cells 1-2 cells off
      a flank gain (w 1.29 / 1.09).
    FP therefore prioritises mainly the area AHEAD OF THE HEAD, downwind - where the flee belief also
    carries mass (and where the D_W victim fled). The flanks count more only close in.

    SEARCHER_TARGETING value 5; SEARCHER_FP_U10_KMH 20; SEARCHER_FP_ROS_WIND_FRACTION 0.1;

2.5 KNOWN RISK - EVERY OSCILLATION IN THIS PROJECT HAPPENED NEAR FIRE.
  What keeps FP from steering into fire:
    - The weight only re-orders candidates that already pass the admissible BFS, which stays 6 cells from
      burning cells and is re-checked every step.
    - No score-based switching.
    - The swept rule ignores the weight.
    - w is smooth in T (no buffer edge to chatter on).
  The screen measures it (section 7): FP vs BF on searcher steps in fire, in smoke, and within 3 / 6 cells
  of burning; FIRE-class oscillation episodes; give-ups by reason. G-O is a gate.
  PAPER:
    - FP is a one-term extension, an urgency weight in the plan only, with a cited estimate. The estimate
      is deliberately not the simulator's rule (an operator model; inverse crime avoided).
    - The instrument records T at each victim's true cell, so the paper can report the estimate's error
      against the actual arrival.

================================================================================
5.1 WHAT THE RULE ACTUALLY IS (correction).
  - UAV.not_UAV_adjacent(pos) tests only whether the DESTINATION CELL holds a UAV (agents.py:402-408). It is
    an OCCUPANCY test, not an adjacency test.
  - The untune records' wording "a cell adjacent to another UAV" (untune_rescreen_report.txt:108, :113,
    :135; untune_merge_notes.txt:83-84) is wrong. This round records the correction and does not edit the
    merged records.
  - Recorded, untuned + recall: 167 / 309 / 115 / 124 refused tracker moves aimed INTO a docked UAV's cell
    (ring 1, ring 2, uniform 1, uniform 2), against 2 / 3 / 2 / 12 as shipped before recall.
  - Tracker direction choosers have no UAV block (uav_executor.py:4252+, :3559+), so they retry the same
    move and >= 2 refusals in 5 steps is DRIFT_TOO_HIGH.
5.2 THE CHANGE - switch UAV_DOCKED_NOT_OBSTACLE = 1 (ships ON; off only on an exact 0; agents accessor via
    _fix2_switch). "Docked" = rtb_docked, the same predicate as session 2's collision alarm
    (wildfire_model.py:1866-1869).
  (a) MOVE RULE: in UAV.move (agents.py:1131), a docked UAV does not block the destination cell for a mover
      that is NOT on a return leg. Return legs keep today's rule, so the docking paths are unchanged by
      construction.
      - not_UAV_adjacent itself, _rtb_cell_free and the test helpers are unchanged.
      - Planners' UAV-free BFS sets (A1-R, the targeting BFS, the pocket escape) are unchanged; this item is
        the move rule.
  Making (a) safe needs three guards, all under the same switch. Without them the change would create the
  states below.
  (b) NO TWO UAVs DOCKED ON ONE CELL. Free-cell docking docks on ANY footprint cell (agents.py:993), with no
      occupancy test, because "the move rule never puts two UAVs on one cell" - which (a) makes false.
      - A UAV standing over a docked UAV that starts its return there would dock on top of it.
      - Guard: dock only where no other DOCKED UAV stands. _nearest_free_dock_cell then no longer keeps its
        own cell as free when a docked UAV is on it.
      - The berth path (FREE_CELL_DOCKING 0) gets the same guard.
  (c) NO TWO AIRBORNE UAVs ON ONE CELL. A docked UAV is not released while another UAV stands on its cell. It
      relaunches the step after that UAV leaves. Without this, a release whose first move is refused would
      leave two airborne UAVs on one cell.
  (d) THE GLOBAL COLLISION CHECK COUNTS ONLY AIRBORNE UAVs, like session 2's local collision alarm.
      - global_analyzer.py:946-970 pairs every UAV, docked included, at distance < 1, as a CRITICAL
        COLLISION_RISK leading to the fail-safe.
      - Without (d), (a) would trade DRIFT alarms for a worse alarm that has never fired in any recorded run.
      - Docked status comes from the UAV's own monitor record (task_context "docked",
        local_uav_monitor.py:339-341).
  Unchanged:
    - firefighters (not UAVs; never blocked);
    - MR2 (counts UAV pairs under SECURITY_DISTANCE, docked included; reported);
    - the dispatcher's yield rule (already airborne-only).
